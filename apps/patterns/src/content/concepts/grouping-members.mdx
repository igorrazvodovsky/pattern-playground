---
title: Grouping members
added: 2026-11-15
purpose: Let access be given to a named group of members at once, so that joining or leaving the group changes what each of them can reach.
principle: >-
  If a group is given a role in a space, every member of the group can act
  there at that role; adding someone to the group gives them that access, and
  removing them takes it away, without anyone changing the space itself.
state:
  - "member: Group -> set User"
  - "role: GroupMembership -> one Role"
actions:
  addToGroup: Whoever manages the group adds a member, who gains every access the group holds.
  removeFromGroup: Whoever manages the group removes a member, who loses the access they held through it.
  grantToGroup: Whoever manages a space gives a group a role there.
misfits:
  - Once access comes through several groups and direct grants, nobody can tell what a member can do or which grant gives it.
  - Removing a member from a space leaves the access they still hold through a group.
  - A group made for another purpose, such as a mailing list or a team for mentions, is reused to give access, and everyone who joins it for that purpose gains the access too.
aliases: [groups, teams, user groups]
depends_on: [joining, assigning-roles]
appears_in:
  - Google Workspace groups in Drive sharing
  - GitHub teams
  - Microsoft Entra groups
  - Notion groups
---
