---
title: Joining
added: 2026-10-18
purpose: Make a person a member of a space, with a role and a level of access.
principle: >-
  If a person joins a workspace, they have a profile in it and can be found by
  the other members; if a member joins a project, they can see it and act in it
  at the access level they were given.
state:
  - "role: User -> one Role"
  - "accessLevel: User -> one AccessLevel"
  - "member: Project -> set User"
actions: [joinWorkspace, joinProject]
misfits:
  - Leaving has no action, so a member who has gone stays a member.
  - Access level is held on the member, not on the membership, so one person cannot have different access on different projects.
aliases: [membership, onboarding, access]
appears_in:
  - Slack workspaces and channels
  - GitHub organisations and repositories
  - Notion workspaces
---

Joining is applied at two scales in the fixture world: the workspace, where a
person arrives with a profile, and a project, where a member is added with an
access level. The second misfit is a merge the world took: access rides on the
member rather than on the pair of member and project, which is simpler and
wrong as soon as one person needs different access in two places.
