---
title: Bookmarking
added: 2026-11-14
purpose: Make it easy for a member to return to an item later.
principle: >-
  If a member bookmarks an item, it appears in their own list of bookmarks;
  whenever they want it again they open the list and go straight to it, however
  they reached it the first time.
state:
  - "bookmarked: User -> set Item"
actions:
  bookmark: A member adds an item to their own list of bookmarks.
  removeBookmark: A member takes an item off their list; the item itself is untouched.
misfits:
  - The list grows without upkeep, and entries go stale when the item moves or is deleted.
  - A save that others can see serves two purposes, keeping and approving, and members stop saving things they would not want to be seen endorsing.
  - Several near-identical keeping concepts in one system (bookmarks, favourites, a reading list and frequently visited sites in the same browser) leave the member unsure where they kept something.
  - A bookmark points at an address, so an item that is renamed or moved without a stable address breaks it.
aliases: [save for later, favourite, star, saved items, reading list]
appears_in:
  - Safari and Chrome
  - Slack saved items
  - YouTube Watch later
  - Pocket
---
