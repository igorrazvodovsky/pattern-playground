---
title: Authorising
added: 2026-11-15
purpose: Let another party act for a member on things the member controls, within limits the member sets.
principle: >-
  If a member gives a party a grant that names what it may reach and what it
  may do there, the party can act within the grant as the member could, and
  anything outside it is refused; when the member revokes the grant, or it
  lapses, the party can no longer act.
state:
  - "grants: User -> set Grant"
  - "holder: Grant -> one Party"
  - "allows: Grant -> set Capability"
  - "limit: Grant -> opt Limit"
  - "lapsesOn: Grant -> opt Time"
actions:
  grant: A member allows a party to reach and act on something they control, and may set a limit on how much and a time at which the grant lapses.
  actUnderGrant: The holder does something the grant allows, and the system lets it; an act outside the grant is refused.
  changeGrant: A member widens or narrows what a grant allows or its limit.
  revoke: A member withdraws a grant, and the holder can no longer act under it.
  lapse: The system ends a grant because its time has come or its limit is used up.
misfits:
  - A grant is asked for before the work has shown what it needs, so the member gives it wide to be safe or refuses it without knowing the cost.
  - What can be granted comes in the units the system offers (the whole mailbox, every repository), which are seldom the units of the task.
  - Nothing ties a grant to the work it was given for, so it stays in force after the work has ended.
  - The grant says what the holder may do and not what it did, so the member cannot judge from the grant whether to widen or narrow it.
  - A refusal reaches the holder in the middle of its work, often when the member is not there to be asked.
  - A holder that asks each time it meets a limit turns granting into a habit, and the member stops reading what they allow.
aliases: [permission, grant, consent, connected app, app access, delegated access, allowance]
appears_in:
  - OAuth consent screens ("Sign in with Google", GitHub app installation)
  - iOS and Android app permissions
  - Slack app installation
  - Claude Code permission modes and allow lists
  - API keys with scopes and spending limits
---
