---
title: Quoting
added: 2026-10-18
purpose: Lift a passage out of a document so it can be kept, referred to and found on its own.
principle: >-
  If a member selects a passage and saves it, it exists as a quote that names its
  source and the range it came from; the member finds it later without reopening
  the document.
state:
  - "source: Quote -> one Document"
  - "range: Quote -> one Range"
  - "text: Quote -> one Text"
  - "createdBy: Quote -> one User"
actions: [createQuote]
misfits:
  - A quote keeps its text when the source changes, so it can say something the document no longer says.
  - A quote's range is in the source's terms, and a revision that moves the passage leaves the range pointing at the wrong place.
aliases: [excerpt, highlight, clipping]
depends_on: [document-keeping]
appears_in:
  - Kindle highlights
  - Readwise
  - Notion block references
---

Quoting is [Commenting](/concepts/commenting) with the remark removed and the
passage kept. Both anchor to a range in a document; a quote exists for the
passage's sake and a comment for the remark's. The third misfit of commenting,
a passage that starts being referred to and needs an identity of its own, is
what quoting provides.
