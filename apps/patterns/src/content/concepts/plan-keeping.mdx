---
title: Plan keeping
added: 2026-11-19
purpose: Keep the steps of one piece of work in the order they will be done, so what is left can be changed before it happens.
principle: >-
  If the party doing a piece of work writes down the steps it means to take, it
  works through them in order and marks each as it goes; whoever can see the
  list can drop, move or add a step that has not started, and the work then
  follows the changed list rather than the one first written.
state:
  - "steps: Plan -> seq Step"
  - "addedBy: Step -> one Party"
  - "keeper: Plan -> one Party"
actions:
  addStep: A party puts a step into the plan at a place in its order.
  startStep: The keeper begins the next step.
  finishStep: The keeper marks the current step done.
  dropStep: A party removes a step that has not started.
  moveStep: A party moves a step that has not started to another place in the order.
misfits:
  - The list shows only its current form, so someone who read it earlier cannot tell which steps were added, dropped or moved since.
  - The list is written before the work has taught the keeper anything, and can go on being marked off after it has stopped describing the work.
  - A step names an act, not what the act will touch or cost, so the whole list can be read without learning what it will change.
  - Steps run in order, so a step that waits on someone holds up every step after it, and nothing in the list says it is waiting.
  - A step marked done says it was carried out, not that it worked.
  - A step can itself be done by keeping a plan, and the outer list shows that plan as a single step.
  - Two parties can change the list at once, and a step one of them drops may be the one the keeper has just started.
aliases: [task list, plan, checklist, agent to-dos]
depends_on: [staging]
appears_in:
  - Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI task lists
  - Cursor and Warp agent plans
  - checklists in GitHub issue and pull-request descriptions
---
