---
title: Assigning roles
added: 2026-11-14
purpose: Let what a member may do in a shared space be given and understood by a named role, instead of action by action for each member.
principle: >-
  If a member is given a role in a space, they can do there what the role
  allows and nothing else; anyone who knows the role's name knows what the
  member can do, and giving them a different role changes all of it at once.
state:
  - "role: Membership -> one Role"
  - "allows: Role -> set Capability"
actions:
  assignRole: Whoever manages the space gives a member a role when they are let in.
  changeRole: Whoever manages the space gives a member a different role, and what the member can do changes with it.
misfits:
  - A set with too many roles, or with custom roles made for each exception, is as hard to read as permissions set person by person.
  - When no role fits a member's part, the manager gives a wider one for convenience, and the member can do more than their part needs.
  - A member cannot tell what their role allows until an action is refused.
aliases: [role-based access, access level, permission level]
depends_on: [joining]
appears_in:
  - Google Docs (owner, editor, commenter, viewer)
  - GitHub repository roles
  - Notion
  - Figma
