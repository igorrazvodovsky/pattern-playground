---
title: Signing in
added: 2026-10-18
purpose: Mark that a member is present, so what they do is theirs and what happens meanwhile can be told to them later.
principle: >-
  If a member signs in, the system treats their acts as theirs until they are
  gone; while they are present, notices reach them directly, and while they are
  not, notices wait.
state:
  - "signedIn: User"
actions: [signIn]
misfits:
  - Signing out has no action, so presence is a fact that is set and never cleared.
  - A member signed in on two devices is one presence, and nothing says which device a notice reaches.
aliases: [login, session, presence]
appears_in:
  - Every system with an account
---

One action and one unary fact, and a concept only because the per-actor time
ceiling in the fixture world hangs on it: nothing a member does is replayed
before they signed in. Authentication, the checking of who a person is, is a
different concept and has no entry; the world assumes it.
