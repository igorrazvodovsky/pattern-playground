---
title: "Memory and continuity"
added: 2026-08-29
updated: 2026-09-27
description: "Decide what the collaboration keeps across sessions, what an actor sees when they come back, and how kept information is removed."
order: 7
presupposes: >-
  There is a policy on what the collaboration may remember: what may be
  kept, for how long, who may read it, and what the actor has agreed to.
  The policy is set before this sequence starts; no pattern here sets it.
enacts: [temporality, privacy]
sub-sequences:
  - id: keeping
    title: "Deciding what is kept"
    steps:
      - step: "Keep one record"
        rule: activity-log
        constituents:
          - slug: progressive-disclosure
            note: "entries expand on demand, so the timeline stays easy to scan and details stay one step away"
        gloss: >-
          Collect the accounts that other sequences write (of work, of
          permissions and approvals, and of reliance) into one record. Give
          every entry the same fields: who acted, what they did, what it
          applied to, and when. Make the record filterable from the start,
          and design it for new and returning actors as well as for audits.
      - step: "Record who made each change and why"
        rule: provenance-marking
        aspect: "recording origin when a change is made"
        gloss: >-
          Mark each change in the record with its origin (a person, a named
          rule, or a model run) when the change is made, because origin
          cannot be reconstructed later. Record any reason the collaboration
          wants to keep at the same time. The return steps read this.
      - step: "Choose what is kept about the actor"
        rule: selective-memory
        constituents:
          - slug: settings
            note: "where the memory controls usually live"
        gloss: >-
          Decide what the system keeps about the actor for next time: facts
          they stated, standing preferences, and less visible state such as
          what they have read, where they were, and work they set aside.
          Choose the default (remember unless asked, or forget unless asked),
          knowing that most actors never change it. Let the actor see and
          edit what is kept, and offer a session that keeps nothing.
    resulting: >-
      The collaboration keeps only what was chosen. Information the policy
      excludes is never written, which is cheaper and more reliable than
      deleting it later.
  - id: return
    title: "Supporting the actor's return"
    initiating: >-
      An actor comes back after an absence, short or long, to a
      collaboration that either continued working or waited. Each step here
      reads what the keeping steps wrote.
    steps:
      - step: "Restore the actor's place"
        constituents:
          - slug: deep-linking
            note: "the kept place is an address that the addressing steps already created"
        gloss: >-
          Decide whether to restore the actor's place automatically or offer
          to restore it: the conversation they set aside, the commitment
          deferred when they last closed, the unfinished change, and what
          they had already read. As part of resuming, check assumptions that
          may be out of date. Keep the option to start fresh.
      - step: "Show what changed while the actor was away"
        gloss: >-
          Show the changes made since the actor's last visit, limited to what
          they have open, without asking them to accept or reject anything:
          which parts changed, who or what changed them, and why. Include
          only changes that could wait; anything urgent was sent when it
          happened. If the summary is generated, mark its evidence.
      - step: "Let the actor return to named versions"
        gloss: >-
          Beyond the current session, where undo no longer applies, let the
          actor restore named versions, such as a publish, a release, or a
          version saved on purpose. Decide which moments get names, who
          names them, and how far back the history goes.
    resulting: >-
      The returning actor can see where they were, what changed, and how it
      changed. Each of these depends on what the keeping steps recorded.
  - id: forgetting
    title: "Enforcing the limits on memory"
    initiating: >-
      Kept information accumulates unless something removes it. The policy
      sets limits on what is remembered, and the design has to enforce them,
      both when the actor asks for something to be forgotten and when
      records are no longer needed.
    steps:
      - cluster: "Apply the limits to both kinds of kept information"
        gloss: >-
          Apply the policy to two kinds of kept information that are removed
          differently, in either order: what is remembered about the actor,
          and the actor's content with its history.
        steps:
          - step: "Let the actor remove what is remembered"
            rule: selective-memory
            aspect: "its forgetting controls"
            gloss: >-
              Let the actor remove remembered information per fact, per
              session, or per mode. Make sure a removed fact does not return,
              including through behaviour the system learned from it.
          - step: "Decide what removal leaves behind"
            rule: deletion
            aspect: "what remains after removal"
            gloss: >-
              Decide whether removing an item also removes its history: offer
              a recovery period, leave a placeholder, or keep an entry for the
              deletion in the record. Give the actor what the policy
              promised, and let the record show what was removed.
    resulting: >-
      What the collaboration remembers is limited by the design, not by
      storage, and the actor can see it, correct it, and remove it.
connections:

---

Other sequences produce records: committed changes, reviewed runs, granted
permissions. This sequence keeps those records but does not revisit the
decisions behind them. It decides once what is kept, designs the actor's
return after an absence of any length, and enforces the limits on what is
remembered.
