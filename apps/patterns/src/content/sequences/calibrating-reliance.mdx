---
title: "Calibrating reliance"
added: 2026-08-29
updated: 2026-09-27
description: "Put evidence on AI output so there is something to check, make accepting it take a moment of judgement where the stakes call for it, and keep a record that shows whether trust matches accuracy."
order: 6
presupposes: >-
  An AI is in use and its output will be acted on: an answer taken
  into a decision, a suggestion accepted into the work, a change approved
  into a document. The stakes of acting on wrong output have been judged
  for each surface, and they set how much of this sequence each surface
  needs. That judgement happens before this sequence, and no pattern here
  makes it.
enacts: [agency, learnability]
sub-sequences:
  - id: evidence
    title: "Making the output checkable"
    steps:
      - step: "Mark what made it"
        rule: provenance-marking
        optional: true
        gloss: >-
          Decide first whether machine-made content is marked, because once
          anything is marked, unmarked content reads as human-made. Decide it
          where the output travels, such as an export, a shared document, or
          a mixed thread, and make the mark survive copying. Skip this where
          the surface already shows its origin, as a conversation does.
      - cluster: "Put the evidence on the claims"
        gloss: >-
          Three marks answer three questions about the same claims: where
          they came from, how sure the system is, and how it got there. Let
          the stakes decide which ones this surface carries, and use each
          only where its question is the one the actor would ask.
        steps:
          - step: "Ground the claims"
            rule: citation
            gloss: >-
              Link each claim that needs it to the passage it rests on.
              Decide which claims need one as a policy, such as quotations,
              figures, and surprising or contested claims, and let a claim
              with nothing to point to say so.
            constituents:
              - slug: link-preview
                note: "lets a source be weighed without leaving the claim, with the passage as the first step and the document as the second"
          - step: "Let the confidence show"
            rule: expressed-uncertainty
            gloss: >-
              Mark doubtful spans with hedges, ranges, or visual marks, so
              scrutiny lands where it is needed. A claim the grounding step
              could not link to anything needs this mark.
          - step: "Expose the path"
            rule: transparent-reasoning
            optional: true
            gloss: >-
              Where the output was reached through several steps, show them
              as a trail the actor can scroll, expand, and challenge. Skip
              this for single-move output, such as a completion or a label.
      - step: "Answer why on demand"
        rule: explanation
        aspect: "its AI explanations"
        gloss: >-
          Let the actor open an explanation of the output, such as the
          factors behind a recommendation and the limits of what it drew on,
          and choose how deep it goes. Place it after the evidence, because a
          convincing explanation makes the actor less likely to open the
          sources.
  - id: acceptance
    title: "Interposing the judgement"
    initiating: >-
      The evidence is on the output and mostly unread: the marks reassure
      whether or not anyone follows them, and accepting still costs less
      than evaluating. The actor could judge and the stakes call for it, but
      nothing asks them to.
    steps:
      - step: "Make the acceptance cost a thought"
        rule: cognitive-forcing-functions
        gloss: >-
          Put a moment of active thought between the output and its
          acceptance: a wait, a reveal on demand, an answer given first, or a
          reason written down. Use it only where the stakes keep justifying
          it. Reasons written down here are kept for the record steps.
      - cluster: "Sharpen the friction"
        gloss: >-
          Two further forms, in no set order: one structures a single review,
          the other keeps the actor's skill up across many.
        steps:
          - step: "Structure what the review checks"
            rule: overreliance-checklist
            optional: true
            gloss: >-
              Where the ways a decision can go wrong are known in advance,
              list them, and make each item point somewhere the actor would
              not have looked. Skip this where they are too varied to list.
          - step: "Withhold the answer sometimes"
            rule: human-goes-first
            optional: true
            gloss: >-
              Where the surface is meant to support the actor's own
              competence, now and then withhold the recommendation, ask for
              the actor's answer, and show the two side by side. Skip this for
              one-off tasks.
  - id: account
    title: "Reading the reliance back"
    initiating: >-
      Acceptances, overrides, opened sources, and cleared checks have built
      up over the surface's use. Without a record, whether trust matches
      accuracy is judged on impressions.
    steps:
      - step: "Keep the record of the judgements"
        rule: activity-log
        gloss: >-
          Record what was accepted and what was overridden, which sources
          were opened, and the reasons given, each with who acted, what they
          did, what it applied to, and when. This is the only record that
          shows acceptances nobody read.
      - step: "Move the friction on evidence"
        rule: cognitive-forcing-functions
        aspect: "where it is spent"
        gloss: >-
          Add friction where acceptances go through unread, and move or
          remove a check that every actor clears in one motion, as an explicit
          decision. An override that keeps recurring goes to tuning.
    resulting: >-
      The output shows what it rests on, accepting it takes a judgement
      where a wrong answer would cost more, and the record shows whether both
      are working. Every mark and pause costs the actor attention.
connections:
  - kind: hands-off
    at: evidence/1
    to: working-on-the-same-thing
    note: "the origin mark matters most where machine and human contributions mix in one document, which that sequence handles per contribution"
  - kind: calls
    at: acceptance/2
    to: learning
    via: mastery
    note: "withholding the answer is practice, and keeping skills practised is that sequence's work"
  - kind: hands-off
    at: account/1
    to: memory-and-continuity
    note: "the record of judgements is part of what the collaboration remembers across sessions"
  - kind: hands-off
    at: account/2
    to: delegating-work/iterate/2
    via: ai-tuning
    note: "an override that keeps recurring becomes a standing rule in that sequence"
---

An AI is in use, and its output is about to be acted on by an actor who did not produce it and cannot tell how far to trust it. These steps are taken for each AI surface rather than once for the whole system: every other AI sequence arrives here where its output meets the actor.
