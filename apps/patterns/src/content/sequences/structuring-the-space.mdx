---
title: "Structuring the space"
added: 2026-08-28
updated: 2026-09-27
description: "Turning a settled information architecture into a space that is partitioned, navigable, and addressable."
order: 1
presupposes: >-
  The actor's objects, activities, and labels have names, and the design is
  tool-shaped: a spatial surface is its primary frame. The structure those
  names sit in is not presupposed; the hierarchy, where there is one, is
  decided inside this sequence and checked against the actor's own
  categories there. If the objects and labels cannot yet be named, the
  design is not ready for this sequence.
enacts: [privacy, agency, shareability]
sub-sequences:
  - id: layout
    title: "Laying out the space"
    steps:
      - step: "Partition the work into contexts"
        rule: workspace
        optional: true
        gloss: >-
          Draw the boundaries where attention and responsibility divide. A
          context also decides who can see what it holds, so usually
          the permission lines come first and the attention lines follow.
          Decide the switcher between contexts here too. Where the work
          forms one context, say so and move on.
        constituents:
          - slug: hub-and-spoke
            note: "the switcher over the contexts: the workspace list is the hub and each context a spoke"
      - choice: "Recognise what kind of space each context is"
        exclusive: true
        gloss: >-
          Read the kind from what each context holds and how the actor moves
          through it: one continuous space, an ordered sequence, one
          collection, or sections that differ in kind. Take one arm per
          region, where a region begins wherever the movement rule changes: a
          file browser and the open file are two regions of one workspace.
          Recognise again per platform, since the same space is often a hub
          on a phone and a tree on a desktop. For sections, start from the
          genre's default in [navigation](/patterns/navigation-overview) and
          let the growth steps correct it. A surface whose only navigation is
          done or cancel is a corridor, decided in the routing choice of
          [making changes](/sequences/making-changes).
        alternatives:
          - step: "Navigate by viewport"
            rule: pan-and-zoom
            gloss: >-
              One continuous space. Make position and scale nameable, so the
              addressing steps can give them addresses.
          - step: "Prescribe the order"
            rule: step-by-step
            gloss: >-
              An ordered sequence whose later steps depend on earlier ones.
              What has been entered has to survive a stop: the addressing
              steps give each step an address, and keeping the entered state
              is handed to [memory and continuity](/sequences/memory-and-continuity).
          - step: "Offer the sequence, allow jumps"
            rule: pyramid
            gloss: >-
              An ordered sequence without dependencies. The actor expects
              their progress remembered, which is handed to
              [memory and continuity](/sequences/memory-and-continuity).
          - step: "Dissolve into the collection"
            rule: overview-detail
            gloss: >-
              One collection browsed item by item. There is no topology to
              choose, and the design continues in
              [composing views](/sequences/composing-views).
          - step: "File into a hierarchy"
            rule: multilevel-tree
            gloss: >-
              Sections that differ in kind, with subsections behind them and a
              categorisation the actor can learn. Check the hierarchy against
              how actors categorise, and prefer broad and shallow to deep and
              narrow.
          - step: "Keep one surface"
            rule: flat-navigation
            gloss: >-
              Tools that differ in kind but fit on one surface.
          - step: "Connect everything to everything"
            rule: fully-connected
            gloss: >-
              Sections that differ in kind, with routine journeys between
              them.
          - step: "Route through a hub"
            rule: hub-and-spoke
            gloss: >-
              Sections that differ in kind and rarely need each other. What
              the hub reports across the spokes is handed to
              [making sense at scale](/sequences/making-sense-at-scale).
  - id: addressing
    title: "Making the space addressable"
    initiating: >-
      The layout has produced states worth returning to: a context entered,
      an item open, a step reached, a position in a space. The only record of
      how to get back is in the actor's head.
    steps:
      - step: "Give every state worth returning to an address"
        rule: deep-linking
        gloss: >-
          Address each of those states, including the step reached and the
          position and scale named by the layout arms. Decide which job each
          value in the address does: identify the thing, narrow the view, or
          point inside it. Leave out what the actor set once for themselves.
          Every address issued has to keep resolving, which the growth steps
          have to honour.
      - step: "Decide which changes deserve a Back step"
        rule: deep-linking
        aspect: "its history dimension"
        gloss: >-
          Decide over the moves the layout produces: switching context,
          opening an item, moving within a space. Moves a view makes on its
          own, such as a filter changed or a panel opened, belong to
          [composing views](/sequences/composing-views).
      - step: "Expose the link"
        rule: deep-linking
        aspect: "its sharing surface"
        gloss: >-
          Add a copy-link control wherever the layout produced a surface with
          no address bar of its own.
  - id: growth
    title: "Growing past the model"
    initiating: >-
      A region has outgrown its model, and the strain shows as one pain at a
      time. Read the pain from evidence, not guesses: actors bouncing in and
      out of items to answer a scanning question, search launched from inside
      a section, Back pressed in runs, and support asked where something
      lives. The topologies are weighed against each other here, for the
      region that hurts.
    steps:
      - choice: "Step up within the family"
        exclusive: true
        gloss: >-
          Take one arm per pain, re-running the recognition over that region
          alone. Count the actors' relearning as part of the cost, since every
          arm changes a model they have learned.
        alternatives:
          - step: "Split the surface into a hub and spokes"
            rule: hub-and-spoke
            gloss: >-
              A flat surface whose tools outgrow the screen.
          - step: "Open direct paths between spokes"
            rule: fully-connected
            gloss: >-
              A hub whose cross-spoke journeys have turned routine, so the
              return through the hub is paid on every trip.
          - step: "Push the depth into a tree"
            rule: multilevel-tree
            gloss: >-
              Global chrome that no longer fits its sections. On a phone the
              same pain usually takes the previous arm's shape instead: the
              tab bar keeps its first few sections and the rest go behind a
              More tab, a small hub.
          - step: "Add sideways paths before changing shape"
            gloss: >-
              A tree whose sideways trips have turned routine. Add links
              between the related sections first, then a command menu and
              search that bypass the hierarchy, and change the shape only
              after those.
            constituents:
              - slug: command-menu
                note: "the escape hatch for the actor who knows the destination's name"
              - slug: searching
                note: "the escape hatch for the actor who knows the item rather than the place"
      - step: "Re-partition the regions"
        rule: hybrid-patterns
        gloss: >-
          When a pain is confined to one part of a region, split the region
          and give that part its own model, instead of changing the model for
          the whole.
      - step: "Keep named states resolving"
        rule: deep-linking
        aspect: "its contract"
        gloss: >-
          Make the reorganised space answer at every address it ever issued.

---

The actor's objects, activities, and labels have names, and the space they
sit in needs a layout. This is usually the first sequence walked. It gives
[composing views](/sequences/composing-views) the surface its views sit on,
and it is walked again, one region at a time, whenever part of the space
outgrows its model.
