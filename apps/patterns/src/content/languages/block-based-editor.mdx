A block-based editor holds documents made of blocks: paragraphs, headings, lists, images, embeds and small structured records. The actor inserts, moves, converts and links blocks, and content can stay loose and gain structure one block at a time.

The patterns run from the editor as a whole, through the links between documents and the work several people do on one, to the moves on blocks: inserting, selecting, moving and converting them.

The starter leaves out views that treat blocks as records, such as tables and boards, and publishing. Add them when the documents become records or are published.
