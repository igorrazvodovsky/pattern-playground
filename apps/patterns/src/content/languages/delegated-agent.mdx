A delegated agent takes a task and works on it without the actor at its side. The actor frames the request and sets what the agent may do; the agent runs, pauses where it needs a decision, and hands back results, which the actor reviews, corrects, or takes in hand.

The patterns run from framing the request and granting autonomy, through what the actor sees while the agent works and where it stops for approval, to receiving the work, correcting it, and keeping a record of what was done.

The starter leaves out the conversation itself, which is the conversation-first starter's when the request is made in one, and the finer evidence patterns of calibrating reliance beyond citation and the acceptance check.
