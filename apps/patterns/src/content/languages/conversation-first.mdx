A conversation-first artifact puts a conversation with an agent at its centre. The actor arrives with a question, a goal or something loose, and the agent answers, asks, or acts from inside the exchange. Other surfaces, such as a document or a form, appear within the conversation or open beside it.

The patterns run from what the agent can do and how an exchange opens, through carrying each kind of request, checking before consequential acts, and recovering when the exchange breaks, to how it ends or pauses and what the agent keeps from it.

The starter leaves out work the agent runs without the actor watching, which is the delegated agent's, and the gradual climb of assistance inside other surfaces, which is the assistance escalation sequence's.
