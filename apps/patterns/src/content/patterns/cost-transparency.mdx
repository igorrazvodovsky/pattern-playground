What the machine's work costs is visible when it matters — estimated before the run, metered during it, and bounded by quotas the actor can see against their remaining allowance.

## Forms

Recurring forms: pre-run estimates of time and money, live token or cost meters, remaining-quota displays, cheap draft modes offered before the expensive run, and hard ceilings that stop work at the limit.
