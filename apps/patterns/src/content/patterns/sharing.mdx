The work is its maker's alone, and it now needs others inside it. Handing copies around forks it; opening everything to everyone destroys the freedom to work rough. Sharing is the explicit act at the boundary: named others are let in at a graded capability — view, comment, edit — before any joint mechanism exists.

The grant is the first collaboration decision, not a detail of storage. Every surveyed shipping product stages it the same way: new work defaults to private, the share act names people (or a container's membership) at a tier, tiers inherit downward, and nobody grants above what they hold. A viewer can be offered a request path to the higher tier, which keeps the boundary explicit in both directions.

## Forces

- Reach vs roughness: the more people the grant admits, the less freely the maker explores in front of them.
- Fine grants vs common ground: permissions cut too fine leave collaborators unable to see the same thing, and the conversation falls back to screenshots.
