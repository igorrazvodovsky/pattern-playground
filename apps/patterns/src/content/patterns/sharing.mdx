* * *

**When the only way to let someone else see or work on something is to send a copy or let them into the whole workspace, copies soon diverge and opening the workspace exposes rough or unrelated work. Letting people in by folder or by item fixes that, but access quickly becomes hard to read. A folder's access passes down to what is inside it while single items get exceptions of their own, links get forwarded to people they were not meant for, and someone who hits a link or an action they cannot use is left stuck with no way to ask for access.**

Therefore,

**Let whoever answers for a workspace, folder or item invite named people, groups or an outside agent—or create a link that carries access when recipients should not need an account—and give each one of the space's roles, starting with the narrowest role that fits. Pass a folder's access down to what it holds, allow a single item to be shared without opening the rest of its folder, and never let anyone give more access than they have themselves. Show on every folder and item who can reach it, whether their access was given here or inherited from above, and how to change or remove it. When someone opens a link they cannot reach, or tries an action above their role, let them ask for the access right there and send the request to the person who can grant it.**
