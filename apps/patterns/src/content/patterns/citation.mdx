Generated claims arrive with pointers to the sources behind them — inline marks, chips, footnotes, a source panel — so the actor can verify by following rather than by re-researching.

## Forms

Recurring forms: inline markers resolving to a source list, hoverable citation chips, a source browser beside the answer, and previews of the retrieved context an answer was grounded in.
