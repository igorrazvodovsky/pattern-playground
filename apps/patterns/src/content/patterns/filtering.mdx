**When the actor wants only the items in a large collection that share some properties, scanning or sorting the whole list is slow, and they miss items they were looking for.**

Design systems such as PatternFly, GitLab Pajamas and Carbon show the filters in force as removable chips or tokens above the results, with a way to clear them all.

Therefore,

**Let the actor narrow the collection by stating [filter](/concepts/filtering) conditions on the properties its items record, each one a [bounded choice](/patterns/bounded-choice) over a property's values, and update the shown items as each condition changes. Keep every condition in force beside the results as a chip or clause the actor can read, change or remove on its own, with a way to clear them all, and say how many items are shown out of how many. When nothing matches, keep the conditions in place and show which ones emptied the list.**

### LLM-powered attribute search

When no attribute matches what the actor types, the query is passed to a language model to find a match among the available attributes.

If that fails, the actor can hand the filtering to an [agent](/patterns/agent) as a task. The agent can reach data beyond the attributes and values the actor sees.

#### Flow 1

1. Enter a query that does not have a match in the available attributes
2. Create a task to handle it
3. Show "thinking..." placeholder
4. Replace it with a task result reference.

#### Flow 2

1. Create a task outside filtering whose result can be used to filter the collection.
2. Follow steps 3-4 from Flow 1.
