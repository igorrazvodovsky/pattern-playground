The word "dashboard" bundles three things: an *artefact* (tiled charts on one screen), a *use* (monitoring at a glance), and an *authorship stance* (composed at design-time, fixed for everyone). The corpus keeps them apart. The use lives at [purpose-keyed view](/patterns/purpose-keyed-view); the authorship stance is [malleability](/patterns/malleability)'s territory; this entry holds the artefact — the *visual* genre, in the charting corpus where its problems (tile layout, chart choice per tile, cross-tile consistency) actually live.

This is a placeholder: it exists so the boundary is visible from both sides. A tiled screen over unconnected numbers is just charts sharing a screen — it earns family membership with [coordinated views](/patterns/coordinated-views) only when the tiles are coupled and the metrics beneath them interconnect.
