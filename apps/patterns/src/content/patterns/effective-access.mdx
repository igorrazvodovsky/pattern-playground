* * *

**When each person holds one role per space, the list on each item is enough. Once access comes through roles, groups, inheritance and links at once, nobody can tell what a person can do across the space, who can take a sensitive action, or which of several grants gives someone their access. Access given for convenience then goes unnoticed, and an actor's reach cannot be read at all.**

Therefore,

**For any person or agent, show the combined result of everything that gives them access, and next to each part, the grant it comes from. Let the same view be opened from an action, listing everyone who can take it and why. Where several grants give the same access, name all of them, so that narrowing one shows whether the access actually goes.**
