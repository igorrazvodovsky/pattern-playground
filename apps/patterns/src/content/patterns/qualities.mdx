# Qualities

The experiential dimensions of an interface — what it feels like to use something. A quality cuts across many patterns, and most patterns carry several qualities at once. Inspired by Löwgren & Stolterman's [use-oriented qualities](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262296922/thoughtful-interaction-design/) — properties a designer learns to perceive and deliberately shape — but the list reflects what this repertoire pays attention to.

## Control and shape

Who decides, who reshapes, and how much structure the interaction requires.

- [Agency](/patterns/qualities/agency) — the distribution of decision-making between system and user
- [Adaptability](/patterns/qualities/adaptation) — how the system modifies its behaviour in response to user, context, or inferred intent
- [Malleability](/patterns/qualities/malleability) — how easily users can reshape their tools
- [Formality](/patterns/qualities/formality) — how much structure the system needs from the user at a given moment

## Encounter in time

How interaction unfolds — turn by turn, beat by beat, dense or sparse.

- [Conversation](/patterns/qualities/conversation) — the dynamic exchange through which actors coordinate action
- [Temporality](/patterns/qualities/temporality) — time as the medium through which trust, rhythm, and agency are negotiated
- [Density](/patterns/qualities/density) — the amount of information an interface provides over a series of moments

## Inclusion and growth

Who can engage, and how their competence develops.

- [Accessibility](/patterns/qualities/a11y) — inclusive engagement across diverse abilities
- [Learnability](/patterns/qualities/learnability) — structural support for the transition from novice to expert

## Visibility and sharing

What is seen, by whom, and how state travels.

- [Privacy](/patterns/qualities/privacy) — strategic control over what is visible to different actors across contexts
- [Shareability](/patterns/qualities/shareability) — the capture and faithful reconstruction of system state through links
