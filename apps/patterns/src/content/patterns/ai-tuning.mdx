situation:
  initiating: >-
    the agent's default behaviour is close but not right, and the mismatch keeps recurring;
    correcting it turn by turn is work the actor repeats forever, so what they need is to
    change the standing behaviour rather than this instance of it
  resulting:
    - >-
      the standing behaviour is the actor's to shape, so a correction made once holds across
      sessions instead of being re-issued each time
    - >-
      how far the system may act on its own is now something the actor set, which moves the
      responsibility for that boundary onto them
    - >-
      tuning accumulates and then goes quiet: behaviour configured months ago reads later as
      simply how the system is, including to the actor who configured it
