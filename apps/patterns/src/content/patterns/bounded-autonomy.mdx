The actor grants the system authority with explicit edges — how much it may spend, touch, or decide, and for how long — so delegation can start small and grow with demonstrated competence.

## Forms

Recurring forms: autonomy levels per task (suggest / confirm / execute), budgets in time, actions, or money, per-capability rather than per-application grants, and thresholds that automatically demote autonomy when risk rises.
