When an actor compares items in a [data view](/patterns/data-view), the attribute that settles the decision often does not exist as a stored column: total cost is split across price and shipping, a response deadline is buried inside an email body, or vendor titles mix brands, models, and conditions in incompatible formats. Showing and hiding existing fields through [attribute visibility](/patterns/attribute-visibility) cannot surface a field that is not there.

Derived attributes lets the actor define a new attribute across the collection—by formula over existing fields, by prompting an AI to extract a value from each item's details, or by normalising inconsistent values into a common format—and treat the result like any native attribute. Once created, the derived attribute participates in [attribute visibility](/patterns/attribute-visibility), [sorting](/patterns/sorting), and [filtering](/patterns/filtering) on equal terms with stored fields. Because derived and inferred values can be wrong or absent, each value keeps its provenance inspectable (hovering a computed total reveals its source fields so the actor can verify or surface them) and marks unfound items explicitly rather than fabricating values.
