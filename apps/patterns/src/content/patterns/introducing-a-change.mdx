* * *

**A change to an artifact people already use can break habits, procedures and shared expectations built on the old version. An announcement that interrupts their work gets dismissed unread, and no announcement leaves them to discover the change by surprise, often in the middle of a task.**

GitHub's Primer and GitLab's Pajamas both advise announcing new features without blocking the actor, close to where the feature is used, and in a form that can be dismissed. Removals and moved controls are harder than additions, because the actor's existing route stops working.

Therefore,

**Keep existing ways of working available through the change where possible. Tell each affected actor what changed and why, at the place where they will meet it, in a form they can dismiss and find again later. Where the change alters how they work, let them try it before committing, and let them go back if it does not suit them.**
