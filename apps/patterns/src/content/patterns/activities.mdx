Sustained, motive-driven engagements that combine multiple [actions](/patterns/actions) toward larger goals. Activities give actions their meaning — the same action (e.g. searching) serves different purposes depending on the activity it's part of.

## Intelligence

Patterns for working with AI capabilities woven into the interface.

- [Bot](/patterns/activities/bot) — the conversational partner mediating between intent and action
- [Prompt](/patterns/activities/prompt) — expressing what you want the bot to do
- [Conversation](/patterns/activities/conversation) — sustained back-and-forth dialogue, built from conversational [operations](/patterns/operations)
- [Generated content](/patterns/activities/generated-content) — what the bot produces
- [Transparent reasoning](/patterns/activities/transparent-reasoning) — making the bot's thinking visible
- [Embedded intelligence](/patterns/activities/embedded-intelligence) — AI capabilities woven into existing workflows
- [AI tuning](/patterns/activities/ai-tuning) — configuring bot behaviour and preferences

## Collaboration

Patterns for multi-actor coordination around shared artefacts and goals.

- [Collaboration](/patterns/activities/collaboration) — coordination framework for shared work
- [Activity log](/patterns/activities/activity-log) — chronological record of who did what
- [Activity feed](/patterns/activities/activity-feed) — aggregated stream of events

## Learning & growth

Supporting actors in developing expertise and understanding over time.

- [Onboarding](/patterns/activities/onboarding) — first-time experience establishing mental models
- [Help](/patterns/activities/help) — assistance and guidance when needed
- [Mastery](/patterns/activities/mastery) — supporting long-term skill acquisition

## Environments

Sustained contexts in which work happens.

- [Workspace](/patterns/activities/workspace) — unified environment for tasks
- [Living document](/patterns/activities/living-document) — documents that evolve through use
- [Live presentation](/patterns/activities/live-presentation) — real-time shared viewing and annotation
