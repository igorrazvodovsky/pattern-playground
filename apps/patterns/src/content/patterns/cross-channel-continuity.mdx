* * *

**When each channel is shaped as a product of its own, an item found in one cannot be found in another, names and categories change between them, and moving in the middle of a task means starting over.**

Therefore,

**Make every channel a way into the same environment. Give each item one name and one address in all channels, let a link from any channel open that item, in the state the actor left it where that matters, and where a channel cannot do something, say where it can be done.**

## Resources & references

- Resmini & Rosati (2011) [Pervasive Information Architecture: Designing Cross-Channel User Experiences](https://doi.org/10.1016/C2009-0-62452-1): consistency, resilience and correlation as properties an information architecture keeps across channels
- Resmini, [Information architecture for ubiquitous ecologies](https://andrearesmini.com/blog/information-architecture-for-ubiquitous-ecologies): touchpoints designed as separate experiences leave gaps between them
