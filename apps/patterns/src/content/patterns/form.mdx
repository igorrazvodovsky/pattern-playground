The structured collection of input from the actor, balancing guidance with the freedom to express complex or messy information. Each field is an act of [data entry](/patterns/data-entry), each constrained field a [bounded choice](/patterns/bounded-choice). The whole commits the actor to the system's shape up front: a question of [formality](/patterns/formality). For the form *mechanism* see <ComponentRef id="actions-application-form--docs">corresponding component</ComponentRef>.

## Forms and conversation

Forms can be embedded in a <ComponentRef id="actions-coordination-messaging--docs">messaging</ComponentRef> interface for a more [conversational](/patterns/conversation) interaction; a [conversation](/patterns/conversation) may in turn resolve into a form once a dialogue stabilises around well-structured data collection. The two are endpoints of one continuum — how much of the structure the actor supplies, and how much the system does.

Distributed across the exchange, the form's fields are asked and answered turn by turn, each control rendered inline in a message — the form instance of the [inline interface](/patterns/inline-interface).

## Forms and AI

A form makes the actor translate their situation into the system's fields. When the system can parse raw input — an image, a PDF, pasted text, a voice dump — against the form's schema, that labour inverts: the actor's job shifts from *entry* to *verification*, reviewing pre-filled answers rather than supplying them. That shift, and the trust loop it requires, belong to [data entry](/patterns/data-entry#ai--verification).

## Related patterns

### Constituent moves
- [Bounded choice](/patterns/bounded-choice) — committing a value from a set the actor didn't author; the move behind selects, radios, and checkboxes.
- [Sections](/patterns/sections) — splitting a long form into adaptive, separately disclosed parts.
- [Progressive disclosure](/patterns/progressive-disclosure) — revealing depth only as it becomes relevant.
- [Wizard](/patterns/wizard) and [Step by step](/patterns/step-by-step) — fragmenting a complex submission into gated, sequential steps.
- [Formality](/patterns/formality) — how much structure the system demands of the actor, and when.

### Complementary
- [Data entry](/patterns/data-entry) — the broader move each field enacts; the form is one of its containers.
- [Conversation](/patterns/conversation) — may resolve into a form when a dialogue stabilises around structured data collection.
- [Localization](/patterns/localization) — locale-aware parsing and format tolerance for input.
- [Good defaults](/patterns/good-defaults) — pre-selecting likely values to remove entry entirely.
- [Status feedback](/patterns/status-feedback) — validation and system status during and after entry.
- [Saving](/patterns/saving) — autosave and manual save strategies for in-progress and committed input.

### Tangentially related
- [Disabled state](/patterns/state-disabled) — alternatives to disabling form elements.

The constituent controls and primitives (Input, Textarea, Select, Checkbox, Button, Details) and the convergent layout, labelling, and validation craft are documented on the <ComponentRef id="actions-application-form--docs">Form component</ComponentRef> page.
