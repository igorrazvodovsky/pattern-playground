The delivery of system events to the actor's attention, balancing awareness against interruption. As one of three ways to give [status feedback](/patterns/status-feedback), every notification has three parts: *purpose*, *type*, and *scope*.

## Purpose

Signifies the intention and the emotional tone of the information being communicated. Each status is associated with a specific color and icon, ensuring a consistent and universal user experience.

- Information
- Success
- Warning
- Error

## Type

Allows to tailor the disruptiveness of the notification.

## Scope

### Contextual

Localised messages tied to a specific place, providing timely feedback or updates relevant to the user’s immediate context.

...

### Global

System-wide alerts that inform the user about events or statuses affecting the entire application, regardless of their current location within it.

## Batching

Events of the same kind can be delivered together as one notification at set times instead of one at a time. The evidence on whether this helps is mixed. In a field experiment on smartphones, batching every app's notifications into three deliveries a day lowered stress and improved attention compared with constant delivery, and turning notifications off altogether raised anxiety about missing things. A workplace study of email found that batching was linked to higher self-rated productivity but found no evidence that it lowered stress. Where batching is used, the delivery times are predictable and chosen by the actor, and events of the highest grade are still delivered on their own.

A batch can be shown as a generated summary rather than a list. A wrong summary is then read as if the source had said it. Generated notification summaries on one phone platform produced false headlines attributed to a news organisation and were withdrawn for news apps; they returned set in italics, with a warning and a switch to turn them off. A generated summary is opt-in, set apart visually from the source's own text, and links each point to the event it came from.
