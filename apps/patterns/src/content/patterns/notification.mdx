The delivery of system events to the actor's attention, balancing awareness against interruption. As one of three ways to give [status feedback](/patterns/status-feedback), every notification has three parts: *purpose*, *type*, and *scope*.

## Purpose

Signifies the intention and the emotional tone of the information being communicated. Each status is associated with a specific colour and icon, ensuring a consistent and universal experience.

- *Information* — neutral events and background updates that need no action.
- *Success* — confirmation that an operation or background run completed as intended.
- *Warning* — a measure, deadline, or allowance tracked for the actor is approaching a limit while there is still time to act.
- *Error* — something failed or stopped and could not complete on its own.

## Scope

### Contextual

Localised messages tied to a specific place, providing timely feedback or updates relevant to the user’s immediate context.

...

### Global

System-wide alerts that inform the user about events or statuses affecting the entire application, regardless of their current location within it.
