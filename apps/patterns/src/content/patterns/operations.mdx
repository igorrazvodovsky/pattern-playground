# Operations

Automatic, infrastructural interactions that should just work. Operations are the reliable substrate beneath conscious action — well-established patterns with standardised implementations that actors use without deliberation.

>The boundary between operations and [actions](/patterns/actions) is fluid. An action practised enough becomes operational; an operation in an unfamiliar context demands conscious attention.

## Conversation building blocks

Primitives for constructing conversational flows. These form the turn-taking substrate that [conversation](/patterns/activities/conversation) as an activity is built from.

### Conversational activities

Core interaction moves: the actor asking a question through [inquiry](/patterns/operations/conversation/conversational-activities/inquiry-user), making an [open request](/patterns/operations/conversation/conversational-activities/open-request), the agent doing [extended telling](/patterns/operations/conversation/conversational-activities/extended-telling), and the agent asking a question through [inquiry](/patterns/operations/conversation/conversational-activities/inquiry-agent).

### Sequence management

Handling breakdowns and transitions within a sequence: [agent repair](/patterns/operations/conversation/conversation-management/agent-repair), [user repair](/patterns/operations/conversation/conversation-management/user-repair), [sequence completion](/patterns/operations/conversation/sequence-management/sequence-completion), and [abort](/patterns/operations/conversation/sequence-management/abort).

### Conversation management

Initiating and ending encounters: the [agent opening](/patterns/operations/conversation/sequence-management/agent-opening), [user opening](/patterns/operations/conversation/sequence-management/user-opening), establishing [capability & scope](/patterns/operations/conversation/conversation-management/capability-and-scope), [closing](/patterns/operations/conversation/sequence-management/closing), or [disengaging without closing](/patterns/operations/conversation/conversation-management/disengage-without-closing).
