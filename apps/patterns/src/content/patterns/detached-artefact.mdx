situation:
  initiating: >-
    the conversation has produced a thing that now matters more than the talk that made it —
    a document, code, a diagram — and in the reply stream it scrolls away, unversioned and
    editable only by asking again
  resulting:
    - >-
      the work-product holds still on its own surface while the conversation continues
      alongside as the way of steering it — talk and thing stop competing for the same column
    - >-
      two surfaces now claim the truth: the artefact and the transcript can disagree about
      what was decided, and edits made directly don't narrate themselves back into the talk
    - >-
      the artefact outlives the exchange — versionable, exportable, shareable — which is
      where the conversation's value ends up living
relationships:
  hosted-by:
    - to: conversation
      note: born in the exchange and promoted out of it; the chat remains the artefact's control surface
  follows:
    - to: generated-content
      note: the promotion acts on output the generation produced in-stream
  complements:
    - to: editing-in-place
      note: once detached, the artefact is edited directly rather than re-asked for
    - to: draft-and-publish
      note: the detached artefact is the surface draft and published states attach to
  related:
    - to: workspace
      note: a minimal workspace grown out of a chat — one context, two surfaces
    - to: living-document
      note: a detached artefact that keeps being regenerated shades into a living document
  enacts:
    - to: malleability
      note: gives the output a surface where direct manipulation applies at all

A piece of generated output is promoted out of the reply stream onto its own persistent surface — beside the conversation, directly editable, iterated in place while the dialogue steers it.
