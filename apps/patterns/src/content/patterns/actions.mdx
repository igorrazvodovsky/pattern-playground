# Actions

Conscious, goal-directed interactions. Actions require the actor's focal awareness — they know what they're trying to achieve and deliberately choose how to get there.

Actions are organised by *lifecycle stage*: where the actor is in their journey from initial exploration to coordinated output. The stages aren't strictly sequential — actors move between them fluidly, and many patterns serve multiple stages. [Filtering](/patterns/actions/seeking/filtering), for instance, supports both initial seeking and later refinement during sense-making.

## Seeking

Finding and accessing information. The actor has intent but may not yet know exactly what they need.

- [Searching](/patterns/actions/seeking/searching) — query-driven retrieval
- [Filtering](/patterns/actions/seeking/filtering) — narrowing scope during exploration
- [Sorting](/patterns/actions/seeking/sorting) — ordering for pattern recognition
- [Data view](/patterns/actions/seeking/data-view/data-view) — structured collections supporting browsing
- [Item view](/patterns/actions/seeking/item-view) — detailed examination of individual entities
- [Command menu](/patterns/actions/seeking/command-menu) — quick access to actions and navigation
- [Progressive disclosure](/patterns/actions/seeking/progressive-disclosure) — revealing complexity gradually
- [Dynamic hyperlinks](/patterns/actions/seeking/dynamic-hyperlinks) — contextual navigation within content

## Evaluation

Assessing and comparing what's been found. The actor has located candidates and needs to judge relevance, quality, or fit.

- <ComponentRef id="actions-evaluation-list--docs">List</ComponentRef> — scannable sequences for comparison
- <ComponentRef id="actions-evaluation-table--docs">Table</ComponentRef> — structured multi-attribute comparison
- <ComponentRef id="actions-evaluation-timeline--docs">Timeline</ComponentRef> — temporal sequence and progression
- [Focus and context](/patterns/actions/evaluation/focus-and-context/focus-and-context) — balancing detail with situational awareness
- [Text lens](/patterns/actions/evaluation/text-lense) — viewing content through different perspectives

## Sense-making

Understanding and integrating information. The actor is building mental models, finding patterns, and creating meaning from what they've evaluated.

- <ComponentRef id="actions-sense-making-card--docs">Card</ComponentRef> — contained units for scanning and comparison
- [Annotation](/patterns/actions/sense-making/annotation) — adding layers of meaning to content
- [Grouping](/patterns/actions/sense-making/grouping) — clustering for categorical understanding
- [Tag](/patterns/actions/sense-making/tag) — collaborative categorisation
- [View](/patterns/actions/sense-making/view) — saved perspectives on information
- [Explanation](/patterns/actions/sense-making/explanation) — clarifying system behaviour and decisions
- [Block-based editor](/patterns/actions/sense-making/block-based-editor) — modular content assembly
- [Dashboard](/patterns/actions/sense-making/dashboard) — needs-based views that frame a collection for a specific purpose

- [Form](/patterns/actions/application/form) — structured input with validation
- [Data entry](/patterns/actions/application/data-entry) — philosophy of frictionless input
- <ComponentRef id="actions-application-dialog--docs">Dialog</ComponentRef> — focused modal interaction
- <ComponentRef id="actions-application-drawer--docs">Drawer</ComponentRef> — contextual side panel
- [Wizard](/patterns/actions/application/wizard) — guided multi-stage tasks
- [Saving](/patterns/actions/application/saving) — committing changes with appropriate friction
- [Deletion](/patterns/actions/application/deletion) — controlled removal with safeguards
- [Action consequences](/patterns/actions/application/action-consequences) — understanding impact before committing
- [Settings](/patterns/actions/application/settings) — configuration and preferences
- [Suggestion](/patterns/actions/application/suggestion) — system-initiated recommendations
- [Next-best action](/patterns/actions/application/next-best-action) — workflow-level recommendations
- [AI completion](/patterns/actions/application/ai-completion) — inline generation
- [Assisted task completion](/patterns/actions/application/assisted-task-completion) — the spectrum from [autocomplete](/patterns/operations/autocomplete) and [autofill](/patterns/operations/autofill) through AI completion and next-best action
- [Cognitive forcing functions](/patterns/actions/application/cognitive-forcing-functions) — analytical friction before accepting recommendations
- [Template](/patterns/actions/application/template) — predefined structures for content creation
