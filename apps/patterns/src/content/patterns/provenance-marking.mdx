Content carries a legible mark of what made it — a label on the AI's turn, a badge on the generated block, metadata in the file — so origin survives the distance between maker and reader.

## Forms

Recurring forms: disclosure labels on AI turns and features, visual conventions (colour, iconography) reserved for machine output, watermarks readable by humans or software, and per-span authorship records in mixed documents.
