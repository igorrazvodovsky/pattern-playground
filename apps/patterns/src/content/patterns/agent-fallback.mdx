* * *

**A control with a fixed vocabulary stops helping where the actor's words match nothing in it. The actor is left with an empty list and an unfinished task, and the ways on are to guess other words or to start again somewhere else and say it all a second time.**

Therefore,

**Where the control has nothing for what the actor typed, offer in the same place to pass the task to an agent. Give the agent what was typed, which control it was typed in and what the actor has open. Where the answer is something the control could have produced, return it in that form: the filters set, or the command chosen with its arguments filled in.**
