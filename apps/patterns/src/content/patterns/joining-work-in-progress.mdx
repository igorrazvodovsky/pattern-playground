* * *

**Structures built by others look arbitrary to someone who was not there when they were set up. A joiner who cannot tell a deliberate convention from an accident either follows conventions without understanding them or breaks ones that mattered.**

The joiner has to learn three things at once: how the artifact works, what this space holds, and why it holds it in this form. The first is what a first run teaches. The other two exist only in this space and in the heads of the people who built it, and they are lost when those people leave unless the space keeps them.

Therefore,

**Show the joiner what is already in the space and the reasons behind it: why it is organised as it is, what its names mean, which conventions are local, and where to find how each came about. Keep those reasons with the structures they explain, so that they are there for whoever joins next.**
