  initiating: >-
    the artefact was accurate when it was written and its context has since moved; keeping it
    current by hand is work nobody schedules, so it quietly becomes wrong while still being read
    as current
  resulting:
    - >-
      the artefact moves with its context, so what a reader finds reflects now rather than
      whenever it was last touched by a person
    - >-
      something is doing work on the document without being asked, and that work has a cost that
      has to stay visible or it runs unbudgeted
    - >-
      the document no longer has one author: the reader can't tell what was written, what was
      regenerated, and what changed since they last read it

The continuous maintenance of a document's relevance as its context evolves, transforming a static artefact into one that stays current through automated updates and human curation. Seamlessly integrates [generated content](/patterns/generated-content) while keeping workload and cost transparent.
