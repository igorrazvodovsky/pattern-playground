# Colour

The goal is to find a good enough tradeoff between simplicity, the needs of different components, and potential branding requirements.
The focus is on functional needs: colour that supports guiding users, draws attention to important information, and makes content more accessible.

> Substrate: [explore the colour swatches, hue slider, and token tables](/storybook/?path=/docs/foundations-material-color--docs) in Storybook.

## Approach

- Colours are defined using OKLCH notation and using Oklab colour space.
- All palettes use the same scale for relative saturation and lightness.
- Palettes (neutral, primary, info, warning, etc.) are defined by their hues.
- The lightness of the colour corresponds to its use.
- Chroma for each lightness level are picked to ensure maximum visual consistency between palettes.
- Foreground colours are calculated based on background colour to ensure the necessary contrast.

## Layering

Neutral palette colours are layered to create depth and show how different parts of the UI relate to each other.
