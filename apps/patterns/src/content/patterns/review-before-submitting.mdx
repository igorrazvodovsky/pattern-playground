Before the final action, the system shows every answer the actor has given on one screen, grouped as they were asked. Each answer has an Edit link that goes to the place it was entered and returns the actor to the review afterwards. The button that commits names what happens ("Create account", "Deploy cluster", "Place order") rather than "Submit" or "Finish".

The closing step of a [wizard](/patterns/wizard) is the most common place for it, but a long single-page form can end with one too.
