* * *

**When the work lives only on the provider's server, the actor has it only while the provider allows: a closed account, a lost connection or a service that shuts down takes it away. A copy on the device does not settle this on its own. A file in a format only one program can read, or data held in browser storage tied to one website, cannot be taken anywhere else.**

Therefore,

**Keep the complete work on the actor's device as the primary copy, usable without a network or an account, in a form another program can read. Show the actor where the work is and let them take it elsewhere. Where there is a server, use it to sync and back up the work, not as its home.**
