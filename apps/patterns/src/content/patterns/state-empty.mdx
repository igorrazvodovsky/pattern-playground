* * *

**An empty surface gives no reason for being empty and no way forward. On first use it is the first thing the actor sees of the place they will work, and a blank one tells them nothing about what goes there.**

Hulick describes three ways to fill an empty state: material that teaches, such as instructions or a preview of a full space; a call to action that creates the missing content; and personality, such as an illustration or a light line of copy, as long as it does not displace the other two. An empty state that only decorates, such as an illustration with no next step, is a dead end.

Therefore,

**Make every empty surface say why it is empty and what would put something in it, and offer the one action that does so where the actor can take it. On first use, add enough explanation or sample content to show what the surface is for.**
