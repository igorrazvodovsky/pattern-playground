* * *

**A blank container gives the actor no way to tell whether the surface is newly created, filtered down to zero matches, restricted by permissions, or broken. On first use it is the first view of the place where the actor will work, and an unexplained gap leaves them staring at chrome with no sign of what belongs there or how to begin.**

An empty surface occurs for several distinct reasons across the life of an artifact, and each reason calls for a different response. On first use, the container is empty because the actor has not put anything in it yet; the surface has to teach what kind of work lives there and offer the direct action, template, or sample item that starts it. During everyday work, a surface becomes empty because a search or filter excluded every item, because the actor finished everything in a queue, or because they do not have access to the contents; there, teaching the feature again is noise, and the actor instead needs to know what hid the content and how to widen the query, request access, or move on.

Across all of these cases, an empty state has two jobs: stating plainly why nothing is shown, and placing the action that resolves the gap—creating the first item, clearing the active filter, or requesting permission—right inside the empty region so the actor does not have to hunt through surrounding toolbars. Visual personality or illustration can soften a blank screen or mark a cleared queue, but an empty state that only decorates without offering a way forward is a dead end.

Therefore,

**Make every empty surface state clearly why it is empty and what would put content into it, and place the primary action that resolves the gap directly inside the empty region. On first use, show what the surface is for and offer a way to create a first item, start from a template, or explore a starter example; on filtered, cleared, or restricted views, name the condition that left the view empty and offer the action to reset or resolve it.**

Do not show first-run promotional copy or introductory tutorials when a view is empty only because a filter matched nothing or an error occurred. Keep illustrations and decorative copy subordinate to the explanation and the call to action.

## Research on this pattern

- *First-run empty states as instructional surfaces*: reviewing empty states across production applications, [Hulick](https://www.useronboard.com/onboarding-ux-patterns/empty-states/) identifies three elements that make a first-use blank slate functional: educational context showing what the populated surface will do, a direct prompt to create or load content, and personality that stays subordinate to both.
