situation:
  initiating: >-
    the surface has nothing to show and the reason matters — the actor hasn't made anything
    yet, or their filter excluded everything, or they don't have access; an empty region says
    none of that on its own and reads as broken
  resulting:
    - >-
      the gap explains itself and points somewhere: the actor knows why there is nothing here
      and what would put something here
    - >-
      first-run emptiness is the largest uninterrupted surface the product ever gets, and it
      arrives in front of an actor who has committed nothing yet
    - >-
      the empty states multiply — every filter, every permission boundary, every new
      container has one — and the ones nobody wrote say nothing
relationships:
  hosts:
    - to: onboarding
      note: the empty state is often the canvas onboarding builds on
  enacts:
    - to: learnability
      note: the educational and guiding variants teach the system from inside the gap they announce

import { Icon } from '@iconify/react';


Tells actors that there’s no content to display. Provides explanation and guidance to help actor progress. Typically surfaces a <ComponentRef id="components-button--docs">Button</ComponentRef> as the primary call to action.

## Variants

### Blank slate

Basic empty state for first use experience.

<Demo>
  <div className="empty-state flow">
    <iconify-icon style={{fontSize: "3rem"}} icon="ph:bell"></iconify-icon>
    <h3>Nothing to report</h3>
    <p>We’ll keep you updated on any new developments.</p>
  </div>
</Demo>

## Informational

Provides information about what to do next.

<Demo label="Access denied empty state">
  <div className="empty-state flow">
    <h3>You don’t have access to this content</h3>
    <p>Make sure the content exists. If it does, ask an admin for a permission to see the content.</p>
    <button className="button" is="pp-button">Request access</button>
  </div>
</Demo>

A contextual onboarding flow to gain deeper understanding of the system.
