* * *

**What the actor has open, in view or selected tells the artifact most of what a request is about. A request read without it means little, and a request read against everything on screen picks up what the actor never meant to include. Context nobody named is also context the actor cannot see being used.**

Therefore,

**Read each request against where the actor is when they make it: the open item, what is in view, the selection and the cursor, narrowest first. Show what was taken in, and let the actor remove it, widen it, or pin it so it stays.**
