* * *

**When actors work through a collection item by item, no single view can be both compact enough to scan and rich enough to verify. Showing every item at full fidelity buries the list and makes comparison impossible; opening one item at a time breaks orientation and forces repeated roundtrips back and forth—pogo-sticking—just to answer a scanning question.**

### Forces

- *Compactness vs. answering in place*: every attribute omitted from the overview forces a roundtrip into an item to answer a scanning question; every attribute added slows down scanning the list.
- *Preference vs. performance*: actors consistently prefer having an overview even where it does not improve measured task speed (Hornbæk et al., 2002; Cockburn et al., 2009), so an overview claims screen space its performance benefit alone may not justify.

Where the detail opens is a layout decision inside the pattern, not the pattern itself. Unlike [hub and spoke](/patterns/hub-and-spoke), which routes between independent sections with no shared model, overview and detail presents one collection at two granularities. Unlike a minimap in [pan and zoom](/patterns/pan-and-zoom), it compresses discrete items rather than coupling two viewports over a continuous plane. Compared with [semantic zoom](/patterns/semantic-zoom), which spends time swapping scales across the whole population, split overview and detail spends screen space to show both levels at once, and combining the two often outperforms either alone.

The pattern carries two recurring costs. First, the overview's fixed subset of attributes sets a ceiling on what actors can compare, sort, and filter without opening items—a ceiling most interfaces still withhold from actor control (Min et al., 2025 found only 8.33% of surveyed sites offered content customisation). Second, simultaneous split panes spend horizontal width: below roughly 40rem, side-by-side views cramp both halves and must give way to an in-place or single-column layout.

Therefore,

**Give the collection two linked views: an overview rendering every item in the same compressed form, and a detail view rendering the focused item in full. Carry the current item between them so choosing an item in the overview updates the detail and returning to the overview keeps the actor's place, and adapt the layout to available width—falling back from side-by-side panes to an in-place expansion, popover, or separate page below roughly 40rem.**

Watch for redundant fields when the detail opens in place directly beneath the overview row: what gives orientation across separated panes reads as clutter once the two touch.

## In practice

### Content

Which *attributes* live in which view—overview only, detail only, or both—is the consequential choice, and interactive controls count as attributes too (a save button can live in both views or only one). The overview's subset fixes what the actor can compare, sort, and filter by; held open to the actor rather than fixed per view, this dimension is [attribute visibility](/patterns/attribute-visibility) applied twice over. Overview attributes are often *abstractions* of their detail counterparts: visual (`badge` for status), semantic (a generated title standing for a conversation), or computational (a calendar of cheapest prices). When the abstraction is computed or generated, the overview stops being a subset of the detail view and becomes a summary in its own right.

<Demo spec="fluid-attributes" surface="gray" label="The content dimension held open: surface attributes from the detail view into the overview, then operate on them." expandable />

### Composition

The pair can scale and nest in several ways: several overviews sharing one detail (a list and a map over the same locations), one overview opening several details, recursion—where the pattern nests inside detail views (such as suggested articles inside an article)—and intermediate detail tiers that soften the jump (a tooltip card leading to a full profile). The composition can also invert: a thesaurus entry opens on the detail view with overviews of synonyms nested inside it, detail first and overview second.

<Demo spec="nested-pairs" surface="gray" label="The recursive case: a product's detail carries an overview of its components, whose detail carries overviews of its sub-assemblies and its materials. Three collections, one shape, repeated — and each rung keeps its own current item, so a path stays open through several levels at once." expandable />

### Layout

Simultaneous split panes make switching a glance rather than a navigation, but below roughly 40rem they have nowhere to go and should fall back to opening in place inside the row picked from. Because opening in place puts the detail directly beneath attributes the overview is still showing, the in-place detail should show the content dimension minus the overview's own set rather than repeating the same readout. Place the layout switcher on the detail view rather than in an external toolbar so the layout is re-chosen from the thing being laid out.
