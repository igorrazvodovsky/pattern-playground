* * *

**When updates are scattered across the people, organisations, projects, actors either check each source in turn or stop checking and miss changes.**

The design choice is less about how items are collected—whether by [following](/concepts/following), shared workspace, or explicit rules—than about order and stopping. Reverse-chronological order is predictable and lets the actor see when they have reached what they already saw; algorithmic ranking surfaces signal under high volume, but hides what was left out. Because a feed has no natural end, noisy sources and uniform visual weight drown out consequential updates and make the stream hard to put down.

Therefore,

**Collect non-interrupting updates from the sources into one scannable stream, with each item carrying its source, content, timestamp, and actions. Default to reverse-chronological order, mark where the actor last caught up or when nothing has changed, and bound the stream so it can be checked and left.**

Keep urgent, time-critical events in [notification](/patterns/notification) so the actor is not forced to poll the feed as an inbox, and keep infinite scroll out of work feeds where catching up is the point.
