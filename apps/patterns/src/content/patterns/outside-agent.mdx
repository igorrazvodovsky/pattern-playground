* * *

**An agent the actor brings usually has to work through surfaces built for a person. It reads screens and guesses at controls. It is often given the actor's own credentials, so it can do anything the actor can, and nothing records which acts were the agent's. The artifact cannot tell the actor from the agent, and the actor cannot limit the agent without limiting themselves.**

Therefore,

**Give the actor's agent its own way in: the artifact's objects and actions described for a program to read, reached under a grant the actor issues. Let the grant name which workspaces and which actions it covers, and let the actor revoke it. Record the agent's acts as its own, and hold the acts the actor would want to confirm until they do.**
