* * *

**When the artifact cannot meet a need, someone usually can, but the actor often cannot tell who: who owns the item, who administers the workspace, who can review a decision. An actor who does not know whom to ask, or how to reach them, is left with the problem.**

Therefore,

**At the point where the artifact falls short, name who can act on the need and let the actor ask them from there. Address the request by the need, to the person, and send it with the item involved and what the actor was trying to do.**
