* * *

**While an agent works through a run of several steps, the actor can see the step under way and perhaps the ones already done, but not what is left, so they cannot tell how far the run has got or what it will do next. When the agent changes its plan along the way, nothing shows it.**

Therefore,

**Keep the run's steps in view in the agent's reply, split into what is left and what is done, with the step under way marked. The agent keeps the list current, adding steps as it finds it needs them, and the conversation stays open while it works.**
