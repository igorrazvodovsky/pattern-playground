Something arrives, or is ready to send, at the wrong moment. Acting on it now breaks the actor's focus, dismissing it loses it, and leaving it in place relies on the actor remembering to come back. Many people use their inbox or notification list as a reminder system for this reason, and the list fills with items they are not ready to act on.

Deferral lets the actor name a later time. The item leaves view until then and comes back in the place it came from, marked as having been deferred. Presets keyed to the actor's working day make the choice fast, and a list of what is deferred keeps the items findable before they return. Scheduled send applies the same move to the recipient: the message is written now and delivered at a time that suits them.

## Where it is practised

- Email clients that snooze a message until a chosen time and then return it to the top of the inbox.
- Chat tools that remind the actor about a message later, and that schedule a message to send at a set time.
- Task tools that move a task's start date forward and hide it until then.
- Phones that snooze an alarm or a calendar alert by a fixed interval.
