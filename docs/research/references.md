# References index

A one-line map of the sources the project draws on. What the project takes from
each source is written once, in its overlay note in the vault at
`PARA/Projects/pattern playground/sources/<name>.md`; each overlay also names
where the source text lives. Dated retrieval notes that gate specific decisions
live at `PARA/Projects/pattern playground/research/<slug>/`.

## Design theory

- [`docs/language/semilattice.md`](../language/semilattice.md) — the project's own essay on Alexander's semilattice argument (1965): no single classification tree captures the pattern space.
- `Design patterns.md` — Alexander, *A Pattern Language* and *The Nature of Order*: patterns as generative, structure-preserving moves.
- `The organization of interaction design pattern languages.md` — Hübscher et al. (2011): organising a pattern language by the design process.
- `Concept design.md` — Jackson, *The Essence of Software*: software as a network of purpose-defined concepts.
- `Making software meaningful.md` — Meng et al. (arXiv:2606.11051, 2026): individuals, values, actions, and facts as a shared vocabulary.
- `Relational design.md` — a relational design framework for human, AI, and software actors.
- `Understanding Computers and Cognition.md` — Winograd & Flores: language, action, and breakdowns.
- Research run: `research/hci-pattern-languages/2026-04-28.md` — the HCI pattern-language literature behind [`pattern-definition.md`](../language/pattern-definition.md).
- Research run: `research/pattern-language-formats/2026-04-30-report.md` — formats for generative pattern languages, from narrative templates to typed graphs.

## AI and interaction

- `Collaboration through agency.md` — Zhang et al. (CSCW 2025): a scoping review of human-AI co-creation through agency.
- `Bridging Gulfs.md` — Park et al. (CHI 2026): semantic guidance for UI generation. `semantic-guidance-for-ui-generation.md` is the project's working distillation of it.
- `DIRA.md` — Bergström & Hornbæk: the DIRA model of the user interface.
- Research run: `research/ai-pattern-catalogues/2026-08-07-practice.md` — a triage of six public AI UX pattern libraries.

## Prose and rhetoric

- `rhetoric-of-hyperlink.md` — Rao (2009): the hyperlink as rhetorical technology; the primary citation for the `Prose` foundation's link rhetoric.

## Harness engineering

- `openAI-harness.md` — Lopopolo (OpenAI, 2026): harness engineering, "map, not manual".
- `harness-eng.md` — He et al. (2026): the harness layer as control, agency, and runtime.
