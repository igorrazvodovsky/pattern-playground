# Pattern and form languages

The project works with two languages:

- The *pattern language* is the connected structure of *moves* — rules for how
  people interact with form, organised so an actor can generate and sequence
  design decisions. It is defined in [design-theory.md](./design-theory.md) and
  [pattern-definition.md](./pattern-definition.md).
- The *form language* is the connected structure of *mechanisms* — the vocabulary
  of components, the rules for composing them, and the levels of scale by which
  small parts assemble into larger ones. In this repository it is
  `packages/components/`.

## Form language and `mechanism`

`mechanism` is the unit-level role — a component, primitive, control, or visual
or behavioural element (see [vision.md](./vision.md)). *Form language* is the
collective term, the layer with its grammar, as *pattern language* is the
collective for *move*.

In [design-theory.md](./design-theory.md)'s aspirational endpoint — Alexander's
structural properties, at once form and effect — the two languages fuse; the
objects above are the early cases.
