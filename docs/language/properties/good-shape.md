# Good shape

Property 6 of [the fifteen](index.md). Placeholder — no substrate translation

*As a move* — operand: a weakly formed shape. Operation: intensify it by
strengthening the centres inside it, so it becomes built from simple, regular,
centre-forming figures. Recursive: the parts of a good shape are themselves
good shapes.

*As a symptom* — parts read as simple, definite figures rather than accidental
outlines.
