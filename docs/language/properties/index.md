# The fifteen properties

Fifteen recurring structural features that Christopher Alexander identified in
living structure, each of which is also a transformation that produces it. This
folder holds one file per property. Most are placeholders: a file fills out only
when its move has been worked in a real substrate.

## How to read them

Each property runs in two directions, and the direction matters:

- *As a move*, while working: an operation an actor applies to what already
  exists. Each file states the operand (what the move acts on) and the
  operation (what it does).
- *As a symptom*, while reviewing: what its presence or absence in existing
  structure tells you, and where to look next.

They are not a checklist for producing new work from nothing. And they do not
choose the next move: patterns select *what* to do; the properties shape *how*
the result is formed.

## Substrates

The method applies at several scales of software development — examine what is
lively in the medium and ask how centres strengthen each other there. Whether
the roster of fifteen transfers is an open question per substrate: Alexander
derived a different set (eleven) for colour, and the strongest prior
translation to a non-spatial medium re-derived twenty-four properties for
human activity rather than reusing the fifteen (research gate:
[research/fifteen-properties/2026-08-07.md](../../../research/fifteen-properties/2026-08-07.md)).
The fifteen are the starting roster, not a commitment; if a substrate's
translations start fighting their names, the vocabulary forks per substrate —
parallel sets, linked — rather than forcing every substrate through the
fifteen.

Substrates in play so far:

- *Interface composition* — screens, flows, conversations. Centres here are
  meaning-carrying differentiations in behaviour and attention; see
  [design-theory.md](../design-theory.md).
- *The repository* — code and its strata of description.
- *The pattern language itself*, as an artifact.

Translations accrete per property as separate documents, linked from the
property's file. A translation is complete only when it states both presence
and absence conditions — what the substrate looks like with the property and
what it looks like without it. Without the absence half, every structure
instantiates the property and the term stops discriminating.

## Status

Untested vocabulary at suggestion grade — the same epistemic stance as the
[relationship vocabulary](../relationship-vocabulary.md). Docs layer only: no
site pages, roles, or edges until translations carry evidence. The project's
qualities are a different kind of thing — experiential dimensions, noun-only;
[design-theory.md](../design-theory.md) draws the distinction.

## The properties

1. [Levels of scale](levels-of-scale.md) — differentiate a zone into connected
   sizes, no jump too large
2. [Strong centres](strong-centres.md) — make a weak centre emphatic; the move
   the other fourteen serve
3. [Boundaries](boundaries.md) — thicken an edge into a zone that separates and
   connects at once
4. [Alternating repetition](alternating-repetition.md) — repeat a unit and give
   the space between repeats its own structure
5. [Positive space](positive-space.md) — shape the space between centres until
   it is a centre itself
6. [Good shape](good-shape.md) — intensify a weak shape by strengthening the
   centres inside it
7. [Local symmetries](local-symmetries.md) — give a new part its own small
   axis, not one global one
8. [Deep interlock and ambiguity](deep-interlock-and-ambiguity.md) — weave two
   adjacent parts into each other at their boundary
9. [Contrast](contrast.md) — sharpen the distinction between two kinds of
   centre so each defines the other
10. [Gradients](gradients.md) — make a quality vary systematically instead of
    uniformly or abruptly
11. [Roughness](roughness.md) — accept local irregularity where it buys a
    better fit in the large
12. [Echoes](echoes.md) — propagate the character of existing centres to new
    ones
13. [The void](the-void.md) — clear an over-differentiated region back to calm
    emptiness
14. [Simplicity and inner calm](simplicity-and-inner-calm.md) — remove
    structure that supports nothing, everywhere
15. [Not-separateness](not-separateness.md) — knit a thing into its context by
    mutual embedding
