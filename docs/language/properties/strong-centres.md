# Strong centres

Property 2 of [the fifteen](index.md). Placeholder — no substrate translation
yet.

*As a move* — operand: any weak centre. Operation: make it emphatic — more
differentiated, better defined, more integrated with what surrounds it. This is
the move the other fourteen serve: each of them is, one way or another, a way
of strengthening centres.

*As a symptom* — every part reads as a centre in its own right, with a field of
support around it, rather than as leftover material between the important
parts.
