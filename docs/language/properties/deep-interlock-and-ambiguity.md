# Deep interlock and ambiguity

Property 8 of [the fifteen](index.md). Placeholder — no substrate translation

into each other so that a zone belongs to both — each hooked into the other
rather than merely adjacent. Usually a late move, applied to structure that
already exists.

*As a symptom* — parts connect through zones of shared ownership; you cannot
always say where one ends and the other begins, and the ambiguity is doing
work.
