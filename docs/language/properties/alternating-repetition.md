# Alternating repetition

Property 4 of [the fifteen](index.md). Placeholder — no substrate translation
yet.

*As a move* — operand: an undifferentiated field, or a repetition whose gaps
are dead. Operation: repeat a unit and, at the same time, give the space
between the repeats its own interlocking structure — a second system living
between the first.

*As a symptom* — repetition where the gaps are as shaped as the units, rather
than mechanical duplication with leftover space between.
