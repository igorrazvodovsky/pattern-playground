# The thirteen heads, split into situation and judgement

The test applied to each `presupposes` clause: a *situation* is what is the case about the product or its site, and stays as prose the walker reads. A *judgement* is something the designer decides, produces an artefact (a list, a ranking, a grading, a picture) that a later step acts on, and is a step. Alexander's head steps are all of the second kind: "formulate the activities as generic centres" (kitchen 1), the fourteen activities ranked (Herman Miller), the twelve stations written before design (Mary Rose), the interview written up as a communal language (closed-eyes). None of them needs the eye; all of them produce a written thing. The vault's own reading (*the list alone*: "before the flows, the wireframes, the components: a plain-language list of the things this product is made of") already recommends the import that the spec's boundary rule forbids.

Two findings that fall out before the per-sequence list. Four files (attention and tempo, making sense at scale, memory and continuity, working together) put the rate the sequence is walked at and its governing principle into `presupposes`, which the spec assigns to the lead; those clauses move regardless of the split. And in three sequences the presupposed judgement is re-taken as step one (governing autonomy, calibrating reliance, attention and tempo), so the head and the first step are the same decision twice; in one of them the double-counting hides a backtrack.

## The list

**Structuring the space.** Situation: the product's posture is tool-shaped, a spatial surface is the primary frame. Judgement: the objects, activities and labels are named. That is the list alone at product grain, the kitchen's step one, and the partition step already acts on it ("boundaries follow how attention and responsibility actually divide" needs the activities in hand). Suggest a head step, *Name the places and the things* — one line each, some of them questions, per the vault note — with the information-architecture foundation as its rule and the object list as its product. This is the one head that discharges three other sequences' presuppositions at once (composing views, acting on material, learning all presuppose the naming).

**Composing views.** Situation throughout, provided the head above exists: the space is structured (a hand-off from structuring the space) and the collection is a named thing (the object list at collection grain). The one residue is the entity's attribute set, which the item-view step presumes known; fold "objects with their attributes" into structuring the space's list rather than opening a second head here. Suggest: keep as prose, rewritten as a hand-off, and drop "discharged upstream".

**Acting on material.** Situation: material is framed and a surface exists (hand-off). Judgement: the operations are named — what can be created, changed and removed, and what each is called. The routing choice is "taken per kind of change" and cannot be taken until the kinds are enumerated; this is the verb list to go with the noun list, and it is Herman Miller's step one (the processes listed before anything is placed). Suggest a head step, *Name the operations on each thing*, information-architecture as rule, product the verb inventory the routing walks over.

**The encounter.** Situation: dialogue is this surface's primary mode (the posture; see the cross-cutting note). Judgement: the agent's capabilities are inventoried — what it can act on, only retrieve, only discuss. Step one, *Draw the capability boundary*, is the statement of that inventory to the actor; the inventory is its material. Suggest a head step, *Inventory what the agent can act on, retrieve, and discuss*, invoking capability-and-scope with an aspect ("its inventory") or standing weak, product the capability list. Step one then acts on a thing rather than on an assumption.

**Delegating work.** Situation: grounding material exists where the actor can name it. Judgement: work has been judged worth mediating. This is *Ranking your work* almost verbatim — the activities ranked by which get an intermediary — and it is the judgement the delegation foundation's set-up station names. The clause "permissions and the test drive are discharged upstream or not at all" is wrong as written: permissions is governing autonomy's grant step and the test drive is the first run of a delegated capability, which learning's arrival region already claims; both are hand-offs, not gaps. Suggest a head step, *Rank the work by what to hand over*, weak, product the ranked list; rewrite the rest as hand-offs.

**Governing autonomy.** Situation: delegation is underway (hand-off). Judgement: the risks have been judged — cheap to redo, runaway cost, uncallable effects. The gate's first step, action-consequences, "acts on the risk judgement the head presupposes, encoding it per act"; the same judgement twice. And the grant's first step draws the edges by "scope, spend, time, and risk class", which needs the grading the gate produces one region later. That is a backtrack the two walks did not record. Suggest moving the action-consequences grading to the head as step one, before bounded-autonomy; the gate's version becomes an aspect re-invocation ("its checkpoint sizing") or goes.

**Assistance escalation.** Situation, trimmed: an actor is doing work the system can see, and some evidence of typical use exists at whatever grade. The clause "the judgement itself is settled upstream" is not so: the judgement of what fills each field is step one (good-defaults, "which fields get one is the decision"), and the evidence is later built by the climb's own weak step. Suggest: keep as prose, cut the judgement clause, and let the sequence own both ends of its evidence.

**Calibrating reliance.** Situation: an AI surface is in use and its output will be acted on. Judgement: the stakes of acting on wrong output have been graded per surface. Every step in the evidence cluster reads "the presupposed stakes decide which marks this surface carries"; the grading is the operand of the whole sequence and nothing produces it. Suggest a head step, *Grade the stakes of acting on wrong output, per surface*, action-consequences as rule with an aspect ("its AI-output face") — a fourth invocation of that shared station, which is itself a finding about the station.

**Learning.** Situation: none stated; the whole head is judgement. "What competent use looks like — which objects matter, what fluent work looks like, where the common ground lies" is the Mary Rose word-picture: an ordered account of what the actor will undergo, written before anything is designed. Onboarding's step one takes its decision "against the presupposed picture", so the picture is material. Suggest a head step, *Write the picture of competent use*, weak, product the word-picture, including what the system will do unasked where that applies.

**Making sense at scale.** Situation, pure: material has outgrown any single framing and the question is not yet a query. There is nothing to discharge, so "discharged nowhere" misdescribes it. The three-rates and re-entry clauses move to the lead. Suggest: keep, and delete the discharge language.

**Memory and continuity.** Split within the clause. Situation: the legal and consent regime the product operates under — what it may not keep. Judgement: what is kept, for how long, and who reads it — a retention policy the selective-memory step and the forgetting cluster both spend. Alexander's analogue is *Program budgeting* and the eighty-twenty rule: an allocation fixed on day one and held against the red pencil. Suggest a head step, *Set the retention budget*, weak, product the policy, with the regime left as situation prose.

**Attention and tempo.** Situation: the other sequences generate events. Judgement: "what may interrupt whom, and when — priority and workload settled before any notification is designed". Step one, *Grade every claim on the actor*, is that settlement; the presupposition double-counts it. What step one actually lacks is its inventory: "every event class the other walks hand over" is enumerated nowhere. Suggest deleting the judgement clause, adding a head step *List the events that can claim the actor* (weak; the product is the event-class list the grading step walks), and moving the rates, the revocation failure and the hidden-cost note to the lead.

**Working together.** Situation, mostly: the people and their real relationships — who works with whom, in what roles — exist independently of the product, and the mapping of them is research rather than design (the closed-eyes interview's territory, which Alexander does write as a sequence, but one for the researcher). The product-side judgements the clause folds in — which tiers exist, what watching is permitted — are already the grant region's two steps. Suggest: keep as prose, trimmed to the relationships being known, with rates and the desertion principle moved to the lead.

## Cross-cutting: the posture

Structuring the space, the encounter, learning's channel and the encounter's endings cluster all presuppose a posture — tool-shaped, conversation-first, or delegated — and the map records the posture choice as tree-shaped and homeless. Under the split it is a situation for every sequence and a judgement for the product, taken once, upstream of all thirteen. That makes it the one head that belongs outside any sequence: a single position on the sequence listing or the map's entry-point paragraph, *Settle the posture*, owning the tree. It gives the open question a home without a grounding sequence.

## Tally and what it would take

Of thirteen heads, four are situation and stay (composing views, assistance escalation, making sense at scale, working together, each trimmed). Nine hold a judgement; three of those are already steps in disguise (governing autonomy — a reorder; calibrating reliance and attention and tempo — the grading exists, the inventory does not), and six want a new head step (structuring the space, acting on material, the encounter, delegating work, learning, memory and continuity). Every new head produces a written artefact — a list, a ranking, an inventory, a picture, a budget — which is what makes it artifact-register rather than process-register; the perceivability objection does not reach any of them.

Spec changes implied: allow the head step's `rule` to be a foundation, or leave it weak, at that one slot; consider a `produces` field on a step, since Alexander's heads are defined by their output rather than their rule; move rate and principle prose from `presupposes` to the lead in the four non-conforming files; and reword the remaining presuppositions as hand-offs where another sequence's head discharges them, so "discharged nowhere" survives only where it is true (the posture, and the research inputs to working together and assistance).
