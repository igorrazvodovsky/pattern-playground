## What actors do with the marks

- Pew Research (July 2025; ~900 U.S. adults with a browsing tracker, 68,879 Google queries, 12,593 with an AI summary): when an AI summary appears, clicks on any result fall from 15% to 8% of visits; clicks on a link *inside* the summary happen on 1% of visits. [pewresearch.org](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)
- This is the population-scale counterpart of Kim et al.'s 61% never-clickers and Narayanan Venkit's 2-hovered-1-clicked: the mark is seen; the source is not visited.

## Forms in use

Four recurring forms, roughly ordered by how close the pointer sits to the evidence.

1. *Span at the claim.* The citation resolves to the exact passage, highlighted in the source, often with the quoted text previewed on hover. NotebookLM (numbered markers; hover previews the quoted text, click opens the source scrolled to the highlighted passage). Anthropic's Citations API makes this the unit of the contract: each claim carries `cited_text` with `start_char_index`/`end_char_index` into a named document, chunked at sentence granularity, so the interface can render the span rather than the document. [claude.com/blog](https://claude.com/blog/introducing-citations-api), [platform.claude.com docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/citations). Shape of AI lists the same form as "inline highlights" (Adobe Acrobat) and "direct quotations" (Granola).
2. *Publisher chip with popover.* Perplexity and ChatGPT: a rounded domain chip at the end of the claim ("northjersey +3"); click opens an anchored popover with title, snippet, favicon, and 1/N paging when several sources back one claim. The "+N" collapses multiplicity without cluttering prose. [aiuxplayground.com teardown](https://aiuxplayground.com/teardowns/perplexity/citations/)
3. *Source strip or sidebar.* A favicon stack in the answer bar ("10 sources") opening a right panel that lists every source as a card while the answer stays visible; Perplexity also has a Links tab as a peer of Answer/Images. The bird's-eye view of the evidence base, complementary to the inline marks.
4. *Bare URL list.* A list of full links after the answer, no anchoring to claims (Shape of AI: Copy.ai). Cheapest to produce; gives the actor nothing to follow *from*.

Two supporting tools seen in Perplexity: select a passage and "Check sources" to re-verify that span without re-running the query; and a "Wrong sources" chip in the negative-feedback modal, separating citation failures from answer failures.

## Cautions the catalogues already record

- Shape of AI: span-level forms "can prematurely converge on a single source"; multi-source references "are less reliable as the source material is more likely to be external and unverified"; "empty states maintain trust, filler content erodes it" when a citation cannot be produced. [shapeof.ai](https://www.shapeof.ai/patterns/citations)
- Narayanan Venkit's participants found numeric markers confusing and preferred hover interactions; they also noticed more sources displayed than cited, which reads as padding.

## Named failures in the wild

- Legal: *Mata v. Avianca* (S.D.N.Y. 2023) — fabricated case citations filed in court; the canonical example of a citation accepted because it looked like one. Magesh et al. 2024 (literature note) found the dedicated legal research tools that followed still misground 17–33% of the time.
- Scholarly publishing: a line of 2026 preprints on hallucinated references surviving peer review at top venues (Russinovich & Siva Kumar's *Phantom References*; Sakai & Kamigaito's *HalluCitation Matters*; Ansari's NeurIPS 2025 failure taxonomy) — the mark-without-check failure reproduced inside the community that invented citation.

## Takeaways for the pattern page

- Lead with the span form as the solution's strongest shape; treat chips, strips, and bare lists as weaker variants along a cost-of-following axis.
- Carry the 1% / 61% numbers into the forces: the move's verification benefit is real but reaches few actors; its trust effect reaches all of them.
- The "Check sources" and "Wrong sources" affordances are small moves worth naming under variants — they are how a product admits that its citations can be wrong.
