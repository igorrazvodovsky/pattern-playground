## What this adds

The 2026-07-16 note skipped lineage on the grounds that the run was a
name-check rather than a corpus build, and that judgment stands: nothing here
touches the one-node carving question the note settled. What it does supply is
the branch the note deferred — "the coordinated-views deepening (Roberts 2007)
wants formal lineage later".

The ancestors say coordinated views descend from brushing, not from
master-detail. *Brushing Scatterplots* is the shared root, and *Snap-Together
Visualization* is where the coordination becomes something a user constructs.
That supports routing coordinated views away from the overview-detail node
rather than under it.

Two descendants are directly useful to the corpus. *Composition and
Configuration Patterns in Multiple-View Visualizations* is a pattern-shaped
analysis of the same territory and should be read before the coordinated-views
entry is written. *Keeping Multiple Views Consistent* is about the constraint
problem the note's writable-views section raises — it names validations and
exceptions as the mechanism, which is a more specific answer than the note's
"which edits are legal through which representation".
