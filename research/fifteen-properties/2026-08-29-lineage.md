## What the lineage shows

The run's sources form two lines that read Alexander's later work without
reading each other. The software line (Coplien & Zhao) descends from *The
Timeless Way* and the Gang of Four, and treats the properties as an
architectural-symmetry question. The urban-morphology line (Jiang's *Wholeness
as a Hierarchical Graph* and *Beautimeter*) descends from spatial-statistics
work on heterogeneity, and treats them as a measurable property of built form.
Nothing is shared between the two.

This bears on how the corpus should use the fifteen properties. The note reads
them as design vocabulary; the two literatures that have formalised them do so
for different objects — code structure on one side, spatial distributions on the
other — and neither has produced a reading for interaction design. That is
absence of prior art rather than a gap in this run.
