## What the lineage shows

The corpus splits cleanly into two halves that do not meet. One is the AI
planning line — *Explainable Planning*, *RADAR-X*, the XAIP survey — whose
ancestry and descendants are all about explanation and model reconciliation.
The other is the LLM-agent oversight line, whose ancestors are reliance and
trust studies plus agent-engineering surveys. *MAPGEN* is the single work
either half shares with the other, and it is a mission-operations paper from
2004.

That is a finding the note can use. *To Trust or to Think* being a convergent
ancestor confirms the note's placement of plan preview in the cognitive-forcing
literature — the empirical papers reach back to it independently. What is
absent is any shared ancestor for the *preview* move itself: nothing in the
citation record joins the plan-preview papers to the pre-AI ancestry the note
names — "management by consent (Billings 1991), level 4–5 automation
(Sheridan), feedforward (Djajadiningrat 2002; Vermeulen 2013), previewed
transforms (Wrangler 2011), program navigation (Mayer 2015)". Those papers
were not fed to this run, and none of the six testable bibliographies cites
them. The thirty-year lineage the note claims is real as an argument, but it is
the note's own reconstruction rather than something this literature carries.
Establishing it means resolving those named papers and testing them directly.
