# Practice survey: making sense at scale — 2026-08-29

Query: what widely shared tools do about viewing accumulated material at levels, marking it, and keeping framings of it. Companion to the same-day literature note; practice evidence only.

Deliberately generic, per the run's framing: specialised domains (BI dashboards, qualitative-analysis suites, analyst tooling) were excluded as a bias risk — this corpus documents mechanisms most tools share. The survey draws on file managers, photo libraries, mail, e-readers, and mass-market generated-curation surfaces.

> *Provenance.* Gathered via web search 2026-08-29. Vendor pages (Apple support, Microsoft Learn, Google blogs and help) are flagged as such; several mechanisms (Gmail label mechanics, Kindle notebook flow) are documented here from third-party guides because the vendor prose describes the steps, not the model. No product was operated first-hand for this note.

## 1. Levels ship in mass-market tools, as few named strata

Apple Photos gives the library four views — Years, Months, Days, All Photos — and moves between them by pinch: "pinch open or closed on the day, month, or year to change views" ([Apple support](https://support.apple.com/guide/photos/browse-and-view-photos-pht56eafa987/6.0/mac/11.0), vendor). This is semantic zoom in a tool used by hundreds of millions: the levels are *named and few*, each a different representation (a year is a curated strip, not a smaller grid), and the gesture is continuous while the strata are discrete. Google Photos ties the same pinch to grid density and (on Android) to layout changes that re-rank what is prominent ([third-party](https://www.testingcatalog.com/how-to-change-grid-layout-on-google-photos-for-android/)). Finder's four views (icon, list, column, gallery) are the same decision at the file-manager scale — one collection, several representations, switched per situation, none mandatory before another. Nothing in these tools orders the lens forms; the actor picks per surface and moment, which matches the literature note's finding 2.

## 2. Marking is one gesture at the point of encounter, and vocabularies are flat

Gmail's labels are non-exclusive marks: "Gmail allows you to add as many labels as you want to an email without needing to make a copy", with all mail in one store and labels as overlays ([third-party](https://www.getinboxzero.com/blog/gmail-labels-vs-folders/); the folder contrast is the point — a folder is a partition, a label is a mark). Stars are the even lighter form: one tap, no vocabulary at all. Finder tags work the same way across the filesystem and integrate with search ([third-party](https://zushapp.com/blog/finder-tags-guide-organize-files-mac)). Kindle highlights are a select-and-mark inside the reading flow. Across all four, the mark costs one gesture, is applied *while doing something else* (reading, triaging), and the vocabulary is flat and user-grown — no tool asks for the scheme up front. Incremental formalisation is shipping practice, not a research position.

## 3. A kept framing is a saved query, and every mainstream shell has one

macOS Smart Folders "look like regular folders, but actually update dynamically according to the criteria you've set" ([third-party](https://www.macworld.com/article/229542/how-to-find-your-saved-smart-folders-in-the-finder.html)); Windows persists the same thing as a `.search-ms` file whose format is documented API surface ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/search/-search-savedsearchfileformat), vendor); clicking a Gmail label chip "shows you every conversation with that label, giving you a filtered view without actually moving the message". The generic floor of the curation region is exactly this: a framing (query + arrangement) kept as a place you can return to. The mass-market tools stop at the personal saved search; the named, purpose-shaped view handed to others is where generic practice thins out and the corpus's purpose-keyed-view / problem-curated-view territory begins.

## 4. Marks accumulate into material of their own

Amazon aggregates every Kindle highlight and note across books at read.amazon.com/notebook, organised by title and exportable ([third-party](https://tabletmonkeys.com/6-best-ways-to-export-kindle-highlights/); the export flow is vendor UI, the 10% publisher clipping cap is third-party-documented). The marks made during reading become a second corpus the actor returns to — the literature note's reflexive point (the walk re-enters over its own products) is visible in the humblest consumer reading tool.

## 5. Generated curation arrives unasked and unexplained

Apple Photos Memories builds "curated collections of your most meaningful photos and videos" from on-device analysis of dates, places, and people ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-by/guide/photos/pht96259d626/7.0/mac), vendor); Spotify's Discover Weekly generates a 30-track view of the listening record every Monday ([Spotify newsroom](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2019-05-02/five-ways-to-make-your-discover-weekly-playlists-even-more-personalized/), vendor). Both are system-made problem-curated-views over accumulated material: a framing the actor did not compose. Two facts about them matter for the sequence's seams. They *arrive* — pushed, on a schedule or at a trigger — which is an attention-and-tempo event, not a sensemaking step. And they carry little visible ground (why these photos, why these tracks), the reliance debt the corpus routes through calibrating reliance. NotebookLM is the text-side counterpart with the debt paid: summaries and answers are grounded only in uploaded sources, with "inline citations link[ing] directly back to the specific part of the source material" ([Google blog](https://blog.google/technology/ai/notebooklm-audio-overviews/), vendor; mechanics [third-party](https://learnprompting.org/blog/notebooklm-guide)) — the generated overview shipping *with* its citation marks, exactly the per-surface call into calibrating reliance the sequence should type.

## Convergence

The generic tools agree with the literature at every point the sequence needs: lenses are switched per surface and never ordered (§1); marking is a one-gesture rider on encounter, with flat vocabularies grown along the way (§2); the kept framing is a saved query, and its audience-directed form is the region's thin end (§3); marks become material (§4); generated curation is real, arriving as an attention event and owing reliance evidence (§5). No generic tool stages these as a workflow — what is walked in order is the *provision* of the capabilities, not their use, consistent with reading the territory as one sequence of design decisions over a looping activity.
