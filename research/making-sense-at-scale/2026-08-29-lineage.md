## What the lineage shows

The note expected Russell et al. 1993 to be "cited by essentially everything
above", and that holds: *The Cost Structure of Sensemaking* is under four of the
ten testable papers and is the densest ancestor in the set. Furnas 1986 does
not reach the threshold — the overview+detail review cites it, but the
sensemaking-tool papers do not.

The shape underneath is a surprise the note does not record. The recent
sensemaking tools — Sensecape, Fuse, Wigglite — do not descend mainly from
sensemaking theory. Their shared ancestors are personal-information-management
systems: *Data Mountain*, *Stuff I've Seen*, *SearchBar*, *Tabs.do*,
*Crystalline*, *Unakite*. The theory papers sit above them as citation, and the
working inheritance comes from tools for keeping track of material. The note
treats sensemaking and personal information management as two literatures that
meet at the design question; in the citation record the tools are one
literature that cites the other for framing.

*Mesh* (2020) under three of the newest papers is the clearest recent common
ancestor, and it is not in the note's reading. Neither is *Selenite* (2024),
the heaviest descendant on the recent side. Both belong in the reading queue
