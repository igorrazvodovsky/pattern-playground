## What the lineage shows

Two ancestries meet here rather than one. The 1982–2000 direct-manipulation
line is shared by every bibliography OpenAlex holds, including the two
bidirectional-programming papers, and it includes both of the run's own canon
reads — *Instrumental Interaction* is one of the convergent ancestors, and the
1982–83 Shneiderman pair sits under it. Hutchins, Hollan & Norman 1985, the
other full-text canon read, does not appear: the papers cite the 1983 and 1997
statements of direct manipulation instead. The note's own named candidate,
Shneiderman 1983, is confirmed — it is the single most convergent ancestor here. On top of
it sits a much newer line — *TaleBrush*, *PromptPaint*, object-oriented drawing
— which is about applying manipulable handles to generative output. The note
reads editing in place as one move; the citation graph says it is an old move
with a three-year-old extension, and the extension has its own small canon.

*How Notations Evolve* appearing as the only descendant is worth carrying: it
also shows up as an input in the [block-based-editing](../block-based-editing/)
lineage, which puts the two folders on the same edge of this literature.
