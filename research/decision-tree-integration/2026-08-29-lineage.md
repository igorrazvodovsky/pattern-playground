## What this can and cannot say

The result is thin, and the reason is worth recording. The note named five
expected ancestors — Alexander 1979, Beck & Cunningham 1987, MacLean et al.
1991, Fincher & Windsor 2000, Borchers 2001 — of which only two could be
resolved to records with bibliographies. The rest are books, a workshop
position paper, and OOPSLA-era material that OpenAlex does not index with
references.

The one thing the graph does say is about *Questions, Options, and Criteria*,
which is the note's option-space lineage. QOC shares no ancestor with either
patterns paper, and the only work reaching back to both QOC and Borchers is
Dearden & Finlay's review. The design-rationale line and the pattern-language
line meet in the HCI-patterns critique and nowhere earlier. If the plan wants
to treat decision trees as an option space over patterns, it is joining two
traditions that have met once, in a paper about whether HCI patterns work at
all — which is where the argument for the join would have to be made.
