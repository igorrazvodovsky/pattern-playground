## What the lineage shows

Three ancestries feed the kept set, and the note's synthesis draws on all three
without saying so. The groupware-systems line (concurrency control, WYSIWIS,
Quilt, Cognoter) supplies the editing-conflict material. The awareness line
(*Awareness and Coordination*, *Portholes*, the London Underground control-room
study) supplies workspace awareness. The language line (Clark's *Grounding in
Communication* and *Using Language*) enters only through *A Descriptive
Framework* and *Distance Matters* — it is the ancestry behind the note's
common-ground claims, and it is not shared with the collaborative-editing
papers at all.

Two things worth carrying forward. *The Problem with 'Awareness'* (2002) is a
high-citation descendant that argues against the construct the note leans on;
it is not in the note's reading and would be the obvious next read. And *Social
Coding in GitHub* is the descendant closest to the project's own territory —
asynchronous, artefact-mediated collaboration rather than real-time shared
workspaces — which suggests the pattern's evidence base could shift forward
twenty years without leaving the citation line.
