## What the lineage shows

The kept set has one root and two branches. The root is the 1989–2004
interruption-cost work — disruptiveness, temporal factors in mental work, the
diary study of task switching — which every branch reaches back to. One branch runs through instant messaging
and notification-deferral policy into Oasis and the intelligent-notification
line; the other runs through smartphone-habit studies into the
digital-wellbeing papers. The note treats these as one literature, and the
citation graph supports that: the deferral papers and the habit papers share the
same 1989–2004 ancestors even though they rarely cite each other directly.

The descendant side moves somewhere the note does not go. The heaviest
descendants are not interface papers — they are workload measurement (pupillary
response), clinical safety (interruptions in healthcare), and multitasking
theory. The design-side descendants (*My Phone and Me*, *"Silence Your
Phones"*) are smaller. If the pattern wants recent evidence, the interface
branch is the thinner one.
