## What the lineage shows

The Boxer result is the useful one. *Boxer: A Reconstructible Computational
Medium* sits under both the 1994 spatial-hypertext work and the 2015 substrate
work, which means the note's conclusion — that the paradigm names a design
material rather than an activity, and therefore belongs as a foundation — has a
citation-graph counterpart. The 2026-07-09 note guessed this ancestor by
inspection — "Kay's dynamic media and diSessa's Boxer sit behind Webstrates" —
and the graph confirms it, with a wider reach than the note claimed. The two literatures the note treats as one paradigm
do converge, on exactly the *computational medium* framing the note used to
name it.

*Chameleon* (2020) is the one descendant that bridges the hypertext side and the
substrate side. It is not in the note's reading and is the obvious next read if
the foundation entry wants a worked interactive-document instance.

*How Notations Evolve* (2026) is also an input in the
[editing-in-place](../editing-in-place/) lineage, where it appears as the only
descendant. Two folders reach the same recent paper from different directions.
