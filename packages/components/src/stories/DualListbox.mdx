# Dual listbox

A side-by-side pair of lists — source pool and selection set — with transfer controls between them.

The control realises the staged-commit end of [Selection](/patterns/selection)'s set-size spectrum: the actor moves items from the pool into the selection set and back, with both sides visible, before a surrounding [Form](/patterns/form) or dialog commits the assignment.

## When to reach for it

Compared with a [multiselect](../?path=/docs/components-combobox--docs) (combobox with checked items), which commits items one at a time and never shows the full selected set beside the remaining pool, the dual listbox keeps both sides on screen. It earns its larger surface when the assignment is bulk, the cost of getting it wrong is high, or the actor needs to cross-reference ("am I leaving the right items behind?"). For lighter selection, prefer the combobox.

## Behaviour

Each side is a [list](../?path=/docs/components-list--docs) in listbox mode (`type="listbox" multiselectable`), so selection follows the listbox keys: click or Space toggles an option, Shift+arrow moves to the next option and toggles it, and Ctrl/Cmd+A selects or clears all. Pressing Enter in either list, or a transfer button, moves the selected options to the other side, and a double-click moves a single option. Each transfer button stays disabled while its list has no selection.

Moved options are appended to the other list, and focus stays in the list they left, on the next remaining option, so the actor can keep working through the pool. Each move fires a `pp-transfer` event carrying the moved items and the direction. The control moves the existing option elements rather than copying them, so whatever owns the markup must not re-render the two lists while they are in use.
