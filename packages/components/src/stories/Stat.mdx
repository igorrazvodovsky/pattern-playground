# Stat

A reading taken in passing: a headline figure, a one-line verdict, and enough supporting detail to decide whether anything needs a closer look. The tile answers "is anything wrong?" without asking the reader to open anything.

<Story of={StatStories.Stat} />

## Composition

Almost everything is other catalogue blocks: the [card](../?path=/docs/components-card--docs) is the surface, its header carries the label and a trend [badge](../?path=/docs/components-badge--docs) (`badge--success`/`badge--danger` say whether the movement is welcome, which is not the same as which way it points). `.stat` itself only sets the figure scale and the vertical rhythm — value with an optional `.stat__unit`, `.stat__verdict`, then muted detail. `data-alert` on the card turns the figure itself into the alarm while the badge keeps reporting direction.

## The figure

A tile is the abbreviation case: the actor is scanning, not checking, so `£1.2m` is the right amount of [figure](../?path=/docs/foundations-figures--docs) and the exact total belongs down the line.
