# Activities

Sustained, motive-driven engagements that combine multiple [actions](../?path=/docs/actions-overview--docs) toward larger goals. Activities give actions their meaning — the same action (e.g. searching) serves different purposes depending on the activity it's part of.

## Intelligence

Patterns for working with AI capabilities woven into the interface.

- <PatternRef slug="bot">Bot</PatternRef> — the conversational partner mediating between intent and action
- [Prompt](/patterns/prompt) — expressing what you want the bot to do
- [Conversation](/patterns/conversation) — sustained back-and-forth dialogue, built from conversational [operations](../?path=/docs/operations-overview--docs)
- [Generated content](/patterns/generated-content) — what the bot produces
- <PatternRef slug="transparent-reasoning">Transparent reasoning</PatternRef> — making the bot's thinking visible
- [Embedded intelligence](/patterns/embedded-intelligence) — AI capabilities woven into existing workflows
- [AI tuning](/patterns/ai-tuning) — configuring bot behaviour and preferences

## Collaboration

Patterns for multi-actor coordination around shared artefacts and goals.

- <PatternRef slug="collaboration">Collaboration</PatternRef> — coordination framework for shared work
- <PatternRef slug="activity-log">Activity log</PatternRef> — chronological record of who did what
- <PatternRef slug="activity-feed">Activity feed</PatternRef> — aggregated stream of events

## Learning & growth

Supporting actors in developing expertise and understanding over time.

- [Onboarding](/patterns/onboarding) — first-time experience establishing mental models
- <PatternRef slug="help">Help</PatternRef> — assistance and guidance when needed
- <PatternRef slug="mastery">Mastery</PatternRef> — supporting long-term skill acquisition

## Environments

Sustained contexts in which work happens.

- [Workspace](/patterns/workspace) — unified environment for tasks
- [Living document](/patterns/living-document) — documents that evolve through use
