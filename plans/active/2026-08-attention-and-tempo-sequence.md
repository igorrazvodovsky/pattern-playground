# Attention and tempo — the eleventh sequence

Phase 5 of [the sequence-map plan](2026-08-sequence-map.md): the tenth sequence authored after the pilot, and the second of the map's missing four. The sequence's authoritative home is `apps/patterns/src/content/sequences/attention-and-tempo.mdx`; this document is the authoring trace — the sequence as first written, the research gate, the read-aloud walk, the test walks with their backtracks, and the corpus changes the authoring surfaced. The corpus changes are recorded here, not executed — each is judgment work for its own sitting.

This territory was the ripest of the remaining four: six authored connections already land in it — the partition boundary (structuring the space), the publish (making changes), the unattended run's interruption (delegating work), the hand-back (governing autonomy), the uninvited proposal (assistance escalation), and the pushed remainder (memory and continuity) — five of the six via notification, plus the encounter sitting's finding that an unprompted agent opening is an interruption by any name. The 2026-04 agency-attentional-coupling plan is the territory's prior work (attentional coupling as a distribution dimension; loose coupling needing designed re-entry touchpoints), and the *Working Through Screens* D band its early coverage.

Unlike the earlier missing-sequence sitting, this one ran a research gate before locking the spine, because the territory has a real experimental literature the corpus barely cites. Both retrieval APIs were rate-limited, so the gate ran as a named-canon read plus a separate practice survey; the notes are a literature note (thirteen papers, seven clusters) and a vendor-doc practice survey, both 2026-08-29. Three findings reshaped the strawman spine before authoring: the pull infrastructure moved ahead of the arrival timing (deferral is only safe when a passive layer catches what waits — silence converts push into polling), deferral itself narrowed to seconds-to-minutes at task boundaries rather than long holds, and the breakthrough valve moved from the arrival region into the budget (it is granted in advance, not negotiated per event). The walks then confirmed the first and third of these independently.

No connection-ownership transfers were due: no authored file holds a `from: attention-and-tempo` entry, and the six incoming connections stay on their outgoing files, following the precedent set when learning and memory and continuity were authored.

Steps are decisions, written as verbs, each invoking a pattern as its rule. Constituents ride inside steps in parentheses.

## Presupposition (the head)

The map's own: a judgement about what may interrupt whom, and when — priority and workload settled before any notification is designed; still a legible gap, discharged nowhere. Three structural facts joined it at authoring. The sequence is the push half of the language's receiving end — five walks hand it events at the moment they happen, and memory and continuity keeps what can wait, so the two sequences split one judgement: interrupt now, or wait for the return. It runs at three rates — the budget once per product, the quiet layer standing per surface, the arrival at every event. And the failure it designs against is revocation, not the missed message: the practice survey found shipping vendors converging on restraint (algorithmic backoff, one-tap grade revocation, gated top rungs) because an overspent budget ends with the actor turning the channel off wholesale — while the literature adds that the costs hide from output metrics, since interrupted actors keep their speed and pay in stress.

The sequence enacts *temporality* (when things arrive, and how disruptive the timing is) and *agency* (the budget is retunable by the actor, and the override always wins).

## The sequence

### A. Setting the budget — once per product

1. *Grade every claim on the actor.* Every event class the other walks hand over, placed on a small fixed vocabulary of interruption grades — act, know now, find later — with the top grade guarded; graded per class, not per event. The platform vocabularies (iOS's four interruption levels, Android's five importance levels) are the shipped form. Rule: [status-feedback].
2. *Route each grade to a channel.* Each grade lands on the disruption ladder — seize, announce without seizing, pass silently to the quiet layer. Below the top rung the principle is negotiation (McFarlane): the arrival announces, the actor chooses when to attend. The ladder's ceiling: an event needing a decision rather than a look is an opened exchange, not a notification. Rule: [notification].
3. *Protect the stretch.* Declared unavailability — focus block, allowed hours, quiet days — and what the channels do with what arrives meanwhile. The weak form: no pattern owns it; the trap on record is stacked protections that do not compose (finding 3).
4. *Design the breakthrough.* The protection's valve, spent from step 1's top rung: who may pierce a quiet, at what rate. Shipping convergence: rate-limited, named-sender exceptions. A protection without a valve gets revoked; a valve without a rate limit protects nothing. The weak form (moved here from the arrival region by walk 1's backtrack; finding 2).
5. *Hand the whole budget to the actor.* Everything above becomes a retunable default; the override always wins and the product can never raise a grade back. Defaults set as if nobody will open the controls, because the evidence that actors tune is thin and self-set regimes decay. The recurring silence of a class is grading evidence. Rule: [settings].

### B. Building the quiet layer — standing, per surface

*Initiating situation.* The routing sent most events below the push line, and the arrival region's deferrals are only safe if a passive layer catches what waits: an actor who cannot glance will poll.

1. *Give the waitable a visible home* — an unordered cluster, the passive channel's two faces:
   - *Wear the state in place.* Graded marks — nothing, a dot, a count reserved for direct address. A mark's grammar is a promise about what a glance can rule out. Rule: [status-feedback], its indication shape.
   - *Pool what does not interrupt.* One flow, scoped by the shipped rule — interrupt for direct address, pool the rest; ordering as a claim, subscription grammar as the gate, the no-end force priced in. Rule: [activity-feed].
2. *Let the actor set their own tempo.* Snooze, remind-me, scheduled send: arrivals and outgoing claims turned into appointments, anchored at the next working block. Self-scheduled interruption, the one kind the budget never guesses about. The weak form (finding 4).

### C. Timing the arrival — per event

*Initiating situation.* An event happens at a moment nobody chose, mid-work the budget cannot see from its once-per-product distance.

1. *Pick the moment.* Deferral as evidenced, not as folklore: hold seconds to minutes for the next task boundary, never park for hours — long holds belong to the pool and the catch-up. Escalate only if unseen (the shipped channel ordering); batch only at a coarse, predictable, opted-into cadence. The weak form (finding 4).
2. *Compress the stream only visibly.* Optional — skipped where events arrive singly. The generated digest, bar set by public failure: marked as not the source, claims resolving to events, opt-in. The weak form; the call into calibrating reliance rides it (finding 6).
3. *Let the glance-sized pass in a glance.* The ladder's floor: window and passenger — one action at most, undo canonical, the dismissal window doubling as the recovery window; what matters also lands in the pool. Rule: [transient-feedback].
4. *Owe the way back.* The territory's best-evidenced obligation: cues and saved state at every grade, because deferring an arrival does not cheapen the return. Past the session's edge the move becomes memory and continuity's restored place. The weak form. (Constituent: deep-linking — the interrupted place is an address.)

## The research gate

The gate produced a query.yml, a canon-read literature note, and a practice survey, both 2026-08-29. What the gate corrected in the strawman, beyond the three spine changes above: the push/pull split cannot ignore the awareness function (actors keep notifications to avoid checking; total silence produced anxiety and polling in some studies, benefits in others — per-person, so the split is tunable, not fixed); evidence that actor-tuned controls calibrate well is thin to absent, which demoted the tuning step's ambition to "defaults carry the weight"; batching helps only at a coarse predictable cadence, single-study; resumption support survives challenge and sharpened (deferral does not cheapen the return); and default-on AI summarisation of notifications has failed publicly, which set the compress step's opt-in bar. McFarlane's taxonomy held as the timing decision's shape with negotiation as the winning cell, which became the routing step's principle.

## The read-aloud walk

The test from Book 2 ch. 11 §5: read aloud, the design should form stepwise, no step contradicting what previous steps built.

> A judgement says what may interrupt whom. First the budget: every event class graded on a small vocabulary with the top rung guarded; each grade routed onto a ladder that announces without seizing, with the decision-needing event leaving the ladder to open an exchange; the protected stretch declared in hours and days, pierced only by a designed, rate-limited valve; then the whole arrangement handed over as retunable defaults set for the actor who never opens the controls. Then the quiet layer those routes presuppose: marks worn where the actor already looks, whose steady absence says nothing needs you; the rest pooled in one flow that is glanced at and left; and the actor given the means to turn any arrival into an appointment. Last, each event: its moment picked — held for the next boundary, escalated only if unseen; its stream compressed only in the open; the glance-sized passing in a glance with its one passenger; and the way back owed, cues and saved state, because deferral never cheapened the return.

Verdict: passes. Two deliberate debts inside the walk: the valve spends the grading step's top rung (recorded at creation, collected two steps later), and the arrival region's deferrals spend the quiet layer (which is why the quiet layer stands between budget and arrival). The five weak steps are absences marked, not contradictions.

## Test walks

A3: evenings and weekends protected by default schedule, a triage-hour focus block declared ad hoc. A4: the valve — the on-call rota may pierce, once per incident, re-ping bounded. A5: all of it per project and per kind in settings, defaults doing the work; the triager who mutes the rule set's summaries every week is telling the grader something. B1: dot per project, count only on direct address; the pool filtered participating-versus-watching. B2: a review request snoozed to tomorrow 9am. C1: the review-request push held until the current edit reaches its save boundary; mobile push only after desktop goes idle. C3: the export-finished toast carries undo for the accidental re-run. C4: the incident pulls the triager out mid-edit; the tracker holds the draft and the way back is the issue's address. *Backtrack found:* the first draft placed the breakthrough decision in the arrival region, as part of picking the moment — as if the valve were negotiated per event. The walk showed it is a standing grant: the on-call rota's right to pierce is decided with the protection, in advance, per sender and class — an arrival only spends it. The step moved into the budget region after the protection (finding 2).

*Walk 2 — the research assistant (continuing the prior sittings' walk 2; conversation-first).* A1: the pipeline's needs-input graded know-now; run completions active; the forty new matches find-later. A2: in a conversation-first product the top of the ladder is the agent opening the exchange — the routing's ceiling is the normal case here, the posture inversion the other sittings kept finding. A3–A4: the writing block protected; the grant deadline's watcher may pierce it. A5: the researcher never opens the controls; the defaults carry everything. B1: the pipeline pane wears a steady all-clear mark — the cheapest signal in the budget; matches pool in the feed. B2: "remind me after the advisor meeting". C1: the run-complete announcement waits for the paragraph boundary; unseen on desktop for long enough, it escalates to the phone. C4: the needs-input diversion returns the researcher to the draft with the cursor where it was. *Backtrack found:* the first draft ordered the regions budget → arrival → quiet layer, treating marks and pool as polish behind the push channel. The walk broke at C1: the deferral step kept promising "the pool catches it, the mark says so" before any step had decided a pool or a mark exists — and the literature note says why the promise is load-bearing (an actor who cannot glance, polls). *On the transfer question:* the walks guided at the joints again — what must stand before deferral is safe, which grants precede which arrivals — and at the posture flip: the embedded product's ladder tops out at an opened exchange, while the conversation-first product lives at that ceiling. The per-event region transfers most directly; its four steps read as a checklist any product's notification review could run.

## Findings — corpus changes the authoring forces

Recorded for their own sittings; none executed here.

1. *The spine found, with the pull layer load-bearing.* The map entry said "no spine connects them"; the authoring resolved the territory into three regions at three rates — budget, quiet layer, arrival — with the quiet layer deliberately between the other two: deferral is only safe when a passive channel catches what waits (walk 2's backtrack; the literature's awareness clusters). The map entry's member list also corrects: disengage-without-closing stays with the encounter's endings and its resumption face with memory and continuity's return; handoff contributes event classes (the hand-back, the scheduled return of finished work), not a step.
2. *The breakthrough is a standing grant.* Walk 1's backtrack: who may pierce a declared quiet is decided with the protection, per sender and class, rate-limited — never negotiated per event. Practice convergence (Slack's once-per-day override, Teams' bounded re-ping, iOS's gated top level) is the shipped form.
3. *Five patternless stations marked from home territory.* The protected stretch (focus modes, allowed hours — every vendor ships one, no pattern owns it), the breakthrough valve (its designed exceptions), the moment (breakpoint deferral, escalate-only-if-unseen, coarse batching), the generated digest (the compressed stream, with its opt-in bar), and interruption-scale resumption (the way back, cues and saved state). Each is a classification-sitting candidate; the two research notes hold the grounding. The scheduled return (snooze, remind-me, scheduled send) is a sixth, the thinnest — possibly one pattern with the moment's deferral rather than its own.
4. *Escalate-only-if-unseen is a shipped convergent move.* Three independent vendors order channels so the interruptive one fires only after the ambient one failed (in-app before push, desktop before mobile, presence-gated terminal bells). Belongs to the moment station's future page; recorded here so the sighting is not lost.
5. *notification's page undersells its own territory.* The pattern confirmed as a shared station (five authored hands-offs land via it), but its body routes shape (toast, callout, dialog) without the grading vocabulary the platforms impose (interruption levels, importance) or the negotiation principle the literature supports, and its decision tree's leaves are components. Candidate enrichment: the grade axis, the revocation force as a resulting clause ("an overspent budget ends with the channel off"), and the ladder-as-negotiation framing. The research notes ground all three.
6. *The generated digest's bar is set by public failure.* Default-on AI summarisation of notifications shipped, misattributed, and retreated to opt-in behind a warning; the compress step carries the obligation and the calls-edge into calibrating reliance via provenance-marking is its typed form. When the digest station is classified (finding 3), this is its opening evidence.
7. *settings → notification is claimed.* The clause's own text — a shared-configuration change must be carried across to the actors it affects — is an event class arriving at this sequence's grading step. One of the map's three unplaced settings edges resolves; settings → undo and settings → status-feedback remain unplaced.
8. *The map's open question is answered.* Attention and tempo is one sequence, not the temporal face of several others, for the same reason memory and continuity is: the budget is one account. Every walk's push spends from it, and distributing the judgement re-creates per-feature notification settings — the attention equivalent of the log-per-feature the keeping region unified. The walks' posture flip (the embedded ladder tops out where the conversational product lives) is variation within one walk, not two sequences.
9. *The reliance seam has a second face here.* The recurring silence of a class (A5) is grading evidence the same way calibrating reliance's recurring override is tuning evidence; a resulting clause on settings or notification could type it, mirroring the candidate already recorded on activity-log. Left as the thinnest finding — the sighting is one step's gloss.


## Revision to the step-length rules (2026-09-27)

The sequence was revised under the step-length rules in docs/specs/sequences.md §Authoring, in the plain wording of the earlier revisions. The file went from about 1,865 words to about 1,275. Five step titles changed wording ("Give what can wait a visible home", "Show the state in place", "Summarise a stream only openly", "Let a message that needs only a glance pass in a glance", "Provide the way back"); no step, rule, order, or connection changed, and sub-sequence ids are unchanged, so the walks and every incoming `at` address still stand.

Where the removed text went:

- The presupposition carried the three rates, the failure the design guards against (the actor turning the channel off), and the costs that output metrics miss. All three hold across regions, so they moved into the lead; the presupposition now states only the judgement settled upstream.
- The reasons behind the weak steps and the settings step came from the research gate above: the awareness function (actors keep notifications to avoid checking), the thin evidence that actors tune well, batching helping only at a predictable cadence, resumption support surviving challenge, and the public failure of default-on summaries (also finding 6). The glosses keep the decisions those reasons produced: defaults set as if nobody opens the controls, hold for seconds to minutes, escalate only if unseen, batch only at a chosen interval, mark summaries and make them opt-in, provide the way back at every grade.
- Pattern content already on the pages: the feed's ordering as a claim and its lack of an end (activity-feed), the dismissal window and the undo passenger (transient-feedback).
- The "no pattern owns" remarks on the five weak steps are finding 3; the missing `rule` marks them.
- The budget and quiet-layer `resulting` clauses restated the next `initiating` and were removed.

The routing step keeps the three levels of disruption and the negotiated arrival in full, because the notification page does not carry them yet (finding 5).

Step-list test: the step lines, with the cluster and its two members, read as a walk without their glosses.
