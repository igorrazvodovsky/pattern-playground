# Learning — the ninth sequence

Phase 5 of [the sequence-map plan](2026-08-sequence-map.md): the eighth sequence authored after the pilot. The sequence's authoritative home is `apps/patterns/src/content/sequences/learning.mdx`; this document is the authoring trace — the sequence as first written, the read-aloud walk, the test walks with their backtracks, and the corpus changes the authoring surfaced. The corpus changes are recorded here, not executed — each is judgment work for its own sitting.

This territory was picked as the ripest of the queue: it is the only latent entry the map does not flag as needing groundwork, and four authored connections already land in it, pinning its stations from outside — two onboarding arrivals (the encounter's capability statement, delegating work's intermediary first run) and two mastery calls (governing autonomy's takeback, calibrating reliance's withholding). The spine evidence: agent → onboarding and onboarding → mastery as `precedes` chains, with state-empty, template, and wizard attached through `hosts`/`enables` and the accelerator pair (command-menu, keyboard-shortcuts) attached through `complements`.

No connection-ownership transfers were due: no authored file holds a `from: learning` entry, and the four incoming connections stay on their outgoing files. Their plain `to: learning` addresses were left unrefined, following the precedent set when delegating work was authored.

Steps are decisions, written as verbs, each invoking a pattern as its rule. Constituents ride inside steps in parentheses.

## Presupposition (the head)

What competent use looks like is known — the practice mapped well enough to say what the newcomer is being brought toward: which objects matter, what fluent work looks like, where the common ground with colleagues lies. That mapping is process-register work, settled upstream; the map's discharge audit already lists it as a legible gap, and this sequence marks the adjacent gap from inside (the upkeep region's weak step — see finding 4). Where the system will act on its own, the target model includes the collaboration itself: knowing what the system will do unasked is part of competent use. The sequence's structural fact is per-actor recurrence: it is walked per actor rather than once per product, and re-entered in miniature at every change — which is what the two agent-first-run arrivals from other sequences are.

The sequence enacts *learnability* throughout (it is the quality's temporal dimension made a walk — initial at the arrival, extended on the road) and *temporality* (the long arc of one actor's relationship with the tool, from first encounter to kept skill).

## The sequence

### A. Shaping the first encounter — the arrival

1. *Bound the first encounter.* The first run made a designed, bounded, low-stakes period aimed at the target model the presupposed picture supplies — objects and state, what acting does, and what the system will do on its own. Joining a system in use adds a third question — what is already here — answered by tracing how things came to be (the archaeology call into memory and continuity). Creates the debt the road region collects: every scaffold erected must later fade. Rule: [onboarding].
2. *Make the emptiness speak.* The first-run canvas: the largest uninterrupted surface the product ever gets, in front of an actor who has committed nothing. Each empty region says why and points somewhere; only the first-run face is decided here — the multiplying degraded-state empties belong to the late pass. Rule: [state-empty].
3. *Choose what the newcomer occupies* — a non-exclusive choice; occupying competent work teaches faster than reading about it, and taking neither leaves the speaking emptiness as the start:
   - *Start them inside competent work.* The conventional shape occupied rather than described; the set is a curation with an owner. Rule: [template].
   - *Corridor the dependent setup.* Where first decisions genuinely depend on each other and are taken once; taken by whichever actor's arrival includes that setup — usually the first into a workspace (walk 1's backtrack; finding 1). Rule: [wizard].
   - The generative third way — intent asked, a draft generated, learning by refining — is a call into delegating work, not a step.

### B. Meeting friction — the channel

*Initiating situation.* Ahead-of-time teaching always misses; the actor hits friction the arrival did not cover, and the answer exists somewhere other than where they are looking.

1. *Meet friction where it happens.* The standing pull channel: it reaches the actor who thought to ask and says nothing to the one who didn't know there was something to ask about, which is why it supplements the arrival rather than replacing it. Answers placed at the point of need, pitched at interface or domain; the whole is a second telling of the system, kept true. When trouble needs reasoning about the specific situation, static content runs out — the hands-off into the encounter, posture-inverted (walk 2's backtrack; finding 8). Rule: [help]. (Constituent: annotation — attaching the answer to the element that raised the question.)

### C. Opening the road past the interface — expertise

*Initiating situation.* The actor is in the tool daily; the scaffolding is in the way and the interface, not their understanding, is the cap. Most actors stay perpetual intermediates, so everything here is optional or the road becomes a march.

1. *Clear the scaffolding and open optional routes.* Collects the arrival's debt (scaffolding fades), and sets the posture once: accelerators, depth, and the tool's movable parts as routes nobody is forced onto. Rule: [mastery].
2. *Name every command.* Discovery moves from scanning to typing a guess; the intermediates' bridge to direct invocation. The applies-only menu makes the absent-but-expected command a mystery — the debt the late pass collects. Rule: [command-menu].
3. *Bind the names to keys.* Acts on the names just given; the knowing-using gap closed where the actor already looks — the hint on the slower path, the glyph at the menu's edge, the annotation in the menu just built. A fixed budget, conventions already spent. Rule: [keyboard-shortcuts].

### D. Keeping the skill — upkeep

*Initiating situation.* Parts of the practice stop being exercised — rare procedures, and work handed over a rung or a grant at a time. Unexercised skill decays, and two sequences arrive assuming it hasn't: the takeback and the withholding.

1. *Choose what stays practiced.* The inventory of skills that must stay alive human-side, graded by what their loss would cost. The weak form: no pattern owns the judgement (finding 4).
2. *Re-expose what would fade.* Retention scheduled rather than left to the forgetting curve — contextual reminders, decision echoes, spaced resurfacing — landing where the actor already is: the review, the takeback, the answer attempted first. Rule: [mastery], its retention face.

## The read-aloud walk

The test from Book 2 ch. 11 §5: read aloud, the design should form stepwise, no step contradicting what previous steps built.

> There is a picture of what competent use looks like, and an actor arrives without it. First the arrival, designed rather than left to chance: the first run bounded and made safe, aimed at the target model — including what the system will do on its own; the emptiness that fills the first screen made to say why it is empty and what would fill it; and the newcomer given something competent to occupy — a template to sand and paint, a corridor for the setup decisions that depend on each other, or a draft generated from their stated intent. Then the standing channel, because ahead-of-time teaching always misses: friction has somewhere to go, the answer arrives in context, and the channel's second telling of the system is kept true as the system changes. Then the road past the interface, opened without being forced: the scaffolding cleared as competence grows, every command reachable by name, the names bound to keys for what has become motor memory — with the intermediates losing nothing by staying on the main path. And last the upkeep: as procedures recur rarely or stop passing through the actor's hands, what must stay practiced is chosen, and what is chosen is re-exposed where the actor already is — because other walks return work to them assuming the skill survived.

Verdict: passes. Two deliberate debts inside the walk: the arrival's scaffolding debt, collected by the road's first step; and the applies-only menu's mystery, recorded at the naming step and collected outside the sequence by the late pass. Obligations carried forward, not contradictions.

## Test walks

Recorded per ch. 11 §7: a sequence fails where a later step forces undoing an earlier step's product.

*Walk 1 — the issue tracker (continuing the prior sittings' walk 1; embedded posture; the actors walked are the workspace's first admin and a teammate joining later).* A1: the joiner's first run is bounded — a sandbox project where draft issues cost nothing — and the target model includes the label taxonomy, what triage does, and what the overnight rule set (handed over in the assistance-escalation walk) will do unasked; the taxonomy's why is answered by tracing how it came to be — the archaeology call. A2: the personal queue's blank slate points at the first assignment; the empty board column says what would move a card into it. A3: both arms taken, at different grain — the admin's arrival includes the setup corridor (project creation's dependent steps), and every later joiner starts from issue templates (the bug report's conventional shape). B1: field hints on the form, the ? dialog, and the domain answer behind the disabled control ("the fiscal quarter is closed") — kept true when the workflow changes. C1: the seeded tour never re-runs; the routes are posted, not pushed. C2: the ⌘K menu names triage, assign, label. C3: shortcut glyphs ride the menu and the tooltips on the slower paths; ⌘-conventions untouched. D1: triage judgement goes on the kept list — the overnight rules hold it now, and the takeback assumes the triager can still triage; the rarely-run permissions change goes on it too. D2: the decision echo at re-entry ("this rule was set when the component split") and spaced resurfacing of the triage rubric inside change review. *Backtrack found:* the first draft marked the occupancy choice exclusive — a product starts newcomers from a template *or* a corridor. The tracker took both, at different grain and for different actors: the corridor belongs to the arrival that includes the dependent setup (the first actor into the workspace), the templates to every arrival after. Exclusivity was dropped and the wizard arm's gloss took the first-arrival condition (finding 1).

*Walk 2 — the research assistant (continuing the prior sittings' walk 2; conversation-first).* A1: the assistant's first statement of what it can and cannot do is the onboarding moment — the encounter's hands-off lands here — and the bounded period is the sandboxed first conversation; the target model is mostly the collaboration itself: what the assistant will do unasked. A2: the conversation's emptiness speaks through its starter suggestions (the `prompt hosts suggestion` surface the assistance-escalation walk classified); the empty library points at the import. A3: the template arm is the structured request (prompt-scaffolding is template's instance in this posture); the generative arm is taken — "what are you researching?" yields a starter survey the researcher refines, the call into delegating work; the corridor is skipped — the set-up form's decisions don't gate each other. B1: the backtrack, below. C1–C3: the embedded survey editor earns the road — its ⌘K, its shortcut set — while the conversational side's route past the interface is prompt fluency, covered by the posture step's "routes, not marches" rather than by the menu. D1: the researcher's dedup judgement goes on the kept list — the pipeline was handed over, and reviewing its output depends on still being able to do it by eye; so does the corpus-selection rationale, exercised rarely. D2: the withheld answer (calibrating reliance's call) is the re-exposure surface: the assistant asks the researcher to judge borderline duplicates first on a sampled fraction. *Backtrack found:* the first draft phrased the channel's limit as an escalation — "when static content runs out, the conversation opens" — as if the conversation were always a farther place. On the conversation-first surface the channel's conversational form *is* the encounter, entered directly from the first question, while the second telling (what the assistant can be asked, what the tool is) still needs authoring and keeping true. The step survived intact — help remains the decision — and the connection note was rewritten posture-aware, matching the encounter sitting's endings precedent (finding 8).

*On the transfer question:* the walks guided at the region boundaries — what a product owes the newcomer before it may assume competence, and what it owes the expert before it may assume retention — more than in fine-grained order. The sequence's distinctive contribution is the tail: the other sequences build competence's consumers (review, takeback, withholding); this one is where the assumption they all rest on is made true.

## Findings — corpus changes the authoring forces

Recorded for their own sittings; none executed here.

1. *The spine reversed at the head, and the occupancy became a choice.* The map's candidate spine — state-empty / template / wizard → onboarding → mastery — read the `hosts`/`enables` attachments as spine arrows: construction order, not decision order. The authored trunk opens with onboarding (the bounded-period decision precedes what fills it), state-empty is its canvas step, and {template, wizard} resolved into a non-exclusive occupancy choice — walk 1 dropped the draft's exclusivity and put the first-arrival condition on the wizard arm. The attachments stay valid as edges; step-versus-constituent status is exactly what the sequence layer stores.
2. *state-empty's two faces split across sequences.* This sequence takes only the first-run face (the teaching canvas); the degraded-state multiplication — the filter that excluded everything, the permission boundary — is reserved for finishing the whole, whose map entry already lists state-empty in the degraded-states walk. Recorded so that sitting takes the non-first-run face rather than re-walking this one.
3. *mastery's maintenance was under-credited by two sittings.* The governing-autonomy and calibrating-reliance notes both say the mastery rung "carries nothing about maintenance yet"; its §Retention already holds the forgetting-curve half (contextual reminders, spaced re-exposure, decision echoes). What is actually missing is the second source of disuse — the skill that stopped passing through the actor's hands because it was handed over, not because it is rare. Candidates: a Retention addition naming delegation as a source of disuse and the takeback/withholding as where re-exposure lands, and touch-ups to the two connection notes.
4. *The stays-practiced inventory is patternless — third sighting, now from home territory.* upkeep/1 is the weak form: nothing owns the judgement of which skills the collaboration must keep alive human-side. Governing autonomy sighted it at the takeback, calibrating reliance at the withholding, and delegating work's sighted-but-unclaimed list carries it as "practice against atrophy"; this sequence marks it from inside. Three sightings from three sequences plus a home step is the strongest patternless-station signal the map holds — a classification sitting's candidate.
5. *activity-log gains a fourth reading: newcomer archaeology.* Onboarding's joining-systems-in-use dimension reads the record for institutional context — why things are configured as they are — beside the work, governance, and reliance readings. Strengthens memory and continuity's claim on activity-log as substrate and gives its spine hunt a fourth entry point: the arriving actor and the returning actor read the same record.
6. *command-menu → unavailable-actions classified.* A hands-off into finishing the whole — the applies-only menu makes the absent-but-expected command a mystery the late pass explains; the `sets-up` edge is the typed form. The first authored connection into that missing sequence, and an entry point for its spine: the degraded-states walk now has an authored arrival.
7. *The generative opening is a third arrival at delegating work's framing head.* The arrival's occupancy choice calls delegating work via prompt (intent → draft → refine). The connection is deliberately addressed to the sequence, not the step: typing framing/1 would stale that file's two-source framing, the same reconciliation assistance-escalation's finding 7 queued for the accept-or-perform flip. One delegating-work sitting reconciles the doorway count for both.
8. *help ↔ agent carries a bare `related` edge.* Walk 2's backtrack named what a note should carry: help shifts to conversation when static content runs out, and the shift's distance is a posture fact — an escalation on embedded surfaces, no distance at all on conversation-first ones, where the channel is the encounter. Candidate note for a sitting that touches help.
9. *Shared stations.* onboarding — already on the map's list from its incoming arrivals — is confirmed from its home spine: the encounter's capability statement and delegating work's intermediary first run both land on arrival/1. mastery joins the shared stations: two authored calls (the takeback, the withholding) land at the upkeep region, and its home spine now walks it twice (the road's posture step and the retention face).


## Revision to the step-length rules (2026-09-27)

The sequence was revised under the step-length rules in docs/specs/sequences.md §Authoring, in the plain wording of the earlier revisions. The file went from about 1,870 words to about 1,180. Two step titles changed wording ("Walk them through dependent setup", "Choose what stays practised") and one sub-sequence title ("Opening routes past the interface"); no step, rule, order, or connection changed, and sub-sequence ids are unchanged, so the walks and every incoming `at` address still stand.

Where the removed text went:

- The lead listed the four sub-sequences. It now keeps the situation and the recurring smaller arrival, which absorbed the arrival `resulting`'s point that it is announced, deferrable, and reversible.
- Pattern reasons and costs already on the pattern pages: low stakes and doing over reading (onboarding), the largest uninterrupted surface and empty states multiplying (state-empty), what a template prescribes read as required (template), pull-based help and the second telling (help), perpetual intermediates (mastery), scanning versus typing a guess and the unexplained missing command (command-menu), and the invisible vocabulary and fixed budget (keyboard-shortcuts).
- The channel's `resulting` (static content runs out and the answer becomes a conversation) is carried by the connection to the encounter.
- The "no pattern holds the judgement" remark on the stays-practised step is finding 4; the missing `rule` marks it.
- Connection notes lost their remarks on how many readings of the record exist and on typed edges, which are findings 5 and 6.

One point was lost: the expertise `resulting`'s claim that the strongest predictor of taking the optional routes is working next to someone already on them, which the product can encourage but not decide. The mastery page does not carry it. Candidate: a resulting clause on mastery, for the sitting that next edits that page.

Step-list test: the step lines, with the choice and its two arms, read as a walk without their glosses.
