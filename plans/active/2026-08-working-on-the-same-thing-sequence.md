# Working on the same thing — the thirteenth sequence

Phase 5 of [the sequence-map plan](2026-08-sequence-map.md): the twelfth sequence authored after the pilot, and the third of the map's missing four. The sequence's authoritative home is `apps/patterns/src/content/sequences/working-on-the-same-thing.mdx`; this document is the authoring trace — the research gate, the sequence as first written, the read-aloud walk, the test walks with their backtracks, and the corpus changes the authoring surfaced. The corpus changes are recorded here, not executed — each is judgment work for its own sitting — except the three seed pages, created in-sitting at the author's direction (the making-sense-at-scale precedent).

The territory had six authored arrivals waiting for landing stations — the transfer to another person (governing autonomy), the origin mark in shared material (calibrating reliance), scheduled send (attention and tempo), the directed thread and the handed framing (making sense at scale), and the save-boundary interleaving (making changes) — which made it the riper of the remaining two; finishing the whole is by its own definition the late pass and stays last.

The sitting ran gate-first, the third to do so. The gate's three strands are `PARA/Projects/pattern playground/research/working-together/` (the folder keeps the sequence's first slug) — a literature note (CSCW canon named-and-grounded, three full-text reads; arxiv retrieval for the conflict question; Semantic Scholar rate-limited after two answers, lineage deferred), a practice survey (five products, mechanisms not marketing), and a *Working Through Screens* quarry read (the J band nearly whole, plus C5/C7/G4/B7/G7 and the presuppositional A2/A7 — the densest single-band claim any sequence has made on the quarry). Three findings reshaped the strawman before authoring: the grant moved to the head (every surveyed product stages an explicit graded grant before any other mechanism), "presence" widened from one missing pattern to a graded awareness layer fed by the work itself, and "concurrent conflict" re-shaped from a missing pattern into a placement rule — prevented by awareness, absorbed by merge, surfaced only at deliberate fork boundaries.

No connection-ownership transfers were due: no authored file holds a `from: working-on-the-same-thing` entry — the six arrivals stay on their outgoing files, per the standing precedent.

Steps are decisions, written as verbs, each invoking a pattern as its rule. Constituents ride inside steps in parentheses.

## Presupposition (the head)

The map's own: the working relationships mapped — who shares what with whom, in which roles; the collaboration foundation records part of this. Three structural facts joined it at authoring. The walk runs at four rates — the grant once per shared space, the visibility layer standing per surface, the contribution per contribution, the parting and the handoff each at its own per-event rate. One principle governs the whole walk: awareness information is captured as a by-product of the work, never authored as a separate report — Dourish & Bellotti's passive-awareness finding and the design answer to Grudin's work/benefit disparity, fused. And the failure designed against is desertion, not the missed edit: material that penalises contributors or watches them too closely loses the collaboration to outside channels (the screenshot in chat, the copy on a desktop) where none of the mechanisms reach — *Working Through Screens* G7 records workers avoiding visibility-intensive tools entirely.

The sequence enacts *privacy* (the grant is the privacy boundary made explicit; visibility stays translucent, not transparent) and *shareability* (the handed view carries the state that produced it).

## The sequence

### A. Granting the space — once per shared space

1. *Open the material to named others.* The explicit graded grant — view, comment, edit — inheriting down containers, capped at what the granter holds; each tier a promise the later regions pay. Force: a grant cut too fine starves common ground (C5's converse). Rule: [sharing] (seeded this sitting).
2. *Declare what the grant shows of unfinished work.* The working/showing boundary, blurred (live ground) to distinct (reviewed ground), declared per kind of material — the same tool's issues can be live while its code is reviewed (both walks). Binds the regions after it: presence's living grades need a blurred boundary; the parting region is the distinct boundary made crossable. The weak form — no pattern owns the declaration (finding 4). Moved here from the parting region by walk 2's backtrack.

### B. Making each other visible — standing, per surface

*Initiating situation.* The grant put several people behind separate screens into one material, each able to look at a different part — relaxed WYSIWIS creating the awareness debt (Gutwin & Greenberg). Awareness that must be authored goes unfed.

1. *Show the workers in the material* — an unordered cluster, one decision's two faces:
   - *Wear the living presence.* Graded marks — stack, cursor and selection, action signal — matched to the declared ground, thinning toward the trail as the surface slows; the directed forms (follow, spotlight) are the marks turned into a channel. Rule: [presence] (seeded this sitting).
   - *Sign the written contribution.* Authorship at the point of writing — the edit attributed, the comment named — so a mark can be answered and the account read later; the machine contributor's signature routes its output into the reliance walk (the call into calibrating reliance). Rule: [provenance-marking], its person-to-person face. (Constituent: activity-log — the signed contributions accumulate as the space's account.)
2. *Give the exchange a shared view.* Common ground made cheap: the view handable as one thing — an address asynchronously, a followed or spotlit screen live — against the documented failure of retracing steps to recreate a display (J2). What the view may not carry is the boundary the grant set. The weak form (finding 4). (Constituent: deep-linking — the view travels as an address.)

### C. Contributing in place — per contribution

*Initiating situation.* The others are visible; now something is said. Communication rides the material's own pathways (J1), and the mark that waits beats the message that interrupts (J5).

1. *Mark the material in place.* The non-interrupting floor: anchored where it applies, read on arrival. Optional — skipped where the material's grain is already the discussion's grain (an issue, a card; walk 1's refinement). Rule: [annotation].
2. *Anchor the discussion.* Threads beside particular places, resolution built in; assigning a thread is the handoff region's move at comment grain. Rule: [commenting].
3. *Type the standing report.* The recurring genres — states, summaries, conclusions — on a small shared vocabulary (J6), bounded by the genre's own inclusions and exclusions. What must reach a colleague now leaves the region and arrives against their interruption budget. Rule: [status-feedback].

### D. Parting and returning — per parting

*Initiating situation.* Joint work needs to not be joint for a while: the rough draft, the large change, the offline stretch. The grant's distinct boundary made into machinery — permitted divergence with an owed return.

1. *Permit the parting.* Working copy separated from what others read; whole-artefact or per-change grain is draft-and-publish's own axis, invoked once. Each publish a named point the keeping can hold. Debt recorded: every parting owes a return surface. Rule: [draft-and-publish].
2. *Review the returning delta.* The change judged as a change, at the parting's grain, conclusion typed; the person-to-person case is the pattern's own pre-AI lineage (finding 6). Rule: [change-review], its person-to-person face.
3. *Reconcile the collision.* The merge substrate absorbs what it can; the remainder blocks reintegration until a person picks between visible versions. The placement rule is the content: only at the return of deliberate divergence — a collision mid-flow means the boundary was declared wrong. Rule: [reconciliation] (seeded this sitting).

### E. Passing work on — per transfer

*Initiating situation.* The work must continue in other hands; what crosses is responsibility, not only material. Its own rate, with or without any parting — broken out of the parting region by walk 1's backtrack.

1. *Pass the work on with its context.* The typed object: a pointer into the material, a named responsibility, a subscription shed explicitly. Debt recorded: the sender is owed received/reviewed/acted-on (J3). Optional — skipped where organisational norms outside the tool carry the passing (J3's converse). Rule: [handoff], its person-to-person face.
2. *Answer the handoff in the open.* Collects the debt: the answer in the standing report's typed vocabulary, visible where the handoff object lives — because after a transfer each side can believe the other is watching (handoff's own resulting clause). Rule: [status-feedback], its typed-answer shape.

## The research gate

`PARA/Projects/pattern playground/research/working-together/` — query.yml, a literature note, a practice survey, and a quarry read, all 2026-08-29. Beyond the three spine reshapings above, what the gate settled: visibility → awareness → accountability is a consequence chain from one design act, not three steps, and accountability must be separately earned digitally (Erickson & Kellogg) — which put translucence into the visibility region's resulting context rather than adding steps; synchrony is a rate set by the coupling of the work, not a split (Olson & Olson; mixed-focus collaboration; batch sizes over one substrate), so the sequence does not fork on it and steps state how they thin across tempo; the sole irresolvable merge case is a concurrent update to the same property (Kleppmann et al.), which the reconciliation seed carries as substrate fact; and the practice survey's handoff finding (pointer + responsibility + subscription, answered in typed states) became the E region's shape.

## The read-aloud walk

The test from Book 2 ch. 11 §5: read aloud, the design should form stepwise, no step contradicting what previous steps built.

> The relationships are mapped. First the grant: named others let into private material at graded tiers, each tier a promise; and with it the declaration of what the grant shows — a boundary between working and showing, blurred or distinct, per kind of material. Then the visibility the grant made owing: the living presence worn in graded marks and every written contribution signed, both as by-products of the work; and the exchange given a view that travels as one thing. Then contribution in place: the anchored mark that never interrupts, the discussion beside particular places with its own resolution, the recurring genres typed onto a shared vocabulary — with whatever must reach a colleague now leaving the material for their budget. Then the parting: divergence permitted rather than smuggled, its return reviewed as a change, its surviving collisions decided by a person between visible versions. And the handoff at its own rate: responsibility passed as a typed object and answered in the open, so neither side has to believe the other is watching.

Verdict: passes. Three deliberate debts inside the walk: the grant's tiers are promises the contribution region pays (recorded at the grant, collected across C); the parting owes a return surface (recorded at D1, collected at D2–D3); the handoff owes the sender an answer (recorded at E1, collected at E2). The two weak steps are absences marked, not contradictions.

## Test walks

Recorded per ch. 11 §7: a sequence fails where a later step forces undoing an earlier step's product.

*Walk 1 — the issue tracker (continuing the prior sittings' walk 1; embedded posture).* A1: workspace membership as the container grant; per-project tiers — admin, member, guest for the client, the guest capped at viewing the roadmap. A2: the boundary declared per material — issues live (edits visible on save), code reviewed (crosses only by merged PR), the roadmap published snapshots. B1: presence thin on issues — the avatar stack on an open issue, a typing indicator in the thread; the trail carries the rest (every field change attributed in the issue's feed); no cursors, the tempo doesn't need them. Contributions signed throughout; the triage bot's changes signed as the bot's — the reliance call taken when its summaries are read. B2: a filtered board handed as its address; the address carries filters and grouping, not the recipient's credentials. C1: skipped on issues — the issue is span-sized, the thread anchors to the whole (the annotation step's skip condition, found here). C1 on code: line comments — the mark in place. C2: threads with resolve; a thread assigned becomes E-region machinery. C3: workflow states as the typed vocabulary; the review's conclusion typed approve / request changes. D1: the draft PR — public to the repository, unmergeable, soliciting nobody until marked ready. D2: the review at per-hunk grain. D3: the merge button deactivates on collision; both versions shown; the unpicked hunk recoverable from the branch. E1: assignment — pointer, responsibility, subscription into the assignee's inbox. E2: the assignee's answer typed: done, or the issue's state moved. *Backtrack found:* the first draft placed the hand-on as the parting region's final step, as if responsibility crossed only when material forked. Assignment broke it: the tracker's primary collaborative act passes responsibility constantly, with no fork anywhere in sight — reaching the hand-on meant walking three fork steps that had nothing to do with it. The handoff broke out into its own sub-sequence at its own rate (finding 2).

*Walk 2 — the research assistant (continuing the prior sittings' walk 2; conversation-first).* A1: the project shared with the advisor as commenter, a co-author as editor; the agent already inside at a tier the delegation walk granted. A2: the notes live between co-authors; the draft reviewed — the advisor sees published revisions, not the rough middle. B1: between co-authors the living grades — cursors in the shared notes; toward the advisor only the trail. The agent's contributions signed as generated; reading them takes the citation-marked path (the call into calibrating reliance). B2: "look at section 3" travels as an address with the framing attached, not as a recipe. C1: the advisor highlights a passage; C2 comments on it; the researcher resolves. C3: revision states typed — draft, under review, addressed. D1: the researcher parts a restructuring as a revision; the co-author keeps editing the live notes meanwhile. D2: the return reviewed per change, suggestions accepted singly. D3: both touched the abstract offline; the collision shows both paragraphs; the unpicked one recovered once when the pick was wrong. E1: "your turn" — the draft handed to the co-author with the responsibility named; E2 the answer typed back. *Backtrack found:* the first draft opened the parting region with the working/showing declaration ("decide whether to permit parting"), keyed once per product. The walk broke at B1: the presence step kept promising living cursors on a ground nobody had declared live, and the declaration turned out to differ per material inside one product — notes live, draft reviewed — exactly as walk 1's issues/code split had hinted. The declaration moved forward into the grant region, per kind of material (finding 3).

*On the transfer question:* the walks guided at the joints again — which promises precede which machinery, and where each product's boundary sits per material. The contribution region transfers most directly; its three steps read as a checklist. The posture flip recurred in a new form: the conversation-first product's human↔human face runs beside its human↔agent one, and the same visibility region serves both once the machine contributor is signed.

## Findings — corpus changes the authoring forces

Recorded for their own sittings; the seeds excepted, none executed here.

1. *The spine found, and the map entry's chain demoted.* "Current extent: annotation → commenting → status-feedback" read the contribution channel as the core; the authoring resolved the territory into five regions at four rates — grant, visibility, contribution, parting, handoff — with the old chain as the contribution region's middle. The entry's member list corrects: shareability is a quality, not a step (it names the bar the shared view clears); the grant was patternless and is now seeded.
2. *The handoff is its own region.* Walk 1's backtrack: responsibility crosses with or without any fork — assignment is the plainest case. Passing work on stands at its own rate beside parting and returning, not inside it.
3. *The working/showing boundary is declared per kind of material.* Walk 2's backtrack, corroborated by walk 1: one product holds live and reviewed grounds side by side (issues and code; notes and draft). The declaration heads the walk, right after the grant — and it is patternless (finding 4).
4. *Three patternless stations marked from home territory.* The working/showing declaration (G7 grounds it; every product surveyed has the boundary, none names it), the handable shared view (J2; follow/spotlight live, the address asynchronously — deep-linking supplies the address but nothing owns the handable view), and — from the gate rather than the walks — the access-request flow (the viewer asking for the higher tier; sharing's seed notes it unworked). Each is a classification-sitting candidate.
5. *Three seeds created in-sitting*: [sharing] (the graded grant; practice survey's strongest staging evidence), [presence] (the graded awareness layer; Gutwin & Greenberg's structure with the by-product principle and the translucence force as resulting clauses), [reconciliation] (the fork-boundary collision surface; the placement rule as content). Each carries its disclosure and awaits its classification sitting.
6. *change-review's initiating situation is too narrow.* It says "the system proposes changes", but its own resources line names the pre-AI lineage (version-control review, track changes) and this sequence invokes its person-to-person face as a step. Candidate widening: any proposer — the mode, not the author, is the pattern. The complements edge to draft-and-publish already carries the pairing.
7. *handoff's human-to-human case now has its shape.* The map listed it missing; the walk supplies it through the aspect invocation plus the practice finding (pointer, responsibility, subscription; the typed answer). Candidate enrichment for handoff's page: the typed-object shape, and the sender's owed visibility (J3) joining its accountability clause. Its serves on the delegation foundation is untouched — the person-to-person face widens the pattern, not the foundation claim.
8. *selection's deferred collaborative face has a home.* Its page defers "co-presence cursors, shared selection state" as deserving separate treatment; presence's cursor-and-selection grade is that treatment. The seeded complements edge types it; selection's To-do can point rather than defer when next edited.
9. *The by-product principle is a candidate resulting clause on presence — and a lens on status-feedback.* Awareness that must be authored goes unfed (Dourish & Bellotti; Grudin). The presence seed carries it; the standing report step survives it only because typing a state costs less than composing a message — J6's offloading is what keeps C3 on the right side of the disparity. Worth a clause on status-feedback when next edited.
10. *Six arrivals confirmed at their stations.* The transfer (governing autonomy) lands at E1; the origin mark (calibrating reliance) at B1's sign step; scheduled send (attention and tempo) at C3's seam; the directed thread (making sense at scale) at C3; the handed framing (making sense at scale) at B2; the save boundary (making changes) interleaves against D1. All stay owned by their outgoing files; no `from:` entries were added.
11. *provenance-marking's related edge to collaboration is a candidate upgrade.* Its note — "mixed human-machine authorship is where per-contribution origin matters most" — is now walked from both sides (this sequence's sign step; calibrating reliance's origin-mark hands-off). When provenance-marking's page is next edited, the person-to-person face (attribution among people, not only human-vs-machine) belongs in its body, and the bare related may deserve a stronger type.

## Revision to the step-length rules (2026-09-27)

The sequence was revised under the step-length rules in docs/specs/sequences.md §Authoring, in the plain wording of the governing-autonomy and delegating-work revisions. The file went from about 2,030 words to about 1,555. No step, rule, order, or connection changed, and sub-sequence ids are unchanged, so the read-aloud walk, the test walks, and every incoming `at` address still stand.

Where the removed text went:

- The presupposition carried the four rates and the design's main risk (people leaving the material for outside channels). Both hold across regions, so they moved into the lead. The lead no longer lists the regions.
- Pattern reasons and costs already on the pattern pages: grants cut too fine sending people to screenshots (sharing), threads that only grow without resolution (commenting), the placement rule (reconciliation), and the pre-AI lineage of reviewing changes (change-review). The translucence of the visibility layer, which the visibility sub-sequence's `resulting` stated, is the presence seed's resulting clause (finding 5).
- The "no pattern owns" remarks on the two weak steps are finding 4; the missing `rule` marks them.
- Every `resulting` except the last restated the next `initiating` and was removed.
- Two remarks were dropped without another home. The first is that a standard report form includes and excludes by design; it joins finding 9's lens on status-feedback. The second is that the reviewed changes returning from a parting meet the same approval step as every reviewed set of changes; no connection carries it, so it is a candidate `hands-off` from parting/2 to governing-autonomy via approval-gate, for the next sitting on this sequence.

The set report forms and the typed handoff are this sequence's own contributions, not yet on the status-feedback and handoff pages (finding 7), so those glosses keep their decisions in full.
