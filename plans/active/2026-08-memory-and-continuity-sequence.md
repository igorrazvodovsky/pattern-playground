# Memory and continuity — the tenth sequence

Phase 5 of [the sequence-map plan](2026-08-sequence-map.md): the ninth sequence authored after the pilot, and the first of the map's four missing sequences — the ones whose spines had to be found rather than read off the `precedes` graph. The sequence's authoritative home is `apps/patterns/src/content/sequences/memory-and-continuity.mdx`; this document is the authoring trace — the sequence as first written, the read-aloud walk, the test walks with their backtracks, and the corpus changes the authoring surfaced. The corpus changes are recorded here, not executed — each is judgment work for its own sitting.

This territory was picked as the ripest of the remaining five: six authored connections already land in it, more than any other unauthored territory — the work record (delegating work), the governance record (governing autonomy), the reliance record (calibrating reliance), undo's session limit (making changes), the endings' remains (the encounter), and the newcomer's archaeology (learning) — and four authored readings of activity-log pin the substrate from outside. The territory's exposed move is the catch-up an actor needs on returning to an artefact after an absence, which no pattern owns. A practice pass confirmed the walk is already shipped in pieces: continue-watching rows and welcome-back prompts for the restored place, [Slack's Catch Up and channel recaps](https://slack.com/help/articles/25076892548883-Guide-to-AI-features-in-Slack) and [Gemini's Catch Me Up over Drive files](https://www.techradar.com/pro/googles-new-gemini-catch-me-up-tool-will-tell-you-if-anyone-has-been-editing-your-precious-work-files) for the delta, and the three big assistants' [memory controls](https://openai.com/index/memory-and-new-controls-for-chatgpt/) dividing exactly at the keep-by-default judgement selective-memory names.

No connection-ownership transfers were due: no authored file holds a `from: memory-and-continuity` entry, and the six incoming connections stay on their outgoing files with their plain `to: memory-and-continuity` addresses unrefined, following the precedent set when learning was authored.

Steps are decisions, written as verbs, each invoking a pattern as its rule. Constituents ride inside steps in parentheses.

## Presupposition (the head)

A policy on what the collaboration may remember — what may be kept and for how long, who may read it, and what the actor has consented to — judged before any remembering is designed; the map's discharge audit already lists it as a legible gap, restated here as the sequence's own head. Two structural facts. The sequence is the receiving end of the language: the other walks write accounts, and this one keeps and returns them — their hands-offs arrive as remains, never as decisions to re-take. And its two halves recur at different rates: the keeping is decided once per product, while the return region is walked at every absence, long or short. Everything in the sequence is designed for a moment nobody is present — the remembering happens while the actor is away, the reading when they come back.

The sequence enacts *temporality* (absence, accumulation, and expiry made design material — the long gaps the other sequences step over) and *privacy* (what is held about a person made visible, scoped, and revocable).

## The sequence

### A. Deciding what is kept — the keeping

1. *Keep one record.* The accounts the other walks write — work, governance, reliance — accumulate into one addressable record with a shared anatomy (actor, action, object, time) rather than one log per feature; narrowability decided with the keeping, since histories only grow. Its readers include the arriving newcomer and the returning actor, who read the same record. Rule: [activity-log]. (Constituent: progressive-disclosure — entries expand on demand.)
2. *Capture the why while it exists.* Origin marked at the moment of writing — the hand, the named rule, the model run — on the record and on the material itself; the rationale captured at edit time or lost. The return region spends what this step writes. Rule: [provenance-marking], its write-time capture.
3. *Choose what carries forward.* The second kind of remains: what the system holds about the actor to shape the next encounter — stated facts, standing preferences, and the carried state of reading marks, open places, and parked work (moved here by walk 1's backtrack; finding 2). Defaults do most of the governing; the grant visible, inspectable, editable; the no-trace session decided with it. Rule: [selective-memory]. (Constituent: settings — where the controls conventionally live.)

### B. Meeting the actor who comes back — the return

*Initiating situation.* An actor comes back after an absence — an hour or a season — to a collaboration that kept living or merely kept waiting. Every other walk assumed presence; each step here reads what the keeping region wrote.

1. *Restore the actor's place.* The parked exchange, the deferred commitment, the half-made change, the marks saying what was already seen. The decision is the re-entry behaviour — restore or offer to — and the confirming of assumptions that aged while nobody watched; a fresh start stays reachable. The weak form: no pattern owns the move, though products ship it everywhere (finding 3). (Constituent: deep-linking — the kept place is an address.)
2. *Show what moved in absence.* The catch-up: pulled at return, scoped to the material at hand, nothing to accept or reject; reads the marks and the record's whos and whys; takes only what could wait — the pushed remainder is attention and tempo's. A generated catch-up is an AI surface and carries the evidence marks (the call into calibrating reliance). The weak form: nothing in the corpus owns the catch-up (finding 3).
3. *Open the road to named states.* Undo ends at the session; past it, recovery runs on named states — which moments get names, who names them, how far back the road reaches, the retention policy made concrete. Restoring a state is itself a change and re-enters making changes' safety pass. The weak form: version history, named from undo's and saving's margins, never authored (finding 3).

### C. Making the bounds real — the forgetting

*Initiating situation.* Kept-by-default accumulates; the presupposed policy set bounds, and something must make them real. Forgetting is a feature with an interface cost the invisible version never paid.

1. *Forget in both channels* — an unordered cluster; the policy's retention judgement lands in two channels that forget differently, and the third channel (the record's own expiry) has no owner (finding 9):
   - *Make forgetting a feature.* Per fact, per session, per mode — and honoured across the channel boundary: an erased fact must not resurface as learned behaviour (walk 2's catch; finding 7). Rule: [selective-memory], its forgetting face.
   - *Decide what removal leaves behind.* Soft-delete window, tombstone, the record's entry for the deletion; removing and expunging are different promises, and the record shows which was kept. Rule: [deletion], what remains after removal.

## The read-aloud walk

The test from Book 2 ch. 11 §5: read aloud, the design should form stepwise, no step contradicting what previous steps built.

> A policy says what may be remembered, and the other walks are already writing. First the keeping: their accounts gathered into one record with a shared anatomy, kept narrowable because histories only grow; origin and rationale captured at the moment of writing, because afterwards is too late; then the held-forward memory — facts, preferences, reading marks, parked places — with the defaults doing most of the governing and the grant visible, editable, escapable. Then the return, reading what the keeping wrote: the actor's place restored with its aged assumptions confirmed rather than trusted; what moved in their absence pulled up on request, scoped and attributed, with nothing to accept; and past undo's reach, a road back through named states as deep as the policy allows. Last the forgetting, making the policy's bounds real in both channels: the memory erased so it stays erased, even as behaviour; the material removed with its promised remains and no others — while the record's own expiry stands marked as the channel nobody owns.

Verdict: passes. One deliberate debt inside the walk: the keeping region's write-time captures are spent by the return region's readings — recorded at creation, collected two steps later. The three weak steps are absences marked, not contradictions.

## Test walks

Recorded per ch. 11 §7: a sequence fails where a later step forces undoing an earlier step's product.

*Walk 1 — the issue tracker (continuing the prior sittings' walk 1; embedded posture).* A1: one timeline per issue and per workspace — human edits, the overnight rule set's actions, approvals, overrides — narrowable by actor, kind, and time; the governance and reliance accounts land in it rather than in per-feature logs; the joiner's archaeology (learning's call) and the returning triager read the same record. A2: agent actions marked with the rule that made them and the run that applied it; the rule's rationale captured when it was set — which is what the learning walk's decision echo later reads. A3: what carries forward per actor — pinned projects, saved filters, notification preferences, and the last-read marks on every thread; an admin can see what the workspace holds about them. B1: two weeks away; the tracker reopens the pinned view and the issue that was mid-edit, and asks before re-applying a stale draft. B2: "since you were away" per view — which issues moved, who or what moved them, the rule set's batch actions compressed into one attributed line; pulled, never pushed. B3: an issue description reverted to the state named at the release cut; the revert lands as an ordinary graded edit. C: a contractor leaves — their held-forward state is erased while their *actions* stay in the record, the two channels split exactly at the policy's line; deleting a test project leaves tombstones in the record, expunging personal data does not. *Backtrack found:* the first draft kept the high-water mark — what the actor had already seen — inside the return region, as the catch-up's own bookkeeping. The walk showed it is written during presence, per surface, while the actor works: it is kept state, not return behaviour. The mark moved into the keeping region as part of what carries forward (A3), and the return steps became pure readers of it (finding 2).

*Walk 2 — the research assistant (continuing the prior sittings' walk 2; conversation-first).* A1: conversations, pipeline runs, and imports in one record the researcher can question conversationally — narrowing by asking. A2: the survey's generated spans marked as generated at the moment they land (the mark must live on the material, not only in the record, or export strips it); the dedup pipeline's decisions carry their why from run time. A3: the assistant's memory — domain, preferences, standing instructions — held as a visible, editable summary; the sensitive grant application runs in a no-trace session; the keep-by-default judgement is exactly where the shipping assistants diverge. B1: a month away; the parked thread resumes through an opening, and the assistant confirms aged assumptions first — the corpus grew, the thresholds may not hold. B2: "while you were away": forty new matches, twelve filed duplicates — a generated summary whose claims cite the changes they compress, the call into calibrating reliance taken. B3: the survey's named states are the advisor submissions; restoring one is an edit like any other. C: "forget that affiliation" — and the catch: erasing the fact is not enough. *Backtrack found:* the first draft treated forgetting as complete within the memory channel. The walk showed the forgotten affiliation resurfacing as behaviour — phrasing tuned by months of use — so erasure has to cross into the persistence channel delegating work's tail owns, or it is theatre. The cluster gloss took the obligation; the step survived (finding 7).

*On the transfer question:* the walks guided at the seam between the regions — what must be written during presence for the return to have anything to read — more than in fine-grained order. The sequence's distinctive contribution is that seam: five other walks route remains here, and this is where the remains become something an actor can come back to.

## Findings — corpus changes the authoring forces

Recorded for their own sittings; none executed here.

1. *The spine found.* The map entry listed members with "nothing sequences them"; the authoring resolved the territory into three regions — the keeping (write during presence), the return (read at absence's end), the forgetting (bound both) — with activity-log promoted from listed substrate to the home spine's head step. What the step adds beyond the pattern is the unification decision: one record with a shared anatomy rather than a log per feature.
2. *Reading marks are memory.* Walk 1's backtrack: the high-water mark, open places, and parked work are carried-forward facts about the actor, written during presence — they belong to selective-memory's territory, not to the return steps that read them. Candidate body addition to selective-memory, whose to-do already asks whether memory here and the delegation foundation's context handling are one territory; the tracker's kept filters and the assistant's remembered domain read as one move at two formalities.
3. *Three patternless stations marked from home territory.* Resumption (restore the place — continue-watching rows, welcome-back prompts, session restore as shipping forms; a new sighting), the catch-up move (second sighting; Slack Catch Up and Gemini Catch Me Up as shipping forms), and version history (undo's and saving's to-dos both name it; making-changes's hands-off note already expects "named versions"). Each is a classification-sitting candidate; the catch-up's has a consumer step waiting at return/2.
4. *Two steps rest on work not yet done.* keeping/2 leans on an unexecuted fold — the why-recorded-at-edit-time obligation into provenance-marking — and return/2 consumes a catch-up move no pattern owns. The sequence is coherent without either.
5. *provenance-marking becomes a shared station.* Calibrating reliance spends the mark at the point of reading; this spine writes it at the point of writing. Second authored spine — and a candidate structure for the pattern page: a read face and a write face, with the write face carrying the deadline argument (origin and rationale cannot be reconstructed later).
6. *activity-log confirmed from its home spine, with a fifth reading.* The four authored readings (work, governance, reliance, arrival) land on one kept record, and the returning actor's catch-up adds a fifth — the map's own prediction that the arriving actor and the returning actor read the same record held on the walk.
7. *Erasure crosses channels or it is theatre.* An erased fact must not survive as tuned behaviour. Candidate resulting clause on selective-memory, or a note on its `complements` edge to standing-instructions — the edge already says the two are "channels of the same persistence" and now has the failure case that proves it.
8. *deletion joins the shared stations.* The safety pass grades it (making changes), and the forgetting cluster spends its remains face; the "what remains after removal" reading was latent in its recovery and soft-delete sections and could be lifted to a named aspect on the page.
9. *The record's own expiry is unowned.* activity-log's "histories only grow" names the force; no pattern owns log retention, redaction, or the record outliving its subjects — a gap smaller than the three stations, recorded beside them. Related residue: settings' constituent ride at keeping/3 does not claim its three make-time edges (settings → undo, → status-feedback, → notification); the map's "unplaced" note stands.

## Remembered state and the return step (2026-11-04)

The [remembered-state] page, written after the sequence, gives `return/1` ("Restore the actor's place") a candidate rule. The step restored four things that belong to three moves: the arrangement and place (remembered state), the unfinished change ([resuming], added 2026-10-11), and the conversation set aside with its deferred commitment (already the hands-off to the encounter via user-opening). One invocation per step makes that two steps with rules and a hands-off.

Remembered state and AI memory are treated as different moves: remembered state brings the actor back to where they were; selective memory carries facts forward to shape what is generated next. That revises finding 2, which had put open places and reading marks under selective memory.

Changes to the sequence file:

- `keeping/3` narrows to selective memory's own scope: stated facts and standing preferences that shape what is generated. Reading marks, open places and set-aside work leave its gloss.
- New `keeping/4`, "Keep the arrangement the actor leaves", rule remembered-state, aspect "what is kept and for whom". It names *the arrangement*: each view's arrangement, the place reached (including how far the actor had read), and the values last chosen. It follows `keeping/3` rather than forming a cluster with it, because the session that keeps nothing, decided at `keeping/3`, also stops the arrangement from being written.
- `return/1` becomes the cluster "Put the actor back where they were", with two members: "Restore the arrangement" (remembered-state, aspect "restoring", deep-linking kept as constituent with the address-wins note) and "Bring back unfinished work" (resuming, carrying the check of out-of-date assumptions). The set-aside conversation and deferred commitment stay in the cluster gloss, routed through the existing hands-off.
- `return/2`'s gloss now reads the point the arrangement records instead of "the actor's last visit", so the write/read seam from walk 1 holds.

A cluster takes one number, so `return/1`, `return/2` and `return/3` keep their addresses. The one incoming address at `return/1` (structuring the space, `layout/2`, step-by-step progress kept across a stop) now lands on the cluster whose resuming member owns it. `keeping/3`'s outgoing hands-off to delegating work still fits the narrowed step.

Step-list test: the step lines, with the new step and the cluster and its two members, read as a walk without their glosses.

Read-aloud test:

> A policy says what may be remembered. First the keeping: one record of what the other sequences write; the origin and reason of each change recorded when it is made; what the system keeps about the actor to shape what it generates, with a default most actors never change and a session that keeps nothing; and the arrangement the actor leaves, kept on the device or for the person, and not written in that session. Then the return: the actor put back where they were, the arrangement restored or offered depending on the gap, and unfinished work brought back with its out-of-date assumptions checked; what changed past the point the arrangement records, shown without asking for a decision; and named versions beyond undo. Last the forgetting, in both kinds of kept information.

Verdict: passes. No step contradicts an earlier one. The read-aloud walk shows a gap that walk 1 confirms (new finding 11).
