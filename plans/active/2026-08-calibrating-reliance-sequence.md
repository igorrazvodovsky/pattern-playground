# Calibrating reliance — the seventh sequence

Phase 5 of [the sequence-map plan](2026-08-sequence-map.md): the sixth sequence authored after the pilot. The sequence's authoritative home is `apps/patterns/src/content/sequences/calibrating-reliance.mdx`; this document is the authoring trace — the sequence as first written, the read-aloud walk, the test walks with their backtracks, and the corpus changes the authoring surfaced. The corpus changes are recorded here, not executed — each is judgment work for its own sitting.

This territory was picked as the ripest of the queue: five authored connections already land in it — citation receives arrivals from composing views' cross-references, the encounter's evidence, and delegating work's named ground, and cognitive-forcing-functions receives calls from delegating work's reasoning trail and governing autonomy's gate — so the sequence's two receiving stations were fixed by other sittings before this one began. It also resolves the map's remaining orphan: provenance-marking.

The graph's evidence is the corpus's most convergent `sets-up` cluster: five patterns — expressed-uncertainty, transparent-reasoning, citation, explanation, and (untyped) provenance-marking — converge on cognitive-forcing-functions, and one edge leaves it for activity-log. The convergence is itself the sequence's argument: every one of the five resulting clauses says the same thing from a different side — the evidence that makes scrutiny possible also makes deferral smoother — which is why the friction sub-sequence exists.

Steps are decisions, written as verbs, each invoking a pattern as its rule. Constituents ride inside steps in parentheses.

No connection-ownership transfers were needed this sitting: all five incoming arrivals already live as outgoing entries on their source files, per the schema rule.

## Presupposition (the head)

An AI surface is in use and its output will be acted on. The stakes of acting on wrong output have been judged per surface — the judgement that sets how much calibration machinery each surface carries. Nothing in the corpus discharges that judgement; it is process-register and settled upstream, and the map's discharge audit already lists it as a legible gap. Unlike the other authored sequences, this one is invoked per surface rather than once per product: it is the cross-cutting sequence the map predicted, and per-surface invocation turned out to be its main structural fact (see the transfer note).

The sequence enacts *agency* throughout (the actor keeps the judgement that fluent output would otherwise take) and *learnability* at the friction (the withholding step is the one forcing function that builds competence rather than merely protecting it).

## The sequence

### A. Making the output checkable — the head

1. *Mark what made it.* Origin decided first because it is not neutral — the moment anything is marked, the unmarked becomes a claim of human origin. Optional: the surface that wears its origin (a conversation is unmistakably the machine's turn) skips the mark; the decision is really made where output travels into mixed company, where the mark must survive copying. Rule: [provenance-marking]. The map's orphan placed (finding 2).
2. *Put the evidence on the claims* — an unordered cluster; three marks answering three questions, each spent where its question is the one the actor would ask:
   - *Ground the claims.* Pointers resolving at the claim to the passage behind it; which claims need one is a policy, and a claim with nothing to point at says so. Rule: [citation]. (Constituent: link-preview — the source weighed without leaving the claim.)
   - *Let the confidence show.* Marks on the doubtful spans so scrutiny lands where needed; the value is in the difference between marked and unmarked. Rule: [expressed-uncertainty].
   - *Expose the path.* The trail where the work took steps. Optional: the single-move output has no trail to show (walk 1's backtrack; finding 3). Rule: [transparent-reasoning].
3. *Answer why on demand.* Deliberately last: a convincing explanation lowers the chance the actor opens the sources beside it, so the actor meets the evidence before the account of it — the one order both walks kept confirming. Rule: [explanation], aspect: its AI explanations.

### B. Interposing the judgement

*Initiating situation.* The evidence rides the output and is mostly unread: the marks reassure whether or not anyone follows them, and accepting still costs less than evaluating. The actor has the competence to judge and stakes that warrant judging, but nothing asks them to.

1. *Make the acceptance cost a thought.* A moment of active cognition between output and acceptance — wait, reveal on demand, commit first, write down why — spent only where the presupposed stakes keep justifying it. The system's two authored calls (the settled-into trail, the habitual approval) land here. The written reason is a debt the account collects. Rule: [cognitive-forcing-functions].
2. *Sharpen the friction* — an unordered pair of optional steps at two timescales:
   - *Structure what the review checks.* Where failure modes can be named in advance; each item points somewhere new or becomes a box to clear. Rule: [overreliance-checklist].
   - *Withhold the answer sometimes.* Where the actor's own competence is what the surface was meant to support; periodic, and the value is in the comparison. Skip: the one-off task has no skill loop (walk 2). Rule: [human-goes-first].

### C. Reading the reliance back

*Initiating situation.* Acceptances, overrides, opened sources, and cleared checks have accumulated. Whether trust is tracking accuracy is being decided on impressions, unless something better is kept.

1. *Keep the record of the judgements.* Acceptances, overrides, opened sources, recorded reasons — the reliance evidence no other record in the system holds. Rule: [activity-log]. Fourth authored spine for the pattern (finding 6).
2. *Move the friction on evidence.* The unread acceptance earns more friction; the check cleared in one motion is a mismatch, moved or withdrawn explicitly. The recurring override leaves as tuning evidence. Rule: [cognitive-forcing-functions], aspect: where it is spent. The reliance-side counterpart of governing autonomy's regrant (finding 7).

## The read-aloud walk

The test from Book 2 ch. 11 §5: read aloud, the design should form stepwise, no step contradicting what previous steps built.

> The surface is in use and the stakes of acting on wrong output are judged. First the origin, wherever the output travels into mixed company — because the moment anything is marked, the unmarked becomes a claim. Then the evidence on the claims: pointers that resolve to passages, confidence let show on the doubtful spans, the trail wherever the work took steps — each spent where its question is the one the actor would ask. The why comes last and on demand, after the evidence it would otherwise talk the actor out of opening. At acceptance, where the marks reassure more than they get read, the yes is made to cost a thought — sized to the stakes, sharpened into a checklist where the failure modes have names, and sometimes inverted so the actor answers first. And the account: every acceptance, override, and opened source in the record, and the friction moved on what the record shows — more where the yeses go through unread, less where the check is cleared in one motion, with the recurring override leaving as tuning evidence.

Verdict: passes. Two deliberate debts inside the walk: the evidence sub-sequence creates the anchoring debt (every mark raises trust on sight) and the acceptance collects it; the friction's written reasons are a debt the account collects. Obligations carried forward, not contradictions.

## Test walks

Recorded per ch. 11 §7: a sequence fails where a later step forces undoing an earlier step's product.

*Walk 1 — the issue tracker's overnight triage agent (continuing the prior sittings' walk 1; embedded posture, the standing run; the surface walked is the morning delta queue).* A1: the agent's labels and comments sit in the same issue histories as human ones — mixed threads, so the mark is taken; the queue itself is unmistakably the agent's and would have skipped it. A2: each stale-marking carries its ground as pointers ("no activity since March; duplicate of #482"); the doubtful duplicate matches carry a confidence mark, so morning review lands on them first; the label suggestion is a single move with no trail — the trail step skips (backtrack, below). A3: "why was this marked stale?" opens the policy factors, after the pointers. B1: the doubtful-marked items are excluded from accept-all and each takes an individual reveal-and-confirm; the routine label changes keep the clear path. B2: release week's proposed closings get a checklist (referenced by an open PR? security label? external reporter waiting?); every few weeks the maintainer triages ten issues unaided before seeing the agent's proposals, and the mismatches teach both ways. C1: the record shows a month of accept-alls with no opened grounding on label changes, and every stale-marking on issues with linked-PR activity overridden. C2: friction is withdrawn from labels (cheap to reverse; the stakes judgement says so) and kept on closings; the recurring stale-override leaves as tuning evidence and the rule is tuned — delegating work's tail. *Backtrack found:* the first draft required the reasoning trail on every surface, following the map's flat list. The label chip is one step — there is no trail to show, and forcing one manufactures narration. The step was rewritten as optional with the single-move output as its skip condition (finding 3).

*Walk 2 — the research assistant's literature survey (continuing the prior sittings' walk 2; conversation-first, the one-off run; the surface walked is the survey report).* A1: in the conversation the origin is worn and the mark is skipped; when the report is exported to the team wiki the decision is live — origin metadata that survives the export, per-section where the actor edited the machine's draft. A2: the survey's claims resolve to cited spans in the archives; the sections retrieval could not ground carry hedges rather than decoration; the search trail (which archives, which queries, what was excluded) is multi-step and shown. A3: "why is this paper in?" on demand — and the inclusion rationale is met after the sources, because a convincing rationale is exactly what stops the opening of them. B1: at the moment of sending the report out (the act governing autonomy gated), the send surfaces the three flagged claims nobody opened before the yes. B2: the survey checklist has nameable failure modes (key venues covered, date range, retracted papers) and is taken; the withholding step is skipped — a one-off task with no skill loop, the skip condition exercised. C1: across sessions the record shows sources always opened on the findings table and never on background sections. C2: citation density and friction are re-spent accordingly; the recurring "stop including preprints" override escalates to a standing instruction — tuning evidence out. *Backtrack found:* the first draft took the origin mark as a required first step on every surface, and the conversation surface gave it nothing to decide — the turn already wears its origin. The step was rewritten as optional, with the decision relocated to where the output travels (finding 4).

*On the transfer question:* per-surface invocation guided more than fine order — which marks a surface carries is the sequence's real decision, and the stakes judgement does most of the selecting. The evidence cluster held unordered across both walks; the one order both walks confirmed was evidence-before-explanation. The joints were the same joints the prior sittings found from their sides: the acceptance step is where two sequences already call, and the account's evidence is what governing autonomy's gate spends.

## Findings — corpus changes the authoring forces

Recorded for their own sittings; none executed here.

1. *The map's chain resolved into three regions with an authored partial order over the evidence.* The comma list — five evidence patterns → cognitive-forcing-functions → activity-log — became: origin first, three claim-level marks unordered, explanation deliberately last, then the friction, then the account. The explanation-last order is grounded in citation's staying-vs-leaving force (Kim et al., CHI 2025: explanations reduce source-checking) and is stored in the sequence; a candidate `precedes` edge citation → explanation (decide the checkable marks before the persuasive account) would type it, and the pair's existing `complements` note already carries the reason.
2. *provenance-marking placed — the map's orphan resolved, first authored spine, while still a seed.* Spine membership raises the priority of its maturing To-do (C2PA and disclosure-policy grounding; the unlabelled-becomes-a-claim force is now walked). expressed-uncertainty is likewise a seed in a spine; its calibration-literature To-do rises the same way.
3. *transparent-reasoning is optional with the single-move output as its skip condition.* Walk 1's backtrack. The pattern's page nowhere states the no-trail case — its temporal modes all assume multi-step work. Candidate: a line in its initiating situation or a forms note, for whichever sitting executes its To-dos.
4. *provenance-marking is optional with "the surface wears its origin" as its skip condition, and the decision lives at the travel boundary.* Walk 2's backtrack. The pattern's travel force (marks stripped by copy, paste, export) already carries the substance; no page change forced, the map entry corrected.
5. *The forcing-function family split cleanly.* The two members that are pattern nodes (overreliance-checklist, human-goes-first) ride as an unordered pair of optional steps; the rest of the family (wait, on-demand reveal, judgement-first, partial explanation, rationale logging) stay dimensions of the family page, per the one-invocation-per-step bar. Rationale logging's bridge to the record is already typed — the family's `sets-up: activity-log` clause is exactly it.
6. *Shared stations.* cognitive-forcing-functions is confirmed from its home spine: both authored calls (delegating work's watch/3, governing autonomy's gate/3) land at acceptance/1. activity-log is confirmed from a fourth authored spine. citation is a new shared-station entry: three authored arrivals (composing views' cross-references, the encounter's evidence call, delegating work's named ground) all land at evidence/2 — add it to the map's shared-station list.
7. *The friction re-spend is the reliance-side counterpart of the regrant.* Governing autonomy's account widens authority on the record's clean runs; this account moves scrutiny on the record's unread yeses — the same record-driven re-decision at a third altitude beside ai-tuning's (behaviour), bounded-autonomy's (authority), and now cognitive-forcing-functions' (scrutiny). No typed edge carries any of the three; the pattern is worth a vocabulary note if a fourth appears.
8. *The recurring override as tuning evidence has no typed form.* The hands-off from account/2 to delegating work's tail is authored at the sequence layer; nothing connects activity-log or cognitive-forcing-functions to ai-tuning at the pattern layer. Candidate: a resulting clause on activity-log (the record as the evidence tuning acts on), for the sitting that executes ai-tuning's or activity-log's To-dos.
9. *The skill-maintenance gap, second sighting.* learning's mastery rung says nothing about maintaining a skill against delegation; governing autonomy reached the gap through the takeback's call, and this sitting reaches it through the withholding step. Two authored sightings raise the priority of the learning-sequence groundwork the map already queues.
