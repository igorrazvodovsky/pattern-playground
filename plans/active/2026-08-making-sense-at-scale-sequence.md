# Making sense at scale — the twelfth sequence

Phase 5 of [the sequence-map plan](2026-08-sequence-map.md): the eleventh sequence authored after the pilot, and the last of the map's latent ten. The sequence's authoritative home is `apps/patterns/src/content/sequences/making-sense-at-scale.mdx`; this document is the authoring trace — the sequence as written, the research gate, the read-aloud walk, the test walks with their backtracks, and the corpus changes the authoring surfaced. Per the authoring bar the corpus changes are recorded, not executed — with one exception taken at the author's direction: two seed pages were created in-sitting (finding 3).

The territory was the map's thinnest latent entry: three fragments with no order among them — the evaluation group with no `precedes` edges at all, the annotation → commenting and tag → grouping externalising fragments, and the curation chain data-view → purpose-keyed-view → problem-curated-view. One authored connection already lands here (structuring the space's hub-and-spoke → purpose-keyed-view, the reporting-surface entry), and the map carried an open question: one sequence, or a split into sense-making and understanding-at-scale.

This was the second sitting to run its own research gate before locking the spine (after attention and tempo): a literature note (arxiv retrieval plus named canon; Cockburn full-text read), a practice survey kept deliberately generic (photo libraries, file managers, mail, e-readers — no specialised analyst tooling, per the author's framing that the corpus documents what most tools share), and a *Working Through Screens* quarry read (bands F and I, plus H4/J5). Four findings locked the spine before authoring: foraging and framing are one interleaved loop, so the territory is one sequence whose walk orders provision, not use; the lens group joins as one choice point per surface, not steps — the graph's missing edges were telling the truth; marking rides the encounter as one-gesture, flat-vocabulary externalising with schematising deliberately late; and generation compresses the gathering rather than adding a step, landing as a lens face that owes its ground.

No connection-ownership transfers were due: the one incoming authored connection (hub-and-spoke → purpose-keyed-view) stays on structuring-the-space as the outgoing side, per the schema rule.

Steps are decisions, written as verbs, each invoking a pattern as its rule. Constituents ride inside steps in parentheses.

## Presupposition (the head)

The map's own: accumulated material that has outgrown any single framing; still a legible gap, discharged nowhere. Three structural facts joined it at authoring. The walk orders provision, not use — viewing, marking, and keeping interleave freely once built, which is the gate's one-loop finding restated as a design fact, and the reason the map's split question closes as *one sequence*. It runs at three rates — the lens per surface, the marking standing at every encounter, the curation per recurring question. And it re-enters over its own products: marks accumulate into material (the e-reader's notebook of highlights), kept framings become things to make sense of, and generated output joins the pile it summarises.

The sequence enacts *density* (every lens is a density commitment), *formality* (the marking region is the quality's maturity principle walked), and *adaptation* (the curated view responds to the recurring purpose).

## The sequence

### A. Choosing the lens — per surface

1. *Choose how the point of attention and the whole share the screen* — a choice point, arms non-exclusive, no tree owner yet (finding 7). The mechanisms differ by what they spend: [overview-detail] spends space (spatial separation; the arm dissolves into composing views' splitting sub-sequence), [semantic-zoom] spends time (temporal separation, named levels — constituents: item-view's ladder, attribute-visibility's shed fields), [focus-and-context] spends fidelity (one continuous view, generated rungs at the rim), [text-lense] spends trust (prose levels as generated tellings; at collection scale, the generated overview of the pile — the reliance call rides this arm). Every arm inherits the overview obligation — open at the whole, detail on demand. The region is skipped where work is discrete items in an articulated process (the *Working Through Screens* F4 condition).
2. *Put the difference on screen.* Optional — skipped where questions are about items, not differences. Rule: [comparing], seeded this sitting (finding 3); juxtapose, superpose, or draw the difference explicitly; made at reading time, bases as editorial claim.

### B. Marking the encounter — standing, per encounter

*Initiating situation.* The material is being met, and every meeting produces judgements that die with the session unless something near-free catches them.

1. *Leave the mark where attention lands* — an unordered cluster, one contract: one gesture, at the point of encounter, private by default, vocabulary flat and grown along the way.
   - *Mark the reading in place.* The interpretive layer over content not the actor's to change; marks accumulate as wear. Rule: [annotation].
   - *Name what recurs.* Multi-membership labels, near-synonyms tolerated while the vocabulary moves; the value collected elsewhere as facets. Rule: [tag].
2. *Promote the structure that has emerged.* Deliberately late: merges, blessings, the recurring label turned field — offered, reversible, after evidence. Rule: [incremental-formalisation], seeded this sitting (finding 3).
3. *Direct the mark at its audience.* Optional — skipped where nobody else reads the material. The few marks worth answering are transformed for other readers (content and anchor re-rendered — Marshall & Brush's bar), and become answerable. Rule: [commenting].

### C. Keeping the framings — per recurring question

*Initiating situation.* Framings that answered keep being rebuilt by hand; composing views supplies the machinery, the marking region's vocabulary supplies facets; this region decides what is kept, for whom, with how much judgement.

1. *Keep the framing that answered.* The generic floor — saved search, smart folder, label-as-view — with three authorship poles; a shared framing hands on the organisation; pruning is part of the keep. Rule: [data-view], its saved-views dimension.
2. *Shape the view around the recurring purpose.* Glance, review, chase, each to its attention budget; the general view stays reachable; the system as a fourth authorship pole — the volunteered framing arrives, an attention event owing its ground. Rule: [purpose-keyed-view].
3. *Let rules carry the judgement.* Optional — skipped where the domain cannot state its problems in advance. Flags with reasons, noise budget, structural escape hatch. Rule: [problem-curated-view].

## The research gate

The gate produced a query.yml, the literature note, the generic practice survey, and the *Working Through Screens* quarry read, all 2026-08-29. Beyond the four spine-locking findings above, the gate contributed: the annotation → commenting edge grounded empirically (few personal marks are shared, and sharing is effortful transformation — Marshall & Brush); the curation region's purpose stated from evidence (the organisation transfers better than the content — distributed sensemaking); the mantra's true scope (Shneiderman's overview-first orders the actor's walk within one surface, so it lands as the choice point's obligation, not as edges among the lens patterns); and comparison flagged as an absent dimension (WTS F5), which became the sitting's first seed.

## The read-aloud walk

The test from Book 2 ch. 11 §5: read aloud, the design should form stepwise, no step contradicting what previous steps built.

> Material has outgrown any single framing. First the lens, per surface: one choice about how the point of attention and the whole share the screen — space, time, fidelity, or a generated telling that owes its ground — every arm opening at the whole; and where the question is a difference, the difference drawn as its own representation. Then the marking, standing: marks left in one gesture where attention lands, private by default, the vocabulary flat and growing; structure promoted deliberately late, offered and reversible; the few marks worth answering transformed and directed at their audience. Then the curation, per recurring question: the framing that answered kept as a named place; the view shaped around the purposes that return, the general view always reachable; and where the domain can say in advance what counts as a problem, the judgement written down as rules that show their reasons. What the walk built is itself material, and the next actor starts from it.

Verdict: passes. Two deliberate debts inside the walk: the marking cluster's grown vocabulary is spent two regions later (the curation's facets — recorded at creation, collected at C/1), and the choice point's generated arm creates the ground debt the calls-connection into calibrating reliance discharges. The one weak-adjacent position is comparing's optionality; no weak steps — every position found a rule, two of them seeded.

## Test walks

Recorded per ch. 11 §7: a sequence fails where a later step forces undoing an earlier step's product.

*Walk 1 — the issue tracker (continuing the prior sittings' walk 1; embedded posture).* A1: the backlog is discrete items in an articulated process, so the lens region is mostly the skip condition exercised — except the issue list itself, which takes the split-view arm and dissolves into composing views' overview-detail pairing, confirming the dissolution handoff. A2: comparing taken twice — two releases' scopes side by side, and the diff of two versions of a spec page. B1: the triager highlights repro steps in a long report (annotation) and grows labels ad hoc — needs-repro, regression, flaky (tag); near-synonyms bug/defect coexist for a while. B2: the recurring regression label is offered promotion to a field; bug/defect offered a merge; both accepted — the walk noted the offer arriving through suggestion's contract, matching the seed's related edge. B3: the highlight on the spec paragraph is rewritten as a comment addressed to its author — the transformation visible exactly as Marshall & Brush describe. C1: the triage queue kept as a named view (query + arrangement); a stale saved view whose query names a retired label gets pruned. C2: the team's triage dashboard — the reporting surface that structuring the space's hub-and-spoke handoff predicted — shaped for the glance; the general backlog view one action away. C3: rules flag stale issues and missing repro, each flag with its reason and a not-useful control. *Backtrack found:* the first draft placed comparing in the curation region, as a kept comparison view. The walk showed the comparison is made at reading time — the two releases are compared once, against whatever lens is up, and the product is not kept; what recurs is the question, not the framing. The step moved into the lens region as its optional second position (finding 4 keeps the deeper placement question open).

*Walk 2 — the research assistant (continuing the prior sittings' walk 2; conversation-first).* A1: the source pile is prose, so the reframed-telling arm carries the surface — per-source tellings at three granularities, and the generated overview of the whole pile with each claim resolving to its source; the reliance call taken per surface, as that sequence expects. The notes canvas takes the swap-with-scale arm (semantic zoom over cards). A2: comparing taken over many generated drafts of one section — the difference drawn explicitly, which is what made the pile of variants tractable. B1: highlights on sources; tags grown — method, contradicts-ours. B2: contradicts-ours recurs until the system offers it as a structured field the assistant fills on new sources; accepted. B3: the annotated source is shared with the advisor, the two marks worth discussing rewritten as comments. C1: unread-plus-method kept as a named view. C2: the standing project overview — generated, regenerated on return, its arrival scheduled — read against the attention budget of the return, its marks carried per the reliance call. C3: skipped — no consensus about what counts as a problem in open research; the skip condition exercised. *Backtrack found:* the first draft opened the lens region with a separate step, "generate the first framing", before the choice point — the generated overview as a stage of its own. The walk dissolved it: the overview is the reframed-telling arm at collection scale, chosen like any lens, with the ground debt its distinguishing cost — a lens with a debt, not a stage before the lenses. The step folded into the choice's fourth arm, independently confirming the gate's no-new-step finding.

*On the transfer question:* the walks guided at the joints once more — which region a product skips (the tracker's lens region, the assistant's rule step), and what must exist before what (facets before framings, marks before promotion). The loop behaviour showed up inside each walk — the assistant re-entered the lens over its own marks — as variation within one walk, never as pressure to split the sequence.

## Findings — corpus changes the authoring surfaces

Recorded for their own sittings; only finding 3's seeds were executed here, at the author's direction.

1. *The spine found: three regions at three rates.* Lens per surface, marking standing per encounter, curation per recurring question — with the map entry's member list corrected: the evaluation group joins as *one choice point*, not steps (the gate's Cockburn finding, confirmed by both walks), and grouping stays with composing views' framing cluster.
2. *The tag → grouping fragment reclassified.* Not internal spine: an applied label is a facet composing views' framing cluster narrows by — tag's `sets-up: filtering` clause is the typed form, and the hands-off at marking/1 now carries it. No new edge needed.
3. *Two seeds created in-sitting.* [comparing] (WTS F5; the LLM-output comparison; Gleicher's taxonomy) and [incremental-formalisation] (Shipman & Marshall; the move block-based-editor already names). An exception to the record-only rule, taken at the author's explicit direction; each awaits its classification sitting to harden past `seed: true`.
4. *Comparing's home cluster is open.* It reads equally as composing views' fourth framing-cluster member (beside sorting, grouping, filtering) and as this walk's lens-region step; walk 1 used it both ways. The seed's disclosure carries the question; deciding it is the classification sitting's job.
5. *incremental-formalisation's edges to type.* Block-based-editor's §heterogeneity mention is its named instance (candidate `instantiates` or `variants` from that page); tag's merge-capabilities line and formality's §Two strategies point at it; the seed authors `enacts: formality` and `complements: tag` already — the remaining edges belong to the classification sitting.
6. *text-lense enrichment candidate.* Its magnifying-lens face at collection scale is the generated overview of accumulated material (the grounded-summary shape: claims resolving to sources), and a resulting clause typing the reliance call (`sets-up: citation`) is the candidate form; the page currently ends at to-dos pointing the same direction.
7. *The lens choice lacks a tree owner.* The mechanism question — how are focus and context separated or blended, and what does each spend — is tree-shaped and evidenced (Cockburn), but no page owns it; the same homelessness as the collaboration-posture tree in the map's open questions. Candidate: a decision tree on one of the lens pages, with the choice point's `tree` field claiming it once it exists.
8. *annotation → commenting edge enrichment.* The existing sets-up clause is confirmed from its home spine; the candidate sharpening is Marshall & Brush's bar carried on the edge — few marks cross, and crossing is transformation work (content and anchors re-rendered), which walk 1 saw directly.
9. *The map's open question is answered: one sequence.* The gate's one-loop finding, confirmed by the walks — the loop shows up as re-entry within one walk, never as two separable activities; understanding-at-scale is the lens region of this sequence, not a sequence of its own.
10. *The reading-order placement.* Making sense at scale takes order 3 beside making changes — the two faces of material work, changing it and reading it — executed in the map's ordering paragraph this sitting as a map edit.
11. *The reporting-surface entry confirmed.* Structuring the space's hub-and-spoke → purpose-keyed-view handoff lands at curation/2, exactly where that sitting predicted; the connection stays owned by the outgoing file.
