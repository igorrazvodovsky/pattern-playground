# Assistance escalation — the eighth sequence

Phase 5 of [the sequence-map plan](2026-08-sequence-map.md): the seventh sequence authored after the pilot. The sequence's authoritative home is `apps/patterns/src/content/sequences/assistance-escalation.mdx`; this document is the authoring trace — the sequence as first written, the read-aloud walk, the test walks with their backtracks, and the corpus changes the authoring surfaced. The corpus changes are recorded here, not executed — each is judgment work for its own sitting.

This territory was picked as the ripest of the queue: it is the map's named bridge from the spatial half of the system to the AI half, two authored sequences already touch it at its ends — making changes hands off into its head (the good-defaults handoff at surface/3), and delegating work held its exit as a forward-referenced doorway (the workflow arrival at framing/1) — and its spine had the most `precedes` evidence of anything unauthored: good-defaults → {autofill, autocomplete, assisted-task-completion}, autocomplete → ai-completion, embedded-intelligence → suggestion, and three rungs draining into cognitive-forcing-functions. It also places the map's remaining named orphan, workflow.

One connection-ownership transfer was executed in the sitting, as the schema rule's mechanical consequence of authoring: delegating work's `from: assistance-escalation` entry at framing/1 moved to this sequence's file as the outgoing `hands-off` at climb/3, its note carried over.

Steps are decisions, written as verbs, each invoking a pattern as its rule. Constituents ride inside steps in parentheses.

## Presupposition (the head)

An actor is doing work the system can see, and there is evidence of typical use — what most actors need most of the time, judged from the task's shape, from general data, or from observed use. Every rung is a bet placed on that evidence; the assistance foundation frames the posture (the actor drives, the system intervenes selectively) but the evidence work itself is process-register, settled upstream, and discharged nowhere — the map's discharge audit already lists it as a legible gap, and this sequence turned out to mark the same gap from inside (the climb's weak step). The sequence's main structural fact is temporal: unlike the other authored sequences it is climbed per surface over a product's life rather than walked once — each rung is a standing decision revisited as evidence accumulates, which is why the climb region exists at all (see the transfer note).

The sequence enacts *agency* throughout (the actor keeps the final say at every rung — the offer contract is that guarantee made structural) and *adaptation* at the climb (the ladder moves on the actor's accepted-or-dismissed signal).

## The sequence

### A. Filling in what is known — the floor

1. *Set the static guess.* Fields arrive holding the best guess from the task's shape and general data; the guess doubles as an example of the expected answer. Which fields get one is the decision — and declining (identity, consent, the sensitive choice) is the decision too, because what is filled tends to stand. Rule: [good-defaults].
2. *Replace the guess with knowledge* — an unordered cluster; two ways the guess gives way to something known, answering different moments of the same field:
   - *Fill from the record.* Optional: where the system already holds the actor's own value with confidence enough that filling won't be wrong. Silent, and the silence has costs — it shows what the system knows, and the pre-filled value gets submitted unread. Rule: [autofill].
   - *Complete in flight.* Optional: where valid answers are many and the actor is still formulating. Candidates looked up, not produced — the rung's defining limit. Rule: [autocomplete].

### B. Joining the work — the offer

*Initiating situation.* Pre-population has run out: what is left is work the actor would do themselves, which the system could take part of if allowed to offer. Taking part means producing rather than retrieving, and producing runs on inferred intent — sometimes confidently wrong — so everything above the floor arrives as an offer or not at all.

1. *Read the intent in place.* The inference works inside the workflow on context the system already holds; the separate surface that asks the actor to restate is declined. Optional: where intent arrives stated — the conversational prompt — there is nothing to read (walk 2's backtrack; finding 3). Rule: [embedded-intelligence].
2. *Make everything it produces an offer.* The contract every higher rung inherits, settled once: accept in one gesture, declining cheaper than weighing, confidence carried. Creates the anchoring debt (the proposal shown before the actor formed their own idea) that the calibration call collects. Rule: [suggestion].
3. *Continue the composition.* The contract's inline form; adds silence at low confidence. Optional: skipped where nothing is being composed. Rule: [ai-completion].

### C. Proposing the next move — the forward edge

*Initiating situation.* The offers so far stay inside the step the actor is taking; the system can also read the work's joints. Escalating past this line changes the offer's subject from the actor's material to the actor's course.

1. *Propose the next step.* A whole action at a transition point, with its rationale; the uninvited arrival is the new cost, and the channel is budgeted (the attention-and-tempo handoff). Rule: [next-best-action].
2. *Guard the accept by what it does.* Proposals graded, the guard sized to the grade; the grade is re-read at the climb. Rule: [action-consequences].

### D. Moving the rungs — the climb

*Initiating situation.* Every rung above the floor is a standing bet, and the bets age: acceptances, dismissals, and corrections accumulate, and whether a rung earns its place is decided on impressions unless the evidence is read.

1. *Keep the evidence of use.* Offered, accepted, dismissed-and-how-fast, corrected-after-accepting — per surface, per rung. The weak form: no pattern owns this record (finding 5).
2. *Move each surface along the spectrum.* The dial move, on the record: up where routinely accepted and the grade allows, down where routinely dismissed — withdrawal decided, not left to decay. Trust and the offer's account of itself scale with every climb (the transparent-reasoning call into calibrating reliance). Rule: [assisted-task-completion].
3. *Hand the recurring procedure over.* The same proposals accepted whole in the same situations are a procedure in everything but form; made an artifact both sides can read and repair, and handed over to run — the exit into delegating work. Rule: [workflow]. (Constituent: rule-composition — each condition composed as checkable clauses.)

## The read-aloud walk

The test from Book 2 ch. 11 §5: read aloud, the design should form stepwise, no step contradicting what previous steps built.

> The actor is working, the system can see it, and there is a judged picture of what most actors need most of the time. First the floor, silent because deterministic: the static guess where a guess is safe to stand, the stored value where the system holds it, the looked-up completion where the actor is still formulating. Then the system starts producing instead of retrieving, and because producing runs on inferred intent, everything above the floor arrives as an offer: intent read in place where it isn't already stated, the contract settled once — one gesture to accept, cheaper to decline than to weigh, confidence carried — and the inline continuation where the actor composes. Then the offer changes subject, from the actor's material to their course: whole actions proposed at the work's joints, each with a rationale, each accept guarded by what it commits. And the climb: the record of what was offered and what came of it, each rung moved on that record and capped by its grade, and the acceptance that recurs whole handed over as a procedure — where assistance ends and delegation begins.

Verdict: passes. Two deliberate debts inside the walk: the offer contract creates the anchoring debt, collected by the calibration call at acceptance; the forward edge's grading creates the cap the climb re-reads. Obligations carried forward, not contradictions.

## Test walks

Recorded per ch. 11 §7: a sequence fails where a later step forces undoing an earlier step's product.

*Walk 1 — the issue tracker (continuing the prior sittings' walk 1; embedded posture; the surfaces walked are the new-issue form and the triage queue).* A1: the form defaults project from the current context and priority to the mass default; the reporter field takes no guess — the value is known, not guessable. A2: reporter and team autofill from the profile; labels and assignee complete in flight from the known lists. B1: intent is read in place — the selected issue, the just-pasted stack trace. B2: the duplicate proposal ("possibly a duplicate of #482") arrives with its confidence and dismisses in one gesture. B3: the triage comment's ghost-text continuation, silent when the model is unsure. C1: when the fix merges, the transition-point proposals — close the linked issue, notify the reporter — each with its rationale, on a channel spent deliberately. C2: closing graded above labelling; the close-accept takes a look at what it touches, the label-accept keeps one gesture. D1: the record shows label suggestions accepted routinely and duplicate proposals dismissed fast on one component's issues. D2: labels climb (the grade allows it); duplicates come down a rung on that component; closing never climbs on acceptance alone — the grade caps it. D3: the recurring triage acceptances become the overnight rule set — composed clauses in a workflow artifact, handed over. The standing agent the prior sittings' walks have been framing, governing, and calibrating is this walk's endpoint: the ladder is where it came from. *Backtrack found:* the first draft bound the floor by surface kind — a form dropdown is autocomplete, so the label picker sat on the floor. Mid-walk the picker's candidates stayed looked up but their ranking turned inferential, and the draft's silent-floor rule mislabelled an inferential surface as deterministic. The boundary was re-drawn from surface to source: the candidate set stays floor, the inferred ranking is already offer material and takes the contract's confidence mark (finding 4).

*Walk 2 — the research assistant (continuing the prior sittings' walk 2; conversation-first; the surfaces walked are the survey set-up form and the conversation).* A1: the set-up form defaults the date range to the field's convention and the corpus to the general pick. A2: the researcher's archives and affiliation autofill from the profile; paper titles complete from the index. B1: on the set-up form the half-filled state is context enough to read intent in place; in the conversation the step skips — intent arrives stated in the prompt (backtrack, below). B2: proposed related papers arrive as offers with confidence; the prompt surface's starter suggestions are the same contract before the first word (the `prompt hosts suggestion` edge is its typed form). B3: the survey's prose sections continue at the cursor; "draft the whole related-work section" shades out of the rung toward the delegated result — delegating work's territory, reached through the exit rather than mid-ladder. C1: when the import lands, "run the dedup?" at the transition, with rationale, inside the assistant's own surface — the channel budget still a decision even without a notification tray. C2: dedup graded cheaply reversible and kept at one gesture; nothing consequential rides the channel unguarded. D1: the record shows dedup accepted after every import and related-paper offers dismissed unread during writing sessions. D2: dedup climbs; the related-paper rung comes down during composition — withdrawal decided, not decayed. D3: import → dedup → format becomes the standing survey pipeline handed over — the run the delegating-work walk then watches. *Backtrack found:* the first draft required "read the intent in place" as the offer region's first step everywhere. The conversation gave it nothing to decide — inferring intent the actor just stated is re-deriving it — and forcing the step manufactured a decision. It was rewritten as optional with stated intent as the skip condition; the offer contract still applies in full (finding 3).

*On the transfer question:* the ladder guided at region boundaries — what kind of offer a surface may make before the evidence supports it — more than in fine-grained order, like the earlier sequences at their joints. Its distinctive contribution is temporal: both walks used the regions as a climbed history, not a build order, and the climb region is what makes that legible — the other sequences decide a design once; this one keeps re-deciding it.

## Findings — corpus changes the authoring forces

Recorded for their own sittings; none executed here.

1. *The map's flat ladder resolved into four regions, and the ai-completion → suggestion order reversed.* The escalation of initiative (inline completion below unanchored suggestion) is not the order of decisions: the offer contract — suggestion, the genus — is settled once before its inline form is taken, so the sequence stores suggestion → ai-completion where the map's ladder read the other way. embedded-intelligence moved from a "via" aside to the offer region's optional head; its `precedes suggestion` edge ("the inference produces an offer") is the region's spine evidence.
2. *assisted-task-completion placed at the climb, not at the offer's head.* The spectrum page's distinct decision is the dial move — where on the spectrum each surface sits — while its offer-loop prose (offer/decide, dismissal load-bearing, trust scaling) duplicates suggestion's contract. Candidate for a sitting: divide the job explicitly — the loop prose consolidates toward suggestion, and the page's spectrum-survey half is now walked by this sequence, which its body could say.
3. *embedded-intelligence is optional with "intent arrives stated" as its skip condition.* Walk 2's backtrack. The page already contrasts inferred with stated intent in its consequences; candidate: a line in its initiating situation naming the stated-intent boundary, for whichever sitting executes its To-dos.
4. *The rung boundary is source, not surface.* Walk 1's backtrack: the same dropdown escalates from looked-up candidates to inferred ranking without changing shape, and the actor cannot see the crossing — calibrating reliance's provenance concern sighted at the ladder's floor. Candidate: a note on autocomplete's narrowing-vs-expanding distinction identifying it with the lookup-vs-inference boundary, and the floor's resulting context in the sequence file already states the rule.
5. *The evidence gap sighted from inside.* climb/1 is the weak form: nothing owns the record of typical use — what was offered, accepted, dismissed, corrected, per surface and rung. The head presupposition names the same gap (the discharge audit lists it); a step now marks it mid-walk. The reliance record (calibrating reliance's account) borders it without holding it — scrutiny evidence and scope evidence are different readings of adjacent records.
6. *The record-driven re-decision, fourth altitude.* The rung moved on the acceptance record joins ai-tuning's (behaviour), bounded-autonomy's (authority), and cognitive-forcing-functions' (scrutiny) as the fourth record-driven re-decision. The calibrating-reliance sitting said the pattern is worth a vocabulary note if a fourth appears; it has — a relationship-vocabulary sitting's judgement.
7. *The accept-or-perform flip is a candidate third doorway into delegating work.* next-best-action's `serves: delegation` note places it at the accept-or-perform boundary — the same recommendation presented for the actor to accept or the agent to perform. Authoring the connection now would stale delegating work's two-doorway framing (framing/1's note and its trace); a sitting reconciles the framing before the edge is typed.
8. *generated-content → next-best-action classified.* A hands-off from delegating work's arrival into this sequence's forward edge: the landed output is a transition point the system can read (the resulting clause is the typed form). The outgoing side is delegating-work, so the entry belongs in that file — a candidate connection for its next sitting.
9. *Shared stations.* action-consequences is confirmed with a third authored spine — making changes' safety pass, governing autonomy's gate, and this sequence's guarded accept — and joins the map's shared-station list. cognitive-forcing-functions gains a third authored call source (the ladder's offer contract, beside delegating work's trail and governing autonomy's gate); calibrating reliance's acceptance gloss says "two other walks arrive" — a candidate touch-up for a sitting.

## Revision to the step-length rules (2026-09-27)

The sequence was revised under the step-length rules in docs/specs/sequences.md §Authoring, in the plain wording of the earlier revisions. "Rung" and "ladder" became "level of help", and the climb sub-sequence is now titled "Adjusting the level of help". The file went from about 1,930 words to about 1,320. No step, rule, order, or connection changed, and sub-sequence ids are unchanged, so the read-aloud walk, the test walks, and every incoming `at` address still stand.

Where the removed text went:

- The floor's `resulting` held the fact the whole sequence depends on: filling from a guess, a stored value, or a lookup is deterministic and can happen unasked, while anything inferred must arrive as an offer, and the line is set by where a value comes from rather than by how the surface looks (finding 4). It moved into the lead.
- Pattern reasons and costs already on the pattern pages: defaults as examples and sensitive defaults (good-defaults), filling silently showing what the system knows and wrong stored values travelling further (autofill), looked up rather than produced (autocomplete), the separate surface that makes the actor restate context (embedded-intelligence), dismissal cheaper than weighing, confidence, and anchoring (suggestion), lighter text and silence at low confidence (ai-completion), uninvited offers and the ignored channel (next-best-action), trust scaling with the level (assisted-task-completion), and inspecting and repairing the procedure (workflow).
- The forward sub-sequence's `resulting` named the accept-or-perform boundary (finding 7). It moved into the gloss of "Propose the next step" as the point where the sequence ends.
- The offer sub-sequence's `resulting`, that each accepted offer is content the actor owns without having made it, is on generated-content's page and was dropped.
- The "no pattern owns" remark on the evidence record is finding 5; the missing `rule` marks it.
- The connection notes lost their remarks on typed edges and doorway counts, which are findings 1, 8, and 9 and the sequence map's entries.

Nothing was lost that is not on a pattern page, in the findings, or in the lead.

Step-list test: the step lines, with the cluster and its two members, read as a walk without their glosses.
