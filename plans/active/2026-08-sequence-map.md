# A system of sequences over the language

## Intent

Organise the language as a system of interconnected *generative sequences* — named, ordered walks through the corpus in the sense of *The Nature of Order* Book 2, ch. 11: each step acts on what the previous steps produced, and the sequences are linked into a system. The system precedent is Book 2 ch. 20: a network of sequences that call one another as the centers they make need supporting centers, with each sequence kept small enough to be adopted on its own; the generative codes carry the same idea with the order stated as approximate. *Working Through Screens* is a quarry for sequence content — its D, E, and H bands cover territories this corpus is missing — and a counter-example on ordering: its 100 considerations are organised for random access, and the book treats committing them to an order as harmful.

This plan holds the candidate list — sequences already latent in the `precedes` graph and sequences the corpus is missing — plus the ordering among them, the interconnection inventory, and the open questions. The phase-1 research gate (2026-08-28) grounds the framing. The phase-2 pilot ([2026-08-composing-views-sequence.md](2026-08-composing-views-sequence.md)) bore out ch. 11 §11's claim that building a sequence changes patterns: it recorded eight corpus changes — reorders, a promotion, candidate edges and a candidate split — each awaiting its own sitting.

The analysis is grounded in the 2026-08 precedes register sweep, which left `precedes` make-time only and acyclic, so chains of `precedes` edges are trustworthy sequence spines. Constituents attach through `enables` and `hosts`; 38 pattern nodes touch no `precedes` edge, and several of them read as steps of unwritten sequences rather than constituents.

## What a sequence is here

The settled parts of this section are promoted to [docs/specs/sequences.md](../../docs/specs/sequences.md) (definition, file format, authoring bar); the entries below keep the map-specific commentary and examples.

- An authored, named ordered walk of steps for one coherent stretch of design work. The order is authored and fixed, but approximate: each step keeps latitude to respond to what is already made, and the order is revised as authoring and use teach (ch. 11 §13; the generative codes state their order as approximate on purpose).
- The step is the unit, and it is *artifact-register*: a decision written as a verb, invoking a pattern as its rule or opening a choice point whose arms carry their own conditions (the navigation-model choice; its decision tree dissolved 2026-09-24). The unit is not called a move — in this corpus a move is what a pattern names, and a step invokes a pattern rather than being one; Alexander's ch. 11 and ch. 20 use *step* throughout. A step that only names a pattern is the weak form. A sequence records the order of decisions, not of construction — steps taken in order may be realised together. Constituents (`enables`-only patterns) ride inside steps, not as steps.
- Order over the steps is a partial order (ch. 11 §7), and any numbering is its collapse. Where the partial order gives no arrows the collapse is declined rather than guessed: those steps stand as an unordered cluster sharing one number.
- Steps draw only from `role: pattern` nodes. Foundations are material, not steps: a foundation-sourced `precedes` edge stays valid in the graph (substrate note in the vocabulary) but renders in a sequence as part of a head's initiating situation, never as a step. Qualities are reading dimensions: a sequence can say which qualities its walk chiefly enacts, but a quality is never a step either.
- A sequence takes its initiating situation from its head. Sequences connect in three ways. A sequence *calls* another mid-course when the centers it makes need supporting centers — the primary relation (Book 2 ch. 20's network; calibrating reliance is a called sequence over every AI surface). A sequence *hands off* when its results set up another's starting conditions (resulting clauses, `sets-up`, crossing territory boundaries). Two sequences *interleave* when their steps must alternate. A *shared station* is a sequence or step that many sequences call (approval-gate, cognitive-forcing-functions, localization).
- Boundary rule: a sequence takes no process-register steps. What a design team does to ground the work (task analysis, posture decision, scope judgement) is stated at sequence heads as *presuppositions* — initiating-situation prose — and discharged by the process-level project this corpus pairs with. The presuppositions are the interface between the two projects: Alexander's late form fused design and process steps in one system, and here that system is split across the pair, so each presupposition names work a process deliverable discharges. This corpus stays self-contained and does not reference the process project by name.

## Missing sequences

Each has scattered members in the corpus but no spine; the sequence framing turns known absent *dimensions* (decision-dimensions.md) into absent *walks*.

- *Finishing the whole.* The late pass over every surface. Its steps are the degraded-states walk — state-empty, state-disabled, unavailable-actions, error and loading, feedback conventions — which exists only as orphan patterns. Localization is a foundation, so it contributes an obligation rather than a step: the corpus's many pattern → localization edges mark substrate work this pass verifies is done, late and cross-cutting. Presupposes: an inventory of the surfaces and the states each can be in — the enumeration the late pass walks; discharged nowhere.

Settings is partly placed: attention and tempo claims settings → notification (the shared-configuration change as an event class arriving at its grading step) and invokes settings as the budget's hand-over step; the two remaining make-time edges (settings → undo, → status-feedback) are still unclaimed — a small fragment of their own or part of finishing the whole.

Grounding is explicitly *not* a sequence. Its content is process-register (map the practice, name the objects, settle the collaboration posture, judge what is worth mediating) and enters the language only as presuppositions at sequence heads. The posture decision is tree-shaped (embedded, conversational, or delegated) and needs a home; see open questions.

Interop and end-user extension (*Working Through Screens* K8–K11) are a watch item, probably outside the garden's scope. Malleability holds the quality-level language; there is no sequence claim yet.

## Ordering among sequences

No single order is the system's order: order belongs to the activity performed on the corpus, not to the corpus itself (designing walks large to small; learning a language walks small to large), and scale alone under-determines order for interaction design, where technology, task, information, and time cut across it. The scale-following arrangement — structuring the space → composing views → making changes → the AI cluster (escalation and the encounter sharing its first place as the two doorways in — the ladder and the conversational opening — then delegating, calibrating, governing) → learning → finishing the whole — is the default *reading* order, one authored walk among several the system supports; each sequence file carries its place in it as `order`, and sequence listings (the nav group, a pattern page's "Appears in") sort by it — sequences sharing a number stand as an unordered cluster, alphabetical within. Memory and continuity and attention and tempo sit with the AI cluster at governing autonomy's number — the three share 7 as an unordered cluster: the governing of autonomy, the keeping of its remains, and the budgeting of its claims on attention; making sense at scale shares 3 with making changes — the two faces of material work, changing it and reading it — and working together runs parallel to the same pair.

Per-product entry differs, and the system should say so rather than force a single entry point: a tool-shaped product enters through structuring the space; a conversation-first product enters through the encounter and invokes the structural sequences from inside it (conversation `hosts` generated-content and extraction — the conversation is the workspace there).
