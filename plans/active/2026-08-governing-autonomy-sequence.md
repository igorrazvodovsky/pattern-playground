# Governing autonomy — the sixth sequence

Phase 5 of [the sequence-map plan](2026-08-sequence-map.md): the fifth sequence authored after the pilot. The sequence's authoritative home is `apps/patterns/src/content/sequences/governing-autonomy.mdx`; this document is the authoring trace — the sequence as first written, the read-aloud walk, the test walks with their backtracks, and the corpus changes the authoring surfaced. The corpus changes are recorded here, not executed — each is judgment work for its own sitting.

This territory was picked as the ripest of the queue: both AI sequences authored the day before drain into it — the encounter's previewed plan and delegating work's reviewed delta both land on approval-gate, and delegating work's intermediary step names drawing the authority's edges as this sequence's opening decision. The delegating-work sitting also routed Noessel's chapter 9 here nearly whole (takeback machinery, vigilance decay, handoff to intermediates), so the source material was already in place.

The graph's evidence ran out at the gate: three `precedes` edges converge on approval-gate (from bounded-autonomy, plan-preview, and change-review), but the map's candidate tail — approval-gate → handoff → activity-log — had no edges behind it, and the authoring resolved it differently (finding 1).

Steps are decisions, written as verbs, each invoking a pattern as its rule. Constituents ride inside steps in parentheses.

No connection-ownership transfers were needed this sitting: all three incoming handoffs (the encounter's gate/1, delegating work's framing/3 and receive/2) already live as outgoing entries on their source files, per the schema rule.

## Presupposition (the head)

Delegation is underway: a standing intermediary holds work the actor framed, and how far it may act unprompted is now the question. The risks have been judged — which actions are cheap to redo, what a runaway costs, which effects cannot be called back. Nothing in the corpus discharges that judgement; it is process-register and settled upstream, and the map's discharge audit already lists it as a legible gap. The delegation foundation frames the stations this sequence's decisions cover — the authority shape at set-up, the execution-boundary checkpoint, handoff and takeback.

The sequence enacts *agency* across the first three regions (authority explicitly held, consented to, and passed rather than ambient) and *temporality* at the account (the record, and the trust arc that widens the grant over runs).

## The sequence

### A. Drawing the edges — the head

1. *Draw the authority's edges.* Scope, spend, time, and risk class, granted per capability rather than wholesale, so trust starts small and grows on demonstrated competence; the bounds only bind if the actor understood them at grant time. Creates the debt the transfer collects: crossing a bound is a designed return, not a failure. Rule: [bounded-autonomy]. (Constituents: rule-composition — the composed clauses read back before they bind; settings — the standing home of the bounds between grants.)
2. *Make the spend visible against its bound.* A budget only binds when spend is visible against it — estimates before the run, meters during, quota in view; the estimate half rides the gate's preview. The meter taxes open-ended use, so it is matched to the bounds that matter. Rule: [cost-transparency]. The map's "metering step" placed: in the grant, as the bound's enforcement surface (finding 2).

### B. Gating the consequential

*Initiating situation.* Inside its edges the run still arrives at commitments — work substantial enough to spend real effort, effects hard to call back. What stands between intention and commit is decided per act, in proportion to consequence; the routine act keeps a clear path.

1. *Grade what the authority covers.* Encodes the presupposed risk judgement per act — time to recover, scope, cascade — and decides how much checkpoint each act carries. Rule: [action-consequences].
2. *Preview the work before it spends.* Intention as a reviewable object — steps, targets, effects, the meter's estimate — so misreadings surface cheap and the consent has a thing to act on. Optional: the standing run's granted rules are its plan, previewed once at grant time (walk 1's backtrack; finding 3). Rule: [plan-preview].
3. *Take the consent.* Initiation and consent in different hands; per action, batch, or diff. Three arrivals — the previewed plan, the reviewed delta, the crossed bound's renewal — make this the system's most shared station (finding 6). Rule: [approval-gate].
4. *Hold the run to what was approved.* The consent covered what was shown, not whatever happens next; a deviation that matters is treated as a crossed bound. The weak form — no pattern owns the binding (finding 4).

### C. Moving the authority

*Initiating situation.* The run has paused mid-authority: a bound crossed by design, confidence run out, a judgement call — or the actor reached in. The pause is the designed return the grant promised; who continues, under what authority, is decided per pause.

1. *Route the paused run* — a choice point, per pause; two further ways out are not transfers — the actor taking the work in hand leaves for making changes and does not come back, and the abandoned goal takes the encounter's exit:
   - *renew the grant in place* — the crossed bound becomes a consent moment; the gate re-invoked, and a recurring renewal is the account's evidence the bound is drawn wrong. Rule: [approval-gate].
   - *move the work to other hands* — control with context attached, to the actor or to an intermediate with the authority or context the agent lacked; the seam is where accountability is most easily lost. Rule: [handoff].
2. *Give the authority back deliberately.* Takeback as handoff's other direction: a readiness signal, an agreement about the resuming bounds, a confidence-repair window in which the returned authority runs narrower. Rule: [handoff], aspect: its takeback direction (finding 5).

### D. Growing the grant

*Initiating situation.* Grants, consents, and transfers have accumulated across runs. The actor answers for outcomes they reviewed only glancingly, and the next grant's width is being decided — on impressions, unless something better is kept.

1. *Keep the record the governance rests on.* Every grant, consent, transfer, and crossed bound with actor, action, object, time; accountability concentrated at the gate has nothing under it until the record can say what each yes covered. Rule: [activity-log].
2. *Re-draw the edges on evidence.* Authority widens over clean runs and narrows when risk rises, as an explicit re-grant rather than a drift; the recurring renewal is the strongest evidence. The authority-side counterpart of ai-tuning's instance-to-standing escalation (finding 7). Rule: [bounded-autonomy], aspect: its incremental regrant.

## The read-aloud walk

The test from Book 2 ch. 11 §5: read aloud, the design should form stepwise, no step contradicting what previous steps built.

> The intermediary stands and the risks are judged. First the edges: authority granted per capability with explicit limits — scope, spend, time, risk — composed as clauses the actor reads back, kept findable between grants, with the promise made that a crossed bound will be a designed return. Then the meter, because a budget the actor cannot see is not a bound but a surprise. Inside the edges, the consequential is gated: each act graded by what recovery costs, the substantial run previewed — except the standing run, whose rules are its plan — consent taken where the plan, the delta, and the renewal all arrive, and the run held to what the consent covered. When the run pauses, the pause is routed: renewed at the gate, or moved to other hands with its context, and given back deliberately — readiness, agreement, a narrower window. Last the account: every exercise of authority in the record, and the edges re-drawn on what the record shows.

Verdict: passes. Two deliberate debts inside the walk: the grant promises a designed return before any return machinery exists, and the transfer collects it; the gate concentrates accountability before any record exists, and the account collects that. Obligations carried forward, not contradictions.

## Test walks

Recorded per ch. 11 §7: a sequence fails where a later step forces undoing an earlier step's product.

*Walk 1 — the issue tracker's overnight triage agent (continuing the prior sittings' walk 1; embedded posture, the standing run).* A1: a per-capability grant — may label, comment, and mark stale; may propose but not execute closing; fifty actions a night; the clauses read back in the rule surface, the standing bounds living in settings. A2: the nightly action count against its budget on the morning surface, monthly spend against quota. B1: labelling recovers in seconds and commits free; stale-marking is team-scoped and arrives as the morning's reviewable delta; closing is gated. B2: skipped — the standing rules are the plan. B3: the morning batch queue, delegating work's delta draining in. B4: one night the agent starts commenting on active issues — behaviour outside what the rules showed — and the run pauses as a crossed bound. C1: the exhausted budget with twenty issues left is renewed at the gate the next morning; the issue that reads as a security report moves to the security lead — an intermediate, not the actor — with what was done and why attached. C2: after the commenting misfire, the agent resumes suggest-only for a week; the actor re-enables execution after reviewing the tuned rule. D: the morning record is already the primary surface; after two clean weeks the grant widens to closing duplicates, and in release week it is demoted. *Backtrack found:* the first draft required the preview per run, following the `plan-preview precedes approval-gate` edge as if every consent needed a fresh plan. The standing run has no per-run plan moment — its granted rules are the plan, and they were previewed once, at grant time. The step was rewritten as optional with the standing run as its skip condition (finding 3).

*Walk 2 — the research assistant's literature survey (continuing the prior sittings' walk 2; conversation-first, the one-off run).* A1: a session-scoped grant riding the request — the connected library and open archives, a spend ceiling. A2: the pre-run estimate with a cheap outline offered before the expensive full survey. B1: retrieval passes free; the report is regenerable and cheap; sharing it out is gated. B2: the survey plan — sources, steps, estimated cost — previewed before the long run. B3: approved. B4: mid-run the agent starts pulling from a source not in the plan; the drift surfaces and the run pauses. C1: the extra source is renewed in place — a consent moment, not a failure. Later the paywalled archive is authority the agent lacks, and the work moves to the actor with what was searched and what remains. C2: the actor supplies access and gives the run back — an agreement that it resumes with the archive added under the same ceiling, the next retrievals streamed live as the confidence-repair window. D: the run's consents sit in the citation-trail record; "allow these three archives" recurs across sessions until it is re-granted as a standing bound. *Backtrack found:* the first draft opened the transfer with a plain handoff step, following the map's approval-gate → handoff order. The walk's first pause needed no transfer at all — the run continues after a renewal in place — so a required handoff forced a transfer where none was wanted. The step was rewritten as the routing choice with renewal as an arm, and the map's chain resolved into a loop through the gate (finding 1).

*On the transfer question:* the joints again guided more than the fine order. This sitting's joints were the standing run against the one-off run (which inverts where the preview lives — at grant time or per run — the way the watching posture inverted delegating work's account) and the pause's routing (the same pause machinery serves the crossed bound, the confidence failure, and the actor reaching in). Within a sub-sequence the order was stable across both walks.

## Findings — corpus changes the authoring forces

Recorded for their own sittings; none executed here.

1. *The map's chain resolved into four regions with a loop, not a spine.* bounded-autonomy → plan-preview → approval-gate → handoff → activity-log read as one chain; the authored form is the grant, the gate, the transfer, and the account, with approval-gate → handoff not an order but the renewal loop — the crossed bound routes back through the gate as readily as into a transfer. No `precedes` edge existed past the gate, so nothing is revoked; the map entry is corrected.
2. *cost-transparency placed — the orphan's first authored spine.* The metering step lands in the grant, as the surface that makes a budget bind; its estimate half rides the preview. Both `complements` edges (to bounded-autonomy and plan-preview) stay valid as the typed form; no new edge is needed on current evidence.
3. *The preview folds into the grant for standing runs.* plan-preview is optional with the standing run as its skip condition: its granted rules are its plan, previewed once at grant time. The `plan-preview precedes approval-gate` edge stays valid — the walk that previews travels it — but the pattern's page nowhere says the standing case exists. Candidate: a forms note on plan-preview (the standing grant as the previewed plan's durable form), for the sitting that executes its named-product-study To-do.
4. *Hold-to-plan is patternless — the weak step.* Both incoming handoff notes name "holding execution to what was approved" as this sequence's territory; plan-preview's resulting clause carries the drift force and its To-do names the gap ("what binds execution to the previewed plan"); no node owns the binding. Candidate: a pattern — execution held to the shown plan, deviation pausing as a crossed bound — or a dimension of plan-preview if the evidence stays thin. The step stands in the weak form until then.
5. *The takeback strains handoff's page.* The takeback's machinery — readiness signal, agreement, confidence-repair window — comes from the delegation foundation's station and Noessel ch. 9; handoff's body carries none of it, and the sequence reaches it only through an `aspect`. Candidate: a directions treatment on handoff (the agent-to-actor return and the actor-to-agent takeback as named forms), which the pattern's existing context-transfer To-do already queues work beside.
6. *Shared stations confirmed from a second authored spine.* approval-gate now has three walked sources — the previewed plan (the encounter's gate and this spine), the reviewed delta (delegating work's receive), and the crossed bound's renewal (this spine's routing arm). plan-preview and activity-log are confirmed from their second spines. The map's shared-station list needs no additions, only the confirmations.
7. *The regrant is the authority-side counterpart of ai-tuning.* Recurring renewals become standing bounds the way recurring corrections become standing behaviour — the same instance-to-standing escalation at a different altitude. No typed edge carries it; bounded-autonomy's To-do (the trust arc, widening limits over good runs, touching ai-tuning) already points at the territory, and executing that To-do rises in priority the way the change-review verdict did last sitting.
8. *settings gains its first sequence appearance, as a constituent.* The standing home of bounds between grants rides the grant step. Its own three make-time edges (→ undo, → status-feedback, → notification) stay unplaced; the map's settings entry is unchanged by this.
9. *Escalation to an intermediate, second sighting.* Walk 1's security-report escalation goes to a third person; handoff covers only the human↔machine case, and the human-to-human transfer is the member the map already lists as missing from working together. Delegating work sighted the same gap from the exception side; the connection is now authored as a forward reference.
10. *Vigilance grounds the gate's throughput force.* Noessel's half-hour vigilance decay is why the actor held as standing supervisor stops judging: the design answer is widening the grant in increments rather than gating everything, which is the account's loop. Absorbed into glosses; the overreliance-evidence overlap stays calibrating reliance's territory (the rubber-stamping call is typed via approval-gate's `complements` on cognitive-forcing-functions).
11. *Automatic demotion is an agent-initiated bounds change.* Thresholds that demote autonomy when risk rises (bounded-autonomy's forms) change the grant without the actor's act — walked in walk 1's release week. The regrant step's gloss says explicit re-grant, and the automatic demotion sits uneasily under it: does a demotion need its own consent moment, or only its record entry? Candidate question for the bounded-autonomy sitting, alongside finding 7's To-do.

## Revision to the step-length rules (2026-09-27)

The sequence was the pilot for the step-length rules in docs/specs/sequences.md §Authoring (steps as instructions, glosses of one or two sentences, results named and referred back to, `resulting` only where it adds to the next `initiating`, the step-list test). The file went from about 1,780 words to about 1,170. No step, rule, order, or connection changed, so the read-aloud walk and both test walks above still stand.

Where the removed text went:

- Pattern reasons and costs were already on the pattern pages, mostly word for word, so nothing moved: trust starting small and consent in name only (bounded-autonomy), the meter taxing open-ended use (cost-transparency), review fatigue (plan-preview), the agent working at the speed of the actor's attention and the approver owning what they glanced at (approval-gate), the mutual assumption at the seam (handoff), and what the record leaves out (activity-log). Where a cost implied a decision, the gloss keeps the decision: meter only the bounds that matter, preview only work graded high enough, make clear which side is watching after a transfer.
- Authoring status moved out of the glosses and notes: the weak form of the hold step is marked by its missing `rule` (finding 4), the shared-station remark on the consent step is finding 6, the tuning counterpart on the regrant step is finding 7, and the spine-hunt and missing-member remarks on the connections are already in the sequence map.
- The standing-run and one-off-run distinction moved into the lead, where it is said once.
- The `resulting` clauses of the first three sub-sequences restated the next `initiating` and were removed. The accountability debt that the gate's `resulting` passed to the account now sits in the consent step's gloss.

One point was lost: "a takeback that just happens re-creates the seam's mutual assumption in the other direction". The handoff page does not carry it, so it joins finding 5's candidate treatment of the takeback direction on handoff.

Step-list test: the step lines, with the choice and its two arms, read as a walk without their glosses.
