# Delegating work — the fifth sequence

Phase 5 of [the sequence-map plan](2026-08-sequence-map.md): the fourth sequence authored after the pilot. The sequence's authoritative home is `apps/patterns/src/content/sequences/delegating-work.mdx`; this document is the authoring trace — the sequence as first written, the read-aloud walk, the test walks with their backtracks, and the corpus changes the authoring surfaced. The corpus changes are recorded here, not executed — each is judgment work for its own sitting.

This territory is the hub of the AI cluster: four authored sequences already hand into or out of it, and the map's assistance ladder ends here. The candidate spine was the fullest of the queue, and the authoring still corrected it in four places — the fullness made the members easy to find, not their joints.

Steps are decisions, written as verbs, each invoking a pattern as its rule. Constituents ride inside steps in parentheses.

One mechanical edit was executed in this sitting rather than recorded: the encounter's placeholder connection (`from: delegating-work` via live-presentation, at its trouble/2) transferred to this file as the outgoing entry, per the schema's ownership rule — the outgoing side owns the entry once both sequences exist.

## Presupposition (the head)

Work has been identified as delegable and judged worth mediating — the judgement that a standing intermediary earns its keep against the actor doing the work directly. Nothing in the corpus discharges that judgement; it is process-register and settled upstream, and the map's discharge audit already lists it as a legible gap. Grounding material exists where the actor can name it. The delegation foundation frames the lifecycle the sequence's decisions sit inside — set-up, execution, exception, disengagement — and its remaining set-up stations, permissions and the test drive, are discharged upstream or not at all (finding 7).

The sequence enacts one quality per region, near enough: *agency* at the framing (initiative stays with whoever framed the request, and the hand-over is metered rather than switched on), *temporality* at the watching (work unfolding over time, watched or reconstructed), *malleability* at the receiving and iterating (the output as material to negotiate, route, and reshape).

## The sequence

### A. Framing the hand-over — the trunk

1. *Articulate the request.* The loose want becomes a request precise enough to act on, in the actor's own words. What comes back depends on what the request happened to carry, so the surface's work is drawing out what is missing before anything runs; the request is durable — keepable, editable, templatable — and a good one is worth more than any single thing it produced. Rule: [prompt]. (Constituent: prompt-scaffolding — slots and quality feedback for the actor who does not yet know what a complete request contains.)
2. *Anchor it in named ground.* Acts on the request just framed: the attaching surfaces — files, @-mentions, standing connections, removable chips — live inside it. The named ground scopes everything the run produces, wrong ground scopes it wrong with the same confidence, and what went in is what a citation later points back to. Rule: [reference-material]. Promoted from the map's constituent reading (finding 2); ordered after the request, reversing the map's spine (finding 1).
3. *Give the goal its intermediary.* The actor can state the goal but not the procedure, so a standing intermediary decides how — the whole benefit and the whole exposure. The mediation's shape is chosen (focused, inline, ambient), its current freedom kept findable; drawing the freedom's edges is governing autonomy's opening decision. This step creates the debt the next sub-sequence collects: the run owes an account of itself. Rule: [agent].

*Resulting context.* A framed, grounded request stands and an intermediary holds it. The account the run owes is still unpaid, and nothing yet shows the actor the work.

### B. Watching work out of sight

*Initiating situation.* The run has started and the actor is elsewhere — beside the surface for minutes, or gone until morning. The owed account arrives in four forms: the work as it happens, the run's standing state at a glance, the reasoning behind the work, the record that outlasts them. The watching posture — attended or unattended — decides which form leads.

1. *Show the work in flight.* Output legible as it forms lets the actor skim ahead, judge early — and stop early, which is what makes interruption meaningful at all. Optional — the run unattended by design skips it, and the glance and the record carry the account (walk 1's backtrack; finding 5). Rule: [live-presentation].
2. *Keep the run checkable at a glance.* The standing view between arrivals: still on task, what has accumulated, when it next acts, which way its variables trend — and the run idling toward its trigger shows it is live, so waiting never looks like silence. A trend turning concerning escalates the glance into an interruption. The weak form — no pattern owns the move (finding 11). (Constituent: status-feedback — delivery calibration for the glance-grade signal.)
3. *Expose the reasoning.* The account of how, matched to the posture: stream-of-thought riding the live surface, or the retrospective mode anchoring the record's entries. The trail makes selective trust possible — and anchors as readily as it informs, which is calibrating reliance's machinery to interrupt. Rule: [transparent-reasoning].
4. *Keep the durable record.* Reviewability and accountability now rest on the record — actor, action, object, time, narrowable or it is no record. Discharges the account debt the intermediary step created. Rule: [activity-log].

### C. Receiving what arrived

*Initiating situation.* The stream has completed and something substantial sits on the surface. The actor's job has shifted from making to judging, and accepting whole costs nothing while checking costs whatever the thing is long.

1. *Stage the arrival for judgement.* Material to negotiate, not an artefact to receive — rated, part-taken, traced to what it was made from; the actor will own the result without having made it. Rule: [generated-content].
2. *Route the output by what it aims at* — a choice point, per output; the ephemeral answer takes neither home and stays in the stream:
   - *show the change as a change* — output aimed at material that already exists and matters becomes a delta, judged in parts; the judge did not write the surroundings, did not watch the work, and cannot ask the author. Drains into governing autonomy's gate. Rule: [change-review].
   - *promote the thing out of the talk* — the output that matters more than the talk holds still on its own surface, the conversation alongside as its control surface; two surfaces now claim the truth. Rule: [extraction].

### D. Iterating to done

*Initiating situation.* The output is not right — close and nameably wrong, or wrong in a way the actor can only reject — and the costs of fixing are compared. The moves ride wherever the routing step put the output.

1. *Choose the correction move* — a choice point, per mismatch; the third move, taking the output in hand, leaves delegation for making changes and does not come back:
   - *name the change* — deltas in the actor's words, scoped or they churn what was already right. Rule: [instructed-revision].
   - *draw again on the same intent* — another answer to the same question, whole, partial, or variants in parallel; the silent discard needs the safety pass's reverse path. Rule: [regeneration].
2. *Make the recurring correction stand.* An instruction issued for the third time is a mismatch in standing behaviour; tuning moves the correction from this instance to every session, then goes quiet. Rule: [ai-tuning]. Relocated from the map's head-side branch to the tail (finding 4). (Constituent, added 2026-08-29: rule-composition — the correction that stands is written as a composed condition, per the classification sitting finding 15 points to.)

## The read-aloud walk

The test from Book 2 ch. 11 §5: read aloud, the design should form stepwise, no step contradicting what previous steps built.

> The work has been judged worth mediating and the grounding material has a place the actor can name. First the request: the loose want articulated in the actor's own words, scaffolded for whoever does not know what completeness looks like, pre-filled where the actor declines to specify. Then the ground: the material the run will work from, named and attached inside the request surface, scoping everything that follows. Then the intermediary: a mediation shape chosen, its freedom findable, and the debt created — the work will run out of sight, so an account is owed. The watching pays the debt in four forms: the work shown in flight where someone is there to watch, the standing run kept checkable at a glance, the reasoning exposed in the mode the posture calls for, the durable record kept because accountability rests on it. When the stream completes, the arrival is staged for judgement and routed by what it aims at — the change to existing material shown as a change, the thing that outgrew the talk promoted to its own surface, the ephemeral answer left in the stream. Then iteration: per mismatch, the change named or the draw repeated, and the correction that keeps recurring made standing behaviour.

Verdict: passes. One deliberate debt inside the walk: the intermediary step owes an account before any account surface exists, and the watching sub-sequence pays it — an obligation carried forward, not a contradiction.

## Test walks

Recorded per ch. 11 §7: a sequence fails where a later step forces undoing an earlier step's product.

*Walk 1 — the issue tracker's overnight triage agent (continuing the prior sittings' walk 1; the embedded posture, entered from the assistance ladder — a triage routine proceduralised into a workflow and handed over to run).* A1: a standing request — the triage rules, with scaffold slots for staleness criteria and target projects. A2: the ground is the tracker itself, a standing connection whose scope is visible and removable. A3: ambient mediation; the agent's freedom lives in settings where the actor can find it, its edges drawn in governing autonomy. B: nobody watches at 3 a.m. — the run is unattended by design; the glance view is the daytime check-in (the agent on task, the queue accumulating, tonight's run pending); reasoning attaches retrospectively to record entries; the record is the morning's primary surface. C: the proposals aim at existing issues, so the change-review arm carries them — a morning queue, per-change accept and reject, draining into the approval gate. D: "stop marking security issues stale" is issued as a revision, recurs across mornings, and is tuned into standing behaviour. *Backtrack found:* the first draft required the in-flight step, following the delegation foundation's launch station (liveness and an immediate stop control). The overnight run has no watcher to show liveness to; requiring the step forced a surface nobody would see, and stopping happens from monitoring, not from a stream. The step was rewritten as optional with the unattended run as its skip condition, and the glance and the record inherit the account (finding 5).

*Walk 2 — the research assistant's literature survey (continuing the encounter's walk 2; conversation-first, entered from the encounter — the framed open request becomes the prompt).* A1–A2: the framed goal arrives as the prompt; the ground is the connected source library plus two papers named by mention. A3: focused mediation — the conversation is the workspace. B: the run is watched live — findings stream in, reasoning as stream-of-thought beside them, the stop in reach and landing as the conversational abort; the record is kept for the citation trail. C: the survey report matters more than the talk that made it, so it is promoted to its own surface; its citations point back to the named ground — calibrating reliance's machinery over this sequence's product. D: "shorten the methods section" as a scoped revision, one bad synthesis section redrawn, "always cite in APA" recurring until tuned. *Backtrack found:* the first draft followed the map and put all four post-arrival members in one choice point. The walk needed two of the arms at once — the report stays detached *while* being revised by instruction — so the arms were not alternatives to each other. The four resolved into two decisions at two moments: a routing choice by what the output aims at, then a correction choice per mismatch, riding wherever the routing put the output (finding 3).

*On the transfer question:* the joints again carried more guidance than the fine order. This sitting's joints were the two doorways in (a framed request from conversation, a proceduralised routine from the assistance ladder — the same sequence receives both) and the watching posture (attended against unattended), which inverts the account's leading tenor the way the encounter's posture inverted its endings. Within a sub-sequence the order was stable across both walks.

## Reading against *Designing Agentive Technology*

The sitting read Noessel's chapters 6–9 (ramping up, running smoothly, handling exceptions, handoff and takeback) against the authored sequence. The delegation foundation already descends from this book, and its lifecycle is the closest prior sequence-shaped account of the territory, so it works as a completeness check on the walk. Three kinds of yield.

*Confirmations, absorbed into glosses.* The instance-versus-standing escalation (finding 4) is the book's skip-now-versus-tune distinction, and the book adds the agent-proposed variant — the agent counts the repeated skips and floats the rule for approval — now in the tuning step's gloss. The framing's restraint is the book's smart-defaults-and-distributed-customisation advice: collect what the run needs and no more, defer standing preferences into early use, run well enough on defaults when the actor declines to specify — now in the request step's gloss, with good-defaults riding as a constituent. The book's tuning tools are forms ("a task of providing data"), which the sitting authored as a call from the tuning step into making changes' surface sub-sequence.

*One correction, executed at the sequence layer.* The book splits what the sequence had merged: launch liveness — the idle agent showing it is on task — is not streaming generation. The watching sub-sequence gained a step between the stream and the reasoning (keep the run checkable at a glance), authored in the weak form because no pattern owns the move, with status-feedback riding as the delivery constituent; the unattended run now skips only the stream, never the liveness obligation, and the attention-and-tempo connection re-anchored to the glance step, where escalation actually starts. Findings 5 and 11 carry the consequences.

*Material routed onward.* Chapter 9 is governing-autonomy source material nearly whole — vigilance decays after half an hour, expertise atrophies with disuse and more in each generation of actors, handoff can go to intermediates rather than the actor, takeback needs a readiness signal, an agreement, and a confidence-repair window. Chapter 7's staged concern notification (explanation, the threshold being approached, what the agent is doing, what the actor will be asked if it is crossed) is attention-and-tempo material, and its play-alongside-the-agent scenario — practice that keeps the actor's skills alive for the takeover — is learning's. Recorded here as pointers for those sittings; findings 12–15 hold the gaps the reading exposed.

## Findings — corpus changes the authoring forces

Recorded for their own sittings; none executed here.

1. *Spine reorder: the request before the ground.* The map's spine opened reference-material → prompt, reading the `enables` edge as order. The attaching surfaces live inside the request surface, and the grounding decision acts on the framed request — so the request is the head and the ground its first consumer. The map entry is corrected; the `reference-material enables prompt` edge stays valid as the constituent-of-the-artifact claim.
2. *Reference material promoted from constituent to step.* Its product is spent beyond its host step — the ground scopes the whole run's output, the citation points back to it, and curating it grows into its own task — the same spent-later shape that promoted item-view and inline-interface. Authored as the framing sub-sequence's second step.
3. *The map's four disposition arms were two decisions.* instructed-revision / regeneration / extraction / change-review answered two different questions conflated by the flat list: where the output lands (by what it aims at — existing material, a thing of its own, or the stream) and how iteration proceeds (by naming, redrawing, or hand-editing, per mismatch). Walk 2 forced the split: an output is detached *and* revised at once. The map entry is corrected.
4. *ai-tuning relocated to the tail.* The map listed prompt → ai-tuning as a head-side branch; the authoring places the tuning step after the correction loop, as the escalation from instance correction to standing behaviour — the corrections worth keeping are the ones the loop keeps producing. Both existing edges (prompt → ai-tuning, agent → ai-tuning) stay valid make-time. Candidate for a sitting: an instructed-revision → ai-tuning edge (the recurring correction escalates to standing behaviour) — no typed edge carries the escalation today.
5. *The watching posture inverts the account, and liveness is not streaming.* Attended runs lead with the live surface; unattended runs lead with the glance and the record, and the reasoning switches mode with them — live-presentation is optional with the unattended run as its skip condition. The source reading resolved what the first draft had merged: the delegation foundation's launch station ("must show liveness and an immediate stop control") is not about streaming output but about the idle agent showing it is on task — an obligation the glance step pays in every posture, so the foundation's wording stands and only the sequence had conflated the two.
6. *The reversal-families missing link is half-closed, at the sequence layer.* The map records undo versus regeneration / instructed-revision as cousins with no relating edge. regeneration ↔ undo in fact carries `complements` ("restores what a regeneration replaced"), and this sitting classifies the connection: regeneration's silent discard is a hands-off into making changes' safety pass via undo. Candidate for a sitting: a resulting clause on regeneration ("what a draw replaced needs its reverse path") with `sets-up: [undo]`, replacing the bare complements, if it passes the make-time test. instructed-revision ↔ undo remains unrelated, and the map's missing-links entry needs its half-correction either way.
7. *Confirmed gaps: delegation's permissions and test-drive stations have no patterns.* The framing sub-sequence walks past both set-up stations — permissions and authorisation (the authority and data access to act on the actor's behalf, distinct from bounded-autonomy's *how far*; asked for only when necessary, with the benefit explained, or it reads as invasive), and the test drive (rehearsing the agent's behaviour before real stakes, on a compressed schedule or with stand-in resources). Neither has a node; the first is a genuine absence the presupposition currently absorbs, the second borders learning's territory — and it binds to transparent-reasoning, since the point of the rehearsal is catching the right decision made for the wrong reason, visible only in the account of how.
8. *agent → generated-content is now a transitive shortcut.* live-presentation's resulting clause carries the arrival (`sets-up: [generated-content]`), so the direct agent → generated-content edge duplicates the two-hop path through live-presentation — but the walk that skips the live step (walk 1) travels the direct edge, so it is not purely redundant. Candidate question for a sitting rather than a candidate drop: whether the direct edge's note should say it is the unattended path.
9. *Shared station confirmed: activity-log.* The map lists it; this is the first authored spine to step on it, and calibrating reliance and governing autonomy both end there while memory and continuity claims it as substrate. Also confirmed from the receiving side: the encounter's open-request → delegating-work handoff and the ladder's workflow → delegating-work arrival both land on the same framing step, which makes the framing head a two-doorway station the way approval-gate is a two-source gate.
10. *The change-review step leans on the pending verdict.* Its gloss binds with the absent-author situation — did not write the surroundings, did not watch the work, cannot ask the author — which is what [2026-08-change-review-verdict.md](2026-08-change-review-verdict.md) proposes adding to the pattern and the current page does not yet carry. The sequence layer and the verdict agree; executing the verdict's edit set is already queued, and this step raises its priority.
11. *The glance-level monitoring move is patternless.* The delegation foundation's monitoring touchpoint and Noessel ch. 7 give its content — still working, accumulating results, next trigger, trends, ambient and glanceable — and the corpus's nearest nodes are the wrong shape: status-feedback calibrates delivery, activity-log is history rather than current state. The watch/2 step stands in the weak form until a node exists. Candidate: a monitoring pattern (the standing run's glanceable state), which would also be the home of the launch station's liveness advice.
12. *The sequence has no ending, and the lifecycle does.* Delegation's disengagement stage — the check-in on whether a persistent task is still worth continuing, the graceful shutdown that keeps context, data portability — is entirely patternless, so no ending region could be authored. And the worth-mediating judgement the head presupposes recurs in use: Noessel's routine performance reporting (the agent showing its own value, even when the news is bad) is what feeds the re-judgement, and nothing owns it. Candidate territory for its own sitting; whether the ending belongs to this sequence or to memory and continuity (what leaves with the actor) is part of the question.
13. *Near-miss exposure is patternless.* The false-negative half of tuning — occasionally surfacing cases the agent just barely rejected, so the actor can check the settings against what was not done — has no node; the record step's leaves-out clause gestures at the problem without supplying the move, and the delegation foundation carries the touchpoint only as a commented-out stub. Candidate seed, feeding both the tuning step and calibrating reliance.
14. *Pause and resume is patternless.* Suspending the standing run intending to resume it — obvious controls, the paused state made loud when a trigger it would have answered goes by, restart on a trigger of its own — is neither abort (the goal is not abandoned) nor disengagement (the delegation is not ending). The foundation lists the touchpoint with nothing to point to. Candidate seed.
15. *The rule change wants a preview no pattern gives it.* Discharged 2026-08-29 — the classification sitting ([2026-08-rule-composition.md](2026-08-rule-composition.md)) resolved the territory to the rule-composition node, with the effect preview inside it as three forms ordered by evidence: replay against the record, simulate against the standing set, and Noessel's boundary sample as the fallback where no record exists. The sequence file was reconciled the same day: rule composition rides the tuning step as a constituent, and the form call's note now carries the node's corrected claim (the clause editor borrows the form's machinery; its field set stays open).
16. *The rule-builder territory is a pattern-shaped gap, and more than the preview.* Sighted when the source reading was pressed on: the constrained natural language builder is not a special case of the form — the corpus reaches the same composition surface from three directions (filter composition in composing views, tuning here, bounds in governing autonomy), and prompt-scaffolding is its one-run twin (the scaffold composes a request, the builder composes standing behaviour). Classified and discharged 2026-08-29 by [2026-08-rule-composition.md](2026-08-rule-composition.md): a new node, rule-composition, narrower than the sighting proposed — the unit a rule list is a list of, at action altitude, with workflow rather than form as the neighbour that had to cede territory. This sequence's tuning step now carries it as a constituent.

*Sighted, not claimed.* Pattern-shaped territory noticed in the same reading and the exchange after it, recorded for whichever sitting reaches it first; none is a finding of this sequence:

- *Tuning by demonstration.* The actor shows instead of says and the system generalises — carry the Bee along the path, drive the corner yourself, the Nest learning from adjustments, mark-as-spam training loops. Distinct from instructed-revision (naming the change in words) and from rule-composition (writing the condition); Noessel's "do it like this" and "do it like that" are the source anatomy. No node owns show-don't-describe.
- *The deferred act.* "Act when X, not now" — scheduled sends, reminders, the standing request with a trigger. rule-composition now owns composing the condition; the move of deferring an act to one still has no node, and attention and tempo will meet the same gap from the other side (reminding, resumption, handoff's scheduled return).
- *Practice against atrophy.* A reserved subset of the real work the agent leaves alone, optionally with the comparison overlay (what the actor did against what the agent would have done) — keeping the skill alive that the delegation erodes, which is what makes takeover meaningful. Learning's territory; its mastery rung has nothing about maintenance.
- *Escalation to another human.* The telechauffeur, the expert network the aphid photo routes to. handoff covers agent-to-its-own-actor; handing to a third party is the human-to-human handoff the map already lists as missing from working together — delegation sights it from the exception side.
- Dimensions rather than nodes, on current evidence: distributed customisation (onboarding's territory, with inquiry-agent as the in-context ask), the staged pre-warning before a handoff (a handoff dimension), and the agent's performance self-report (folded into finding 12).

## Revision to the step-length rules (2026-09-27)

The sequence was revised under the step-length rules in docs/specs/sequences.md §Authoring, following the governing-autonomy pilot, and in the same plain wording: "agent" for the intermediary, and step and sub-sequence titles in everyday words. The file went from about 2,050 words to about 1,620; glosses now have a median of 41 words and a maximum of 68. No step, rule, order, or connection changed, and sub-sequence ids are unchanged, so the read-aloud walk, both test walks, and every incoming `at` address still stand.

Where the removed text went:

- Pattern reasons and costs were already on the pattern pages: a good request being worth more than any single output (prompt), wrong material scoping the answer wrong with the same confidence (reference-material), the whole benefit and the whole exposure (agent), a silent wait reading as failure (live-presentation), reasoning that anchors as readily as it informs (transparent-reasoning), what the record leaves out (activity-log), material negotiated rather than received (generated-content), the two surfaces claiming the truth (steering-surface, in other words), and tuning that goes quiet (ai-tuning). Where a cost implied a decision, the gloss keeps the decision: ask only for what the run is missing, let the actor narrow the record, show enough of the surrounding material to judge a change, let the actor limit a revision to one part.
- The watch sub-sequence's `initiating` listed the four forms of the account, which repeated its four steps. The distinction between a watched and an unwatched run, which several steps depend on, moved into the lead.
- The `resulting` clauses of the first three sub-sequences restated the next `initiating` and were removed.
- The presupposition's placement of the sequence in the delegation lifecycle (set-up, execution, exception, disengagement) was dropped; sequence prose does not name foundations as substrate.
- The spine-hunt remarks on the attention-and-tempo and memory-and-continuity connections were authoring status, and both sequences have since been authored.

Nothing was lost that is not on a pattern page or in the connections.

Step-list test: the step lines, with both choices and their arms, read as a walk without their glosses.
