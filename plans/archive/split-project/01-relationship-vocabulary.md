## Move 1: Situations: node-side judgement homes that emit their edges

*The move.* Patterns gain frontmatter `situation.initiating` (prose, told as move-history) and `situation.resulting` (clauses; a clause with `sets-up:` emits a `precedes` edge carrying the clause as a derived edge-side `situation` field). The vocabulary doc states three rules: one authorable home per judgement; the condition is authored on the source side; no corpus-wide condition vocabulary. Decision trees become the second judgement home, their leaf maps moving from a hardcoded extractor constant into `decision-trees:` frontmatter. The generative-profile sidecars (`operatesOn`/`produces`/`enacts`, seventeen files across both packages) are merged into the construct and deleted. Six conditional-`precedes` specimens decompose into clauses (deletion→undo, the assistance escalation ladder, flat-navigation→hub-and-spoke, searching→navigation-overview, live-presentation→generated-content, ai-completion→cognitive-forcing-functions); a register note under `precedes` fixes the designer's traversal as primary. state-disabled's `situation.initiating` is written as the negative-space specimen.

*Answers to.* Plan workstream B and its research gate ("edges gain an optional condition… patterns gain resulting-context"); vision.md's mature move record, whose `situation` field this realises (the vision diff removes `situation` from the still-unrealised list); pattern-definition.md's minimum test questions 1 and 10 (recurring situation; what moves set it up).

*Backtalk.* The gate reversed the strawman's edge-side half — the deletion.mdx diff is the demonstration: one judgement previously smeared across `enables: undo`, a tree recommendation, and a `related` gloss now reads as one resulting clause plus the tree. Edge count held at 69 through the decomposition, so the construct changed where judgements are authored, not the topology. The profile absorption is a retirement the open question predicted: the sidecar channel "demonstrably didn't spread past its proof-of-concept nine" (vocabulary doc, open question 1). The construct proved immediately generative — the carried negative-space item closed with no new vocabulary because a last-resort claim turned out to *be* an initiating situation (state-disabled.mdx diff). Strain: vision.md still lists `consequences` among the unrealised fields while `situation.resulting` is defined as "what holds after the move is applied, including the new problems it opens" — the two now overlap without a stated boundary.

*Question.* `sets-up` emits only `precedes` — is that a principled claim (a join between one pattern's resulting context and another's initiating situation is *inherently* sequential) or a current-scope limit? Pattern-definition.md's question 10 names moves that "set it up, complete it, enable it, or compete with it"; if a conditional `alternative` or conditional `enables` ever needs a home, does the one-home rule force it into a clause that must then emit a non-`precedes` edge, or does the construct deny such judgements exist?

*Verdict:* accept — current scope, not theory; a line added to the vocabulary doc marks `sets-up` → `precedes` as scope and notes decision trees as the existing home for conditional choice. (2026-07-11)

## Move 2: The consumer contract: structure allowed, evaluation is the drift

*The move.* The epistemic stance in relationship-vocabulary.md gains an enforcement clause: no pipeline step — extractor, query function, renderer, or agent tooling — may match, filter, or route on a condition or situation; they render as prose for judgement. "What makes a condition rule-grade is not that it is typed, structured, or stored — it is that a machine evaluates it." The same wording lands in graph-relationship-model.md's epistemic stance; invariant 9 records it as a non-machine-checkable invariant; a contradiction with references/report-pattern-language-formats.md (which recommends queryable postconditions) is noted in place, stance governing.

*Answers to.* graph-relationship-model.md ("it is not a rules engine"); the plan's research-gate framing ("the doc's epistemic stance is the constraint to defend"); vision.md Track 2's third bullet ("using them as context for judgement, not as predicates to be matched").

*Backtalk.* The contract is what allowed Move 1 to exist at all: the gate found that drift to rule-grade happens at evaluation, not structuring, so situations could take shape (clauses, `sets-up` lists) without breaching the stance. RelatedPatterns.astro complies visibly — the derived `situation` is used only as a label fallback, with a comment citing the section. But invariant 9 is the vocabulary's first invariant that "holds by review of consumers" rather than by check — a rule whose enforcement is a promise. And vision.md Track 2 simultaneously promises "alternative-conflict detection" and a query layer over the graph; the contract's scope (conditions and situations, not edge types) leaves the boundary between traversal and evaluation unstated.

*Question.* Where exactly does the contract draw the line for the promised query layer — is "surface every pattern whose initiating situation mentions deletion" a rendering of prose for judgement or a machine matching a situation? If the answer depends on who consumes the result, the contract needs to say so before the first query function is written, not after.

*Verdict:* accept — the boundary question is not this split's to settle; parked as open question 7 in the vocabulary doc. The intended consumption is needs-statement → agent-proposed pattern chains (situations as prose context for judgement); mention-queries are maintenance instruments, not the usage shape. (2026-07-11)

## Move 3: Component realisation leaves the graph; `enables` narrowed to within-language

*The move.* `enables` is redefined to hold "within the language: both endpoints are entries in the graph." The pattern→component relation becomes *component realisation*, a named non-edge: authored as `<ComponentRef>` body prose, validated by the existing cross-reference gate against Storybook's `index.json`, deliberately unrendered by RelatedPatterns, with no frontmatter channel and no `realised_by` rel. The doc's flagship `enables` example (Button → Form) is replaced with in-graph pairs; the extractor stops parsing `rel=` on `<ComponentRef>` (it warns instead), and warns on relationship targets naming no pattern — surfacing four standing component-aimed dangles, all migrated to prose.

*Answers to.* Plan workstream D; pattern-and-form.md's "Doors to the workshop" TODO, which anticipated a realisation vocabulary at "the linked-datasets stage… not for moving the boundary"; the workspace-split closure that removed component nodes (memory: components left the graph 2026-07-10).

*Backtalk.* The audit of all 38 `enables` edges found zero realisation claims to migrate — the workspace split had already removed the carrier, so the doc's old definition (and its Button → Form example) described a relation the graph could no longer hold; the definition change aligned text with settled data. The silent-drop channel had hidden four authored claims for the whole life of the graph — a channel that always drops is a trap the warning now closes. But the boundary document was not updated: pattern-and-form.md line 34 still says the materials "stay available in the graph data, reachable through `enables` edges (Button *enables* Form…)" — false twice over after this move (Button is not a node; `enables` no longer covers mechanism→move), and its TODO stands unresolved though workstream D is precisely its answer. The grounding doc now contradicts the vocabulary it points at.

*Question.* Realisation is now authored in prose, unrendered, and unextracted — pattern-and-form.md's claim that the form language stays "reachable" from the pattern language has quietly changed truth-value. Which document is right: does the language still promise graph-reachable materials (in which case realisation needs *some* projection), or has the boundary doc's claim been superseded (in which case it owes a revision, not silence)?

*Verdict:* reframe — workstream D solved authoring but never weighed the machine consumer; the reachability promise was load-bearing. `realised_by` minted as the claim's single frontmatter home (cross-dataset node metadata, still not an edge); prose ComponentRefs demote to citations. Landed in-branch: schema, extractor, validator check, docs (vocabulary §Component realisation + changelog 2026-07-11, pattern-and-form.md passage + TODO resolved, graph spec, pattern-content rule), form as type specimen. Backfill of ~50 pages scoped as plans/active/2026-07-realised-by-backfill.md. (2026-07-11)

## Move 4: `hosts` minted on a new Locative axis

*The move.* A new directed edge type: *A hosts B* — A is the surface on which B's trigger or entry point is surfaced. Alias `hosted-by`, no SKOS equivalent, a new Locative row in the axis table, grounded in Winston, Chaffin & Herrmann's explicit exclusion of spatial inclusion from meronymy. Seven pairs migrate across the episode (form hosts autocomplete, autofill, good-defaults, data-entry, validation; block-based-editor hosts ai-completion; state-empty hosts onboarding), each previously a `complements`/`enables`/`related` edge whose note said "container", "canvas", or "where it lives". A hosting note-tell joins the extractor's advisories.

*Answers to.* Plan workstream C and its evidence update ("treat hosting as a corpus-wide relation, not a toolbar special case"); the research gate's spatial-inclusion datum; pattern-definition.md's demand that relationships "carry semantic weight… 'embeds in'… is more useful than a generic related link".

*Backtalk.* The theory and the corpus converged: every shunt of a hosting claim into `enables` or `complements` had lost part of the authored claim (workstream C evidence; the A′ sweep kept finding them), and Winston et al. — the same source the part–whole relations already stand on — explains why. Minting immediately paid out: the note-tell surfaced three more genuine hosting claims within the same episode (4 → 7, findings 3 → 0 in workstream D's fold-in). Held residue is honest: data-entry's other surfaces (conversation, inline-interface) stay bare `related` plus prose because those pages don't yet own the claim. Strain: the relation's register is artifact-flavoured. Its definition speaks of surfaces, containers, and canvases — vocabulary pattern-and-form.md assigns to the form language — yet both endpoints are pattern nodes, and the carried move-vs-artifact plan (plans/active/2026-07-move-vs-artifact-naming.md) already flags that some patterns sit "closer to artifact-names" than moves.

*Question.* When form *hosts* autocomplete, the claim addresses form as a surface, not as a move — does the Locative axis speak the pattern language's register at all, or does `hosts` work precisely because certain patterns are legible in both registers, and if the latter, should the type's definition say which register it addresses so the move-vs-artifact plan doesn't re-litigate it?

*Verdict:* accept — the register is fine: spatial containment is not artifact-specific; activities can be "spatial" too and contain each other. The locativity reads in the language's own terms, not only through form-like pages. (2026-07-11)

## Move 5: Hygiene sweeps: the corpus audited against its own definitions

*The move.* Workstream A re-types the four conversational `instantiates` to `enables` and audits the remaining `instantiates`/`enables` corpus (nine further fixes, one drop as mediated, one contradiction resolved). Workstream A′ audits all 93 `precedes` edges against the generative-sequence definition: 24 re-typed, inverted, or dropped; 69 kept; the condition-bearing escalation ladder deliberately held for workstream B. Three definitional repairs follow from the sweeps: `enables` softened from strict dependency ("depends on to function") to incorporation ("incorporates or builds on", optional assists included); a written foundation tiebreaker (concept applied → `instantiates`, substrate produced → `precedes`); the register note under `precedes`.

*Answers to.* Plan workstreams A and A′; the vocabulary doc's own definitions ("hygiene against settled definitions"); the 2026-06-24 changelog entry's promised-but-unrun sweep.

*Backtalk.* A quarter of the `precedes` corpus was mistyped against definitions that had been settled for weeks — the definitions were right and unenforced, which is the direct evidence for Move 6's advisories and for the plan's own observation that "nothing at authoring time surfaces them." The sweeps also ran in the correct order relative to B: condition-bearing edges were held as specimens rather than "cleaned", so the hygiene pass fed the construct decision instead of destroying its evidence (plan item 5's gate, honoured in the A log). The `enables` softening is the one place a sweep changed a definition rather than the data — justified as aligning text with settled corpus practice, with Winston et al. ceding no theoretical ground, but it widens the type: what was lost is the ability to read "B cannot function without A" off the type alone (changelog, what-was-lost).

*Question.* With necessity dropped, `enables` spans strict dependency to "optional assists and interchangeable constituents… part of how B is realised where it appears" — at the soft end, what distinguishes an optional assist a move incorporates (autocomplete *enables* data-entry) from mere co-deployment (`complements`)? If the answer is only the author's sense of "woven into B's realisation", is that distinction legible to the future agent that pattern-definition.md's guidance question 5 asks about?

*Verdict:* accept — the distinction held in practice across the sweeps; the soft-end tension with `complements` is now a named overlap watch in the vocabulary doc's §Retirement, where it becomes the retirement question if sweeps keep re-typing across the line. (2026-07-11)

## Move 6: Lifecycle apparatus: advisories, retirement path, health dial

*The move.* The extractor gains four advisories (mixed-cluster, notes-voicing, note-verb, hosting-tell), all suggestion-grade; the voicing rule is promoted into the doc's authoring model and `.claude/rules/pattern-content.md`; frontmatter `group` is emitted as node metadata. The vocabulary doc gains a Retirement section: per-type counts in the extractor summary, `related`'s share of all edges as the health dial (baseline 25.3%), a retirement question closing every gardening sweep, `tangential` as first standing candidate, and an explicit no-type-count-budget stance ("the risk being policed is overlap, not number").

*Answers to.* The plan's extractor items in workstream A and the B-sitting's "vocabulary exit path" addition; the epistemic stance's "hints, not predicates" (all checks advisory, never error-grade); core-beliefs.md's garden framing ("structure enough to be navigable, loose enough to evolve").

*Backtalk.* This move answers Move 5's central finding — settled definitions with no authoring-time surface — and the note-verb advisory retro-detects most of the precedes sweep mechanically, which is a strong validation. The exit path fills a real asymmetry: the doc had entry gates (SKOS check, changelog discipline) but no retirement signal. Strain: the record-keeping lives in ephemeral output. The changelog states "a standing advisory line is the record that the hold is deliberate", but advisory lines and the health dial print to a build console; the doc's durable memory is otherwise the changelog itself. And the dial's premise cuts against the doc's own account of `related` as the honest "default catch-all; no axis claim" — a semilattice (core-beliefs.md, multiple projections) may legitimately carry a large associative fraction.

*Question.* The dial treats `related`'s share as failure residue — "where meaning goes when the vocabulary doesn't fit" — but core-beliefs.md wants the garden loose enough to evolve, and some adjacency is genuinely untyped. What would a *healthy* related-share look like, and how will a future sweep tell "the vocabulary isn't carrying the corpus" apart from "the corpus honestly contains this much unclaimed adjacency"?

*Verdict:* accept — the dial is read as a prompt to look for conversion opportunities, not as failure residue; `related` itself is legitimate and important for gardening. Dial bullet in §Retirement adjusted to carry that reading. (2026-07-11)
