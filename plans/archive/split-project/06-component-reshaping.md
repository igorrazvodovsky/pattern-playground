# Episode 06: Component reshaping — walkthrough

Scope: branch `split-project` against merge-base `ef66e6a852ff04cc9de2f64b8734fa4fe97c1d7c`. Territory is the component-level reshaping inside the form language; the workspace split itself is another episode's object. The raw diff for `packages/components/src` is ~511 files (the merge-base predates the package existing), so this review works from the branch log, targeted per-commit diffs (`b0f7052`, `77fd3f3`, `b536758`, `7ef634f`, `cfac4e8`, `9f7cb00`, `3f183b1`, `bdabad9`, `9655747`, `f56f519`, `32fb357`, `c3f6c57`), and sampled current files rather than a full read.

The episode reshapes the component library around the two-language model settled in `docs/language/pattern-and-form.md`. Two control pages decompose across the seam (Combobox → Bounded choice, Form → composite move + mechanism + Inline interface); the `umbrella` role is retired in favour of `collection` vs composite pattern; Storybook twins of migrated pattern pages are deleted or demoted to `role:component`; demos consolidate into a shared, move-keyed `demos/` tree including the lifted DataView substrate; the Storybook catalogue re-buckets from activity-level folders to Primitives / Components / Utilities / Foundations, with hidden-text utilities consolidated onto `.visually-hidden`; and two primitives (Switch, Radio button) fill gaps in the form language.

## Move 1: Umbrella redefinition: collection vs composite pattern

*The move.* Commit `cfac4e8` retires `role:umbrella`. Pages that gather smaller pages are sorted by Winston et al.'s (1987) meronymy test into three relations: composite pattern (`role:pattern`, `atomic:composition`, children linked by `enables`), general pattern with variants (`instantiates`), and collection (`role:collection`, members linked by `surveys`, now mapped to `skos:member`). `docs/language/relationship-vocabulary.md` gains a "three part-whole relations" table with a non-transitivity rule; `pattern-classifier` step 5 is rewritten; `elements.mdx` is deleted; four collection pages (`actions`, `activities`, `operations`, `qualities`) convert from `.mdx` to `.md`.

*Answers to.* No prior plan calls for retiring `umbrella` — `plans/completed/2026-05-role-metadata.md` and its companion minted it. The commit carries its own grounding: `research/typed-relationships-authoring/2026-06-23.md` and the newly created `plans/active/2026-06-typed-relationships.md` land in the same commit, so the move answers to research committed alongside rather than to anything on record beforehand.

*Backtalk.* The Form split (`77fd3f3`, earlier on the branch) had filed Form as `role:umbrella` and deleted its profile under the rule "an umbrella carries no single generative profile" (`plans/completed/2026-05-workspace-split.md`, rule 6). The redefinition re-reads Form as a composite move — its constituents *make it up* rather than being filed under it — and `relationship-vocabulary.md` now states composite-move pages *should* carry a profile, directly reversing what rule 6 did to Form. The strain the umbrella role produced is named in the diff itself: "Misfiling a composite move as a collection (the retired `umbrella` conflation) collapsed component-integral parts into membership."

*Question.* `enables` now carries part–whole at every altitude — Button `enables` Form and Bounded choice `enables` Form on the same edge type, disambiguated only by endpoint roles. Given that `pattern-and-form.md` calls `enables` *the* bridge between the two languages, is one edge type honest for both "mechanism makes move possible" and "constituent move composes into composite move", or has the bridge quietly become two relations sharing a name?

*Verdict:* accept — the question was answered by the branch's own later work and is stale as posed: episode 01's realisation workstream took the bridge role away from `enables` entirely (component→move is a ComponentRef cross-dataset reference, not an edge; `relationship-vocabulary.md` states this explicitly), and the current graph confirms it — all 38 `enables` edges are within-language, none has a component endpoint. The remaining within-language spread (hard constituent vs. optional assist) is an adjudicated decision, recorded in the vocabulary doc with the Winston non-necessity grounding. Nothing left to fix.

## Move 2: Twin retirement: pattern site becomes sole authority for moves

*The move.* Storybook pages duplicating migrated pattern-site entries are resolved across many commits: inbound links rewritten to `PatternRef` (`52e5c0b`, `a7cddd3`, `0a6a8e7`), move-only twins deleted outright (`c3f6c57` removes ItemView/Sorting/Dashboard/Grouping/View from `stories/actions/`; `71d7f33` retires Filtering's), and five root composites demoted to slimmed `role:component` mechanism docs rather than deleted (`32fb357`: BlockBasedEditor, CommandMenu, NavBar, Sections, Toolbar). The Autocomplete twin is an early full retirement (`b536758`): Storybook MDX/stories/profile deleted, demo re-homed to `demos/autocomplete.tsx`. The end state is a Storybook tree of `role:component` entries plus foundations — no pattern twins.

*Answers to.* `plans/active/2026-05-pattern-demos-migration.md` Phase 5 ("The Storybook tree should contain only: `role:component` entries, Storybook-native pattern stories, and material foundations") and the residue test in `docs/specs/pattern-role-model.md`, executed as territories of the workspace-split closure plan.

*Backtalk.* Two refinements the policy forced. First, the NavBar deletion was reversed the same day — a CSS-first artifact (`.navigation` layout + composed Priority+) counts as mechanism residue, and `32fb357` promoted that reading into `pattern-role-model.md` ("deletion is reserved for a twin with no artifact at all"). Second, deletions surfaced cross-page story borrows: `plans/completed/2026-07-workspace-split-closure.md` records a corpus-wide borrow audit after the Reference drop, and amends the verdict checklist to sweep borrowed embeds in both directions. Both are in-branch learning, already encoded in spec and plan.

*Question.* The demoted mechanism docs keep pattern-language tags (`activity-level:action`, `lifecycle:*` on e.g. `stories/Card/Card.mdx`) while the pages themselves are now pure form-language contracts. Are activity-level and lifecycle facets meaningful metadata on a mechanism, or residue of the twin era that misstates what a converging contract is?

*Verdict:* accept — the tags stay for now; a tags revisit is on the author's horizon but deliberately not scheduled. On the substance: a component *can* legitimately carry an associated activity level in theory — how useful that is in this project is a separate, open question. The twin-retirement policy itself, with its two in-branch refinements (artifact-residue bar for deletion, borrow-audit sweep), stands as executed.

## Move 3: Demos as shared, move-keyed substrate (incl. DataView lift)

*The move.* Demonstration code consolidates into `packages/components/src/demos/`, consumed by both surfaces (`9655747` moves prompt, workflow, block-based-editor, dynamic-hyperlinks demos out of stories files and writes the "Shared demos" section of `docs/specs/workspace-layout.md`). Ownership is keyed by move: the pattern page naming the move owns `demos/<slug>.tsx`; other pages and Storybook borrow by import; scaffolding no move owns lives in shared files. `f56f519` lifts the DataView composition out of `stories/actions/seeking/DataView/` into `demos/data-view/`, implements grouping, and adds `slices.tsx` as the host-side registry of configured framings that move pages embed.

*Answers to.* `docs/specs/workspace-layout.md` (Shared demos, the may/may-not-hold rule, the promotion register), the ownership rule recorded in `plans/active/2026-05-pattern-demos-migration.md` (settled 2026-07-02), and `plans/completed/2026-05-collection-move-demos.md` for the DataView consolidation and slice registry.

*Backtalk.* The DataView lift reversed its own plan's Phase B default: the collection-move plan targeted `src/components/data-view/` as package API, but the composition landed in `demos/data-view/` because it is "Product-sample-data-coupled demo substrate, not package API" — a reframe the plan's tail note records. The tree also forced the grey-zone rule in `workspace-layout.md`: reusable engines with only a demo consumer (the n-gram engine, HeatmapPlugin) park in `demos/` with a promotion register entry, so `demos/` is explicitly "a parking lot for un-promoted mechanism, not its permanent address."

*Question.* Demos are now a third population in the components package — neither converging mechanism nor diverging move, but couplings of the two. `pattern-and-form.md` gives the two languages the whole vocabulary; `workspace-layout.md` governs `demos/` only by dependency rules. Is a demo form-language material that happens to be shared, or is it the realisation layer the "doors to the workshop" TODO anticipates — and if the latter, should the move-keyed ownership rule live in the language docs rather than only in a plan note?

*Verdict:* accept — the demos' theoretical status is a genuinely open question, and the author declines to force it. What *is* settled: patterns must not own their implementation, only point to it; and individual components cannot own connective tissue like the DataView composition — so a third home is structurally necessary even if its name isn't. Whether that third population deserves language-doc standing may be beyond this project's scope; the author flags that established software-architecture ideas (layered architecture's application layer,  or Daniel Jackson's concepts, etc.) might have something to say about the move/mechanism/coupling triad and that the question should stay open rather than be closed by an amateur coinage.

## Move 4: Storybook catalogue re-bucketing + Utilities + `.visually-hidden`

*The move.* Storybook's information architecture moves off the activity-level tree. `bdabad9` relocates `stories/operations/` pages to an atoms bucket; later commits flatten the file tree (`ls stories/` is now flat) and retitle pages so top-level buckets are Primitives (25), Components (24), Utilities (3), Foundations (5), Data visualisation (3). `9f7cb00` mints the Utilities bucket (Overflow leaves "the vestigial Operations folder", Counter moves from Primitives, a new Visually hidden entry documents the hidden-text class) and deletes the "Established categories: Operations, Actions, Activities…" line from `.claude/rules/documentation.md`. `3f183b1` completes the substrate side: `.inclusively-hidden` and `.visually-hidden` were "equivalent techniques for the same job"; one class remains under the conventional name, ~30 files renamed, Shadow-DOM copies kept by necessity.

*Answers to.* Answers to nothing on record. No plan names the re-bucketing, the Utilities bucket, or the consolidation; the commit messages are the only rationale on record. The move is nonetheless the form-language half of what the old rule text itself flagged: "Atomic Design categories (primitive, component, composition, pattern) persist as metadata tags, not as the primary organising dimension" — deleted in `9f7cb00` — described organising the *component* catalogue by *pattern-language* strata.

*Backtalk.* The re-bucketing is mid-flight: seven pages still carry old titles (`Actions/Application/Form`, `Actions/Coordination/Messaging`, `Actions/Sense-making/Card`, `Actions/Evaluation/List`, `Actions/Coordination/Dual listbox`, `Operations/Callout`). The strain is not cosmetic — `ComponentRef` ids on pattern pages encode Storybook title paths (`form.mdx` points at `actions-application-form--docs`; `bounded-choice.mdx` at `actions-sense-making-card--docs`), so every retitle is a cross-surface link break waiting for a sweep. And the deleted rule line was replaced by "Each level has a role." with no statement of the new category set, so the taxonomy this episode committed to exists only in the titles themselves.

*Question.* Primitives/Components/Utilities reads as an atomic-composition scale, which the role model says is "compositional metadata," not a placement decision (`pattern-role-model.md`). Is the catalogue's primary dimension now officially `atomic:*`, and if so, where does that convention get written down so the next entry (and the seven stragglers) land by rule rather than by feel?

*Verdict:* fix, scoped out — finishing the re-bucketing is now `plans/active/2026-07-storybook-rebucketing.md` (retitle the stragglers, sweep ComponentRef ids, verify against the validator). The taxonomy question stays *open by design*: the catalogue is moving away from Atomic as its primary dimension, but the slicing is undecided — possibly just a flat list of components plus Utilities and Foundations — and the author will answer it when implementing the plan, whose first step is to record whatever is decided. Sitting finding folded in: Messaging's old MDX Meta title is dead text (its stories file and all inbound refs already use `Components/Messaging`), so the straggler list is six retitles plus one stale-text cleanup.

## Move 5: Form split: composite move + mechanism + Inline interface

*The move.* Commit `77fd3f3` splits Form three ways: the pattern-site `form.mdx` slims to the move (later re-roled `pattern`/`atomic:composition` by Move 1, with a situation construct); a new Storybook `Form.mdx` + stories documents the mechanism ("hosts a set of fields, governs how they are laid out, grouped, and submitted, and wires their validation"); and the conversational-forms chunk becomes a new sibling pattern, `inline-interface.mdx`, with the conversational-form demo moving to `demos/conversational-form.tsx`. Dual listbox is demoted `role:pattern` → `role:component` (site page deleted, Storybook `DualListbox.mdx` created), absorbed as a cell in Bounded choice's control table.

*Answers to.* The decomposition rule in `docs/specs/pattern-role-model.md` and the worked-example narrative in `plans/completed/2026-05-workspace-split.md` ("Form → Form (umbrella) + Form (component) + a new sibling move"), which records the split's nine transferable rules.

*Backtalk.* The richest residue-test lesson on the branch: the first walk attributed every chunk to an existing neighbour and returned "clean umbrella"; the second pass found the conversational chunk had no honest neighbour and authored Inline interface — the false-clean-umbrella trap now written into the spec's decomposition signals. The split also exposed a hybrid decision tree (top fork move-routing, lower branches control-routing) that was split at the altitude seam, and Dual listbox showed a spectrum can absorb a would-be sibling instead of linking to it.

*Question.* Form is the one bilingual same-name entry this episode produced (per seam-naming: the word names both act and artifact). The mechanism doc opens by deferring moves to the pattern page, and the pattern page defers mechanism to the component — but its `Form.stories.tsx` still titles the mechanism `Actions/Application/Form` and the pattern's decision-tree leaves point into that path. What, concretely, keeps a bilingual pair reading as *one entry with two feet* rather than two pages that happen to share a noun?

*Verdict:* fix — the sitting found the binding already exists and is machine-checked (`realised_by` on the move side, first-paragraph `PatternRef` deferral on the mechanism side, both validator seams; a retitle fails loudly rather than rotting silently), but the convention was written down nowhere. Fixed by extending the seam-naming signal in `pattern-role-model.md`: a bilingual pair is bound by one validated reference per direction, and each page opens by saying what it is not the source for — the move page owns why and when, the mechanism page owns how it's built. The stale `Actions/Application/Form` title itself is move 4's scoped plan. The split's substance — including the false-clean-umbrella lesson already encoded in the spec — stands as executed.

## Move 6: Combobox split: Bounded choice (move) + Combobox (mechanism)

*The move.* Commit `b0f7052` authors `bounded-choice.mdx` (`role:pattern`, operation-level: "committing a value from a set the actor didn't author and can't fully survey") with a profile, edges (`instantiates` Selection, `enables` Filtering and Form), and later a "Choosing a control" table spanning checkbox/switch/radio/select/combobox by set size × cardinality. The Storybook Combobox page stays `role:component`, its profile deleted, its outbound section flipped to "Used by" with Bounded choice as the lead link. `b536758` then retires the Autocomplete Storybook twin, with its recent-plus-suggested demo re-homed to `demos/autocomplete.tsx` importing the combobox primitives.

*Answers to.* The residue-test example named in `pattern-role-model.md` itself ("the page's own generative core (Combobox → Bounded choice)"), the worked example in `plans/completed/2026-05-workspace-split.md`, and the ground prepared by `plans/completed/2026-05-combobox-territory.md` / `-primitives-extraction.md`, which had landed Combobox as the contract multiple moves compose with.

*Backtalk.* The split validated the seam-naming rule cleanly — the widget name did not transfer, so the move was renamed, where Form's did and stayed. It also clarified a three-level hierarchy the plan records (Selection → Bounded choice → controls sized to set cardinality), which is what let Dual listbox and Radio button land as table cells rather than nodes. One asymmetry: the profile followed the move here (deleted mechanism-side, authored move-side), the precedent Move 1's redefinition later had to reconcile for Form.

*Question.* Bounded choice sits at operation level with `instantiates: selection`, while the territory plan had earlier declared "combobox decomposes cleanly under the existing role binary" and left the move unauthored. What made Bounded choice a node now rather than remaining Selection's named force — and does the "coarsest node that doesn't lie" rule say where the next candidate on that hierarchy (e.g. a searchable-select move) should stop?

*Verdict:* accept, with a small fix — the author supplied the missing criterion: Bounded choice graduated under *inbound link pressure*. Other entries needed to make claims about bounded choice specifically, and edges can only land on nodes, so the coarse node started lying by being unaddressable at the granularity of the claims about it. That criterion also answers the stopping rule (a searchable-select move stays a control-table row until an edge needs it as a target) and was outbound-blind in the spec — the three recorded fission signals were all page-visible symptoms. Fixed: `pattern-role-model.md` gains inbound link pressure as a fourth fission signal, citing Selection → Bounded choice.

## Move 7: Primitives filled by precedent: Switch and Radio button

*The move.* Switch lands as a Lit `pp-switch` custom element (`components/switch/switch.ts` + CSS) with a `Primitives/Switch` Storybook page carrying the checkbox-vs-switch commit-semantics section and the APG contract. Radio button (`7ef634f`) lands as CSS only — `styles/radio.css` styling native inputs, plus `Primitives/Radio button` MDX/stories/profile — with no custom element.

*Answers to.* Switch: `plans/completed/2026-05-switch-component.md`, followed closely (contract, stories, no indeterminate state), though the plan's `Operations/Switch` title landed as `Primitives/Switch` after Move 4's re-bucketing. Radio button: answers to nothing on record — no plan names it; its evident motivation is the Bounded choice control table (`b0f7052` precedes it by two commits, and the workspace-split narrative names "Combobox/Select/Radio (controls sized to set cardinality)").

*Backtalk.* The pair exposes an unstated investment scale in the form language. The Switch plan argued explicitly for the heavier treatment ("matching the level of investment given to Input, Range, and Select rather than the lighter Checkbox treatment"); Radio button took the lighter Checkbox road silently. Both verdicts look defensible — native radios need less encapsulation than a switch role — but the criterion lives in one plan's Context paragraph, not in any spec. Downstream, `Form.mdx` borrows the RadioButton stories for its fieldset section, so the primitive slotted straight into the composite mechanism's grouping story.

*Question.* Mechanisms converge — so when the form language admits a new primitive, what decides between "CSS over native element" and "Lit custom element"? If the answer is the commit-semantics/encapsulation argument from the Switch plan, is that a convention worth one line in a spec before the next primitive (segmented control, say) has to re-derive it?

*Verdict:* accept — per-case judgement is the author's current working mode, deliberately: the investment level is decided in each primitive's own plan (or, for a slot-filler like Radio button, by the shape of the gap itself), not by a standing convention. No spec line owed. Both landings stand as executed.
